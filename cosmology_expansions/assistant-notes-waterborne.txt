Because the protocol was frozen, those records should not be selectively deleted after seeing the result. The correct follow-up is a new holdout study whose inclusion rule is fixed in advance and whose universe is restricted to curated myths, fables, epics and religious narratives. Survival of the primary and three-part effects there would materially strengthen the motif claim; disappearance would show that this first result depended heavily on classifier contamination.</p>
