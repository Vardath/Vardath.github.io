<div class="deepening" data-after="3-squatter-man-petroglyphs-and-the-hanging-figure-geometry"><h2>Vardath hypothesis: Squatter Man as the world-current form</h2><p>In the Vardath reading, Anthony Peratt's Z-pinch comparisons provide a visual vocabulary for the Squatter Man figure: central column, pinched middle, lateral structures, bright nodes and changing upper forms. The cosmology interprets that morphology as a remembered view of the rod-like current at the centre of a world-forming event.</p><p>The hypothesis goes further than Peratt's published auroral model. Vardath proposes that a primordial current persisted long enough to organise chambers around itself, with matter, water and atmosphere later occupying those stable regions. <div class="deepening" data-after="4-the-axis-atlas-cross-tree-rod-mountain-and-gate"><h2>Vardath axis atlas: Yggdrasil, Sleipnir, Bodhi, Christopher and Gilgamesh</h2><p>In this cosmology, a rod is the axis in abstract form; a tree is the same axis imagined as living and branching; a mountain is material gathered around its lower emergence; a cross is the vertical axis intersecting the fourfold upper structure; and a gate is the point where the axis becomes a crossing between regions.</p><p><strong>Yggdrasil:</strong> the nine worlds are interpreted as nine chambers around the primordial current. Roots continue below, branches spread above, and wells or rivers belong to water circulation between levels.</p><p><strong>Odin, Loki and Sleipnir:</strong> the Vardath reading now treats them as narrative phases of one event rather than separate mechanical roles. Rider, mount and transformation are different ways of describing the same rod/transport form. Sleipnir's eight feet are read as eight support contacts related to four main legs or towers. The manuscript image closest to the one discussed is NKS 1867 4to, fol. 97v (1760), showing Odin on eight-legged Sleipnir with Gungnir.</p><p><strong>Buddha and the Bodhi tree:</strong> the tree is the living rod; Buddha is the personified Meru-form at its base; the hair/crown is interpreted as a cluster of luminous or ice-like eye nodes above the central emergence.</p><p><strong>Jesus and Saint Christopher:</strong> the cross is the meeting of vertical rod and fourfold transverse structure. Christopher gives the same family a river-crossing form: giant, staff, world-bearing child and a staff that later becomes living and fruitful.</p><p><strong>Aaron and the fruit-bearing rod:</strong> budding staff, iron rod, tree of life/death, golden or immortal fruit and eye-centre are grouped as variants of the world-axis in different phases.</p><p><strong>Gilgamesh / Gilga-mesh:</strong> the Vardath interpretation treats Gilgamesh as the man/mesh memory of the rod after it spreads into a sky lattice, with Mashu as mountain-gate. “Gilga-mesh” is used here as a modern mnemonic inside the model, not as a historical etymology.</p></div>
