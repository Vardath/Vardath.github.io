<p><strong>What these exact hits show:</strong> the statistics are not being illustrated with unrelated art. The Masonic sample contains literal square/four-eight and passage/ascent records, while the alchemical sample contains a much denser historical run of serpent/entwining records. The cards also show why the broad ≥3-family prediction failed: some individual images are multi-family, but most corpus hits specialize in one or two families rather than reproducing a single complete diagram.</p>
