<h2>Norse dragon stones, runestones and picture stones</h2>
<p class="art-source-note">Runestones and picture stones carrying serpents, dragons, knot-beasts, heroic scenes and rune bands. The section includes archaeological stones and later documentary photographs/drawings of them.</p>
