<div class="deepening" data-after="18-meltology-plasma-heating-and-the-expanded-brick-hypothesis"><h2>Vardath hypothesis: Meltology as a material trace of reset</h2><p>Within Vardath Cosmology, Meltology is interpreted as a possible material expression of the same event rather than as a separate catastrophe. A major plasma burst is proposed to reactivate the old world-current and return the chamber briefly to a more energetic and deformable state.</p><p>In that reading, exposed material may fracture, glaze, fuse, foam, soften or recrystallise differently according to composition and local field strength. The interest is therefore not only whether a formation looks melted. It is whether unusual material textures can be placed in the same event window as water movement, rubble displacement and renewed Squatter-Man/rod behaviour.</p><p>The deeper connection is cyclical. The primordial current is constructive: it organises the first chamber around itself. Later bursts are reconstructive: they temporarily reactivate the same pathways, move water and matter, alter exposed surfaces and then allow the world to stabilise in a changed arrangement.</p><h3>The source is proposed to persist rather than restart</h3><p>The current model no longer requires a completely new energy source for every reset. The original Birkeland-like current is proposed to remain inside the world as the deep telluric skeleton left over from formation. In quiet periods it is weak or hidden. At intervals it pulses more strongly through the same inherited pathways.</p><p>A reset is therefore a reactivation of the original world-forming current rather than a fresh current appearing from nowhere. A strong pulse can increase directional flow in the mesh, grow local convergence points, tighten the finger-trap toward an axial rod, alter the support of the upper water boundary, incorporate celestial convergence nodes into the temporary Squatter-Man body, and drive the material effects grouped under Meltology.</p><p>This solves the recurrence problem at the level of the hypothesis, but it leaves a deeper physical question open: what maintains the primordial current over long periods and what sets the timing and amplitude of its pulses?</p><h3>The waking world as a biological phase</h3><p>The model now adds a biological consequence to the same pulse. As the frozen eye-nodes and upper canopy thaw, additional vapour and gas are proposed to enter the chamber. Atmospheric pressure and oxygen availability rise while plasma and charged flow intensify through the ground. The active age is therefore imagined as warmer, denser, more oxygen-rich and more electrically excited than the quiet age.</p><p>Within the Vardath hypothesis, those conditions are proposed to change the limits of growth and repair. Telomere loss is imagined to slow or reverse, tissues remain regenerative for longer, and organisms tolerate mutagenic radiation or plasma exposure that would be damaging in the present quiet world. Mutation becomes excessive but not automatically lethal.</p><p>This is the proposed setting for the strange biology remembered in giant and monstrous-race traditions: very large animals, oversized invertebrates, Blemmyae-like forms, one-footed or large-eared peoples and other bodies that would not fit the present environment. The most extreme version of the idea allows abnormal growths or tumour-like masses to continue organising after separation from a host and become independent living forms. These are working biological claims inside the cosmology, not established modern biology.</p><p>The important structural idea is that the “age of giants” and the “age of gods” are the same active interval. The rod is awake, the atmosphere is changed, the upper boundary is open, the world is electrically pulsing and life becomes unusually plastic and overgrown.</p></div>

The god/rod is present but “asleep.”</p><p><strong>5. Pulse and convergence growth.</strong> A stronger pulse travels through the old current paths. Local lattice convergences begin sparsely and accumulate directional strands. Different stages can range from a few active lines to a dense mature node approaching the full 32-direction surface form.</p><p><strong>6. Waking-world biology.</strong> The altered atmosphere and ambient plasma are proposed to create a hyper-growth interval. Giant animals, monstrous races and unusually plastic life belong to this stage in the model.</p><p><strong>9. In the present working model, at least some planets may be electromagnetic convergence points with matter accumulated around them. During major activation, the rising rod can couple to those nodes and temporarily incorporate them into its larger coherent body.</p><p><strong>10. Layer contact.</strong> The axial throat changes adjacency between neighbouring chambers. In the Vardath interpretation this is the setting for Watcher, giant and beanstalk/world-tree traditions: the vertical route becomes traversable and upper structures or beings can descend into contact.</p><p><strong>11. Water-boundary reorganisation.</strong> The upper ocean is treated as the lower watery boundary of the world above, normally held firm by the directional telluric mesh. Flood, serpent-water and waters-above/waters-below traditions belong to this phase.</p><p><strong>12. The world returns to a constrained biological and energetic regime.</p><p><strong>15. Cultural memory.</strong> Later traditions preserve different fragments: world tree, beanstalk, dragon, giant, river crossing, four horsemen, iron rod, many eyes, radiant gods, strange races, staffs, saints, rulers, sky rolled like a scroll, flood, planetary release and the sleeping, frozen or dying-and-returning god.</p><h3>The sleeping god and the frozen interval</h3><p>In this mature cycle, stories of a god, king, saint or enchanted figure who sleeps, dies, freezes, goes beneath the earth or waits to return are read as memories of the quiet phase rather than separate myths. The rod has not ceased to exist; it has cooled and become hidden inside the world.</p><p>Fairy-tale freezing and sleeping motifs therefore belong at the end of the event. A Maleficent-like or winter-bringing figure, a sleeping ruler, a dormant world-tree, or a death-and-return story can all become narrative forms of the same transition: the active rod withdraws, the world freezes into its sealed state, and the underlying current waits for the next pulse.</p><p>The next awakening begins when the lattices interact strongly enough for plasma flow to increase again. The cycle is therefore not simply destruction followed by rebuilding; it is a recurring alternation between a <strong>frozen / sleeping world</strong> and a <strong>thawed / waking world</strong>.</p></div>
