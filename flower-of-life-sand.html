Main change: each circle now acts primarily through its rim / edge path. The strongest force is on the Flower of Life arcs, not at the centre.

3D-ish weave: where circles overlap, each circle has an alternating over/under height along its rim. The locally highest rim segment dominates the force direction, like woven snakes crossing.

Sand visibility: golden pixels are simulated moving sediment; warmer deposits are settled sediment. Waterline is an optional view, not a filter hiding all your sand.

Waterline: colour scaling uses a robust high percentile of sediment height rather than a single extreme peak, so one pile cannot make the entire lattice disappear under water.

Notes

This is still an exploratory 2.5D transport model rather than a full granular mechanics simulation. It does, however, encode the key ideas you asked for: full overlapping circles, edge-following motion, alternating weave height, finer grains, and an optional water level for coastline reading.
