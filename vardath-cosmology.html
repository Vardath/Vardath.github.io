1. How the idea developed

The model changed because several early versions failed. Those failures are part of the theory, not something to hide.

Original

Three spiral shells

Two active spiral-shell organisms were proposed to carry successive world-disc sections while a third dead/reversed shell remained as a stronger structural scaffold. Tiamat was tentatively associated with that old shell.

Correction

Not three simple stacked bowls

The later model no longer requires three independent shells sitting one above another. The “three worlds” can instead be consecutive stabilized turns or generations of one larger growth geometry.

Refinement

The active part is the braid

Between stabilized layers lies a two-strand lattice. One strand can move upward while the other moves downward; after half a braid phase their roles exchange.

Surface consequence

Land rides on top

Continents and island arcs are treated as fractured material transported by the changing substrate. Convergence jams and uplifts; divergence tears apart; oblique motion shears and rotates.

Boundary: this is a speculative cosmology, not an established physical description of Earth. The page records analogies, explicit toy mathematics and ways the model can fail.

2. Braided ammonite geometry

The large-scale envelope is modelled as a growing spiral; the transition zone is modelled as an opposed braid.

Spiral
R(θ)=R₀e^(bθ)

A logarithmic spiral gives ammonite-like self-similar growth. Fibonacci/φ scaling is a possible calibration rather than a built-in requirement.

Braid
zA=pθ+a sin(kθ)
zB=pθ−a sin(kθ)

Because the derivatives are p±ak cos(kθ), one strand naturally rises while the other descends whenever the weave term dominates the slow pitch.

Finger trap
S²=L²+(2πNa)²

For nearly fixed strand length, increasing axial length reduces braid radius. A broad lattice can therefore contract toward a thin scroll/rod state without its strands disappearing.

Three apparent worlds

At one epoch: lower = previous stabilized turn, present = current stabilized turn, upper = next turn being formed. The braid is the moving transition between them.

3. What the chronology tests did

Archaix timing claims were used as targets for internal consistency, not as independent proof.

Failed

138 / 395 / 792 as three simple rotations

A fixed shell plus two constant-rate rotating shells cannot make all three numbers the required pairwise recurrence periods. The simplest model was rejected.

Clue

Near 2:1 relation

2×395=790, close to 792. The braid interpretation gives a natural distinction between an opposite-phase encounter and a same-phase recurrence, but it still does not independently derive Phoenix 138.

Growth experiment

φ-calibrated toy law

With R(t)=R₀φ^(t/552), √2 growth takes about 397.56 years and doubling about 795.11 years. Those are near the proposed 395/792 targets but drift and were not exact predictions.

Lesson

Timing needs geometry

The chronology pushed the model away from abstract clocks and toward an explicit spatial rule involving growth, folding, overlap and phase.

4. Many mythologies as different views of one structured event

In the older discussions, the important comparison was not “the gods have different names for the same characters.” It was that very different traditions repeatedly preserve the same order of structural motifs. The shorthand became:

Axis → Gate → Operator → Vehicle → Manifestation → Medium → Witness → Reset
Axis

Mountain, tree, pillar, throne

Mount Mashu, world mountains, trees, pillars and throne/column imagery were grouped as versions of a vertical organizer. In the lattice model the candidate counterpart is the convergence axis or scroll rod.

Gate

Doors, windows, portals, solar passages

1 Enoch’s heavenly windows/portals and Gilgamesh’s guarded Mashu passage emphasize boundary crossing. The model maps these to convergence throats where surface directions meet the axial degree of freedom.

Operator

God, lord, Watcher, guardian

Stories personify the transition through beings who open, divide, command, forbid or judge. Marduk orders Tiamat; Watchers cross a forbidden boundary; guardians control passage.

Vehicle

Chariot, wheel, throne, mount

Ezekiel’s integrated creature/wheel/throne image, Enochic chariot/fire material and the Gita’s chariot setting were compared as recurring descriptions of moving structured transport.

Manifestation

Eyes, wings, many limbs, serpents, dragons

Krishna’s many-formed luminous universal body, Ezekiel’s eyes/wings and widespread serpent/dragon imagery were treated as different attempts to describe something complex, luminous, coiling or multiply articulated.

Medium

Wind, fire, lightning, radiance

The older synthesis repeatedly found intense light, fire, wind and lightning around the appearance. The Vardath interpretation considered an electrical/plasma-like pulse that changes the visibility or state of the canopy/lattice.

Witness

Revelation and special sight

Arjuna receives special sight; prophetic texts describe opened heavens; Gilgamesh reaches the distant flood survivor. The common motif is access to something normally hidden.

Reset

Flood, destruction, separation, new order

The sequence often ends with catastrophe and re-ordering. In the present model that maps to surface displacement, layer separation and restabilization after the transition.

The hypothesis does not require the stories to agree in theology or character names. A person beneath a convergence might remember an opening heaven; a traveller approaching the throat might remember a guarded mountain; someone seeing the moving mesh might remember wheels, dragons or many eyes; survivors would remember flood, destruction and a changed world. What should recur is the event geometry and order.
Connection to the mature model: the older mythology work supplied an event sequence before there was a mechanism. The braid model now gives candidate mechanics: axis = scroll rod; gates = convergence throats; wheels/serpents = views of braid geometry; opened heavens = temporary layer proximity; giants = proposed scale difference between turns; flood/reset = surface displacement and restabilization; hidden heaven returning = the layers separating again.

5. Maps, convergence lines and land shapes

Flat-map test

A literal north-pole azimuthal disc fails badly on southern continent shapes and distances. A logarithmic spiral used directly as longitude also fails because coastlines do not close after 360°.

What survived

The spiral belongs to the relationship between layers, not ordinary longitude. Geography must remain closed inside each stabilized layer.

Old navigation networks

Portolan wind-line systems provide a useful visual analogue: repeated convergence circles and 32 horizontal directions. In the Vardath geometry the extra structural direction is the axis through the convergence point: 32 around + 1 through.

S-shaped lands

If rubble rides on alternating shear cells, large S-curves are natural. The proposed chain includes the Americas, Mediterranean–Arabia–Zagros, India–Burma–Sunda, and eastern Australia–New Guinea–Indonesia.

Control: plate tectonics already explains rifting, collision, subduction and transform motion. The lattice model matters only if one fixed flow field predicts coastlines, faults, mountain belts and measured plate vectors without moving its nodes after seeing the answer.
