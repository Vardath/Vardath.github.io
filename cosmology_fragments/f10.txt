<p>The September 12 branch proposed three enormous spiral-shell
organisms or shell systems. Each could carry world-disc sections that
preserve whole eras or moments. Two remained active; a third was
dead/reversed and supplied the stronger structural influence. Tiamat was
proposed as a symbolic identity for that dead structural shell. Apsu and
Kingu were deliberately left unassigned.</p><p>The Archaix-style chronology used three target tracks:</p><ul>
<li>Phoenix ≈138 years;</li>
<li>Dark Satellite ≈395 years;</li>
<li>Nemesis X ≈792 years, with a broader transit window.</li>
</ul><h2 id="the-first-hard-failure">The first hard failure</h2><p>Treating these as the three pairwise beat periods of three
constant-rate rotators fails. Once two relative angular rates are fixed,
the third is constrained. No assignment of 138, 395 and 792 to the three
pairwise differences works exactly.</p><p>That failure was productive. It forced the model to distinguish
different kinds of recurrence:</p><ul>
<li>full rotations;</li>
<li>gate crossings;</li>
<li>half/full phase returns;</li>
<li>radial growth thresholds;</li>
<li>slower modulation envelopes.</li>
</ul><h2 id="the-eight-gate-phoenix-construction">The eight-gate Phoenix
construction</h2><p>A fitted toy model gave shell A a 1104-year turn relative to the dead
frame. Eight equally spaced gates then produce:</p><p><strong>1104 / 8 = 138 years.</strong></p><p>Two opposite gates were made stronger, yielding a 552-year primary
spacing. Shell B was given a 736-year turn, so in 2208 years:</p><ul>
<li>A completes 2 turns;</li>
<li>B completes 3 turns.</li>
</ul><p>The relative phase repeats after 2208 years. This can be represented
as relative rates 0:2:3, or after adding a common frame as an equivalent
5:3:2 pattern.</p><p>The strength pattern was fitted with an explicit score; it was not
independently derived. The value of the exercise was conceptual:
<strong>events can occur at repeated gates inside a slower global
cycle.</strong></p><h2 id="the-unresolved-spatial-condition">The unresolved spatial
condition</h2><p>Angular alignment is not enough. If shells grow, two angularly
aligned world layers may sit at different radii or heights. A real
mechanism must specify:</p><ul>
<li>shell radii and thickness;</li>
<li>axis orientation;</li>
<li>chirality;</li>
<li>growth rate;</li>
<li>location of each stabilized world layer;</li>
<li>how old turns remain accessible;</li>
<li>what force actually couples overlapping layers.</li>
</ul><p>The mature lattice model is partly an attempt to supply that missing
spatial mechanism.</p><hr/><h1 id="12-fibonacci--ammonite-growth-and-the-395792-near-relation">12.
Fibonacci / ammonite growth and the 395–792 near relation</h1><p>The ammonite image suggested self-similar growth. A continuous toy
law was tested:</p><p><strong>R(t)=R₀ φ^(t/552).</strong></p><p>Under that deliberately calibrated law:</p><ul>
<li>one φ increase takes 552 years;</li>
<li>a √2 scale increase takes about <strong>397.56 years</strong>;</li>
<li>a doubling takes about <strong>795.11 years</strong>.</li>
</ul><p>Those values sit close to the proposed ~395 and 792 tracks, but they
are not exact. Repetition accumulates phase error. If doubling is forced
to exactly 792 years, the implied φ-step becomes about 549.84 years and
a quarter-step about 137.46, not exactly 552 and 138.</p><p>The simple law therefore does not derive all target periods
simultaneously.</p><p>The later braid added a second reason the 395/792 pair is
interesting. A two-strand system naturally distinguishes an
<strong>opposite-phase half cycle</strong> from a <strong>same-phase
full cycle</strong>. If the full recurrence is 792, half is 396—one year
from the ~395 chart value. That is a structurally natural 2:1 relation,
but it is still not proof that those chronology tracks describe a
physical braid.</p><h2 id="144000-days-is-not-144000-in-size">144,000 days is not 144,000
in size</h2><p>The research also separated two very different uses of 144,000:</p><ul>
<li>a duration of 144,000 days;</li>
<li>a hypothetical terminal shell size of 144,000 units.</li>
</ul><p>They have different dimensions. A number alone cannot turn time into
length. Under the toy φ law, choosing Rmax/R₀=144,000 produces a
conditional time of roughly 13,624.8 years, but the starting scale is
arbitrary. Fibonacci numbers do not stop at 144,000.</p><p>That distinction remains important whenever the same
sacred/chronological number appears in a physical-scale hypothesis.</p><h1 id="13-the-mature-braid-the-sky-rolls-into-a-finger-trap-scroll-rod">13.
