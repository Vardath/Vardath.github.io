radiance or divine approach repeatedly appear.</p><h2 id="axis-and-gate-become-perceptible">Axis and gate become

<li>world tree;</li>
<li>rod or pillar;</li>
<li>cross;</li>
<li>ladder;</li>
<li>tunnel;</li>
<li>wheel or eye;</li>
<li>guarded portal.</li>
</ul><h2 id="layers-approach">Layers approach</h2><p>The active braid changes adjacency. Normally separated regions can
become close through the throat. “Heaven opens” is the mythological
expression of this phase in the Vardath reading.</p><h2 id="contact">Contact</h2><p>Beings, information, debris or energy may cross the temporary

to this role in the speculative synthesis.</p><h2 id="deformation-and-catastrophe">Deformation and catastrophe</h2><p>The surface rubble does not remain rigid while the substrate moves.
