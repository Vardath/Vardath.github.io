<h2 id="historical-archive-and-living-model--february-2025-to-september-2026">Historical
archive and living model — February 2025 to September 2026</h2><div class="callout good"><p><strong>Working thesis:</strong> a long-running speculative
investigation into whether recurring forms in myth, pictographs,
numerical systems, old maps, catastrophic traditions and the shape of
the lands can be organized as different views of a single structured
event: an enclosed world with an axis and paired channels, periodic
activation, opening or convergence of normally separated regions,
violent redistribution of water and surface material, and
re-stabilization into a new phase.</p></div><blockquote class="source-boundary">
<p><strong>Research boundary.</strong> This is a personal research
model, not established cosmology. The page keeps four things separate:
(1) what the old conversations actually proposed, (2) what source
traditions and physical measurements independently say, (3) mathematical
toy models we constructed, and (4) later Vardath interpretations that
connect them. Similar symbols do not prove a common physical event.</p>
</blockquote><hr/><h1 id="1-the-investigation-before-the-three-shells">1. The
investigation before the three shells</h1><p>The present three-layer / braided-lattice model is not where the
cosmology began. The earliest recoverable thread reaches back to
<strong>26 February 2025</strong>, when the Rainbow Serpent was
reimagined as a charged, plasma-like or filamentary phenomenon whose
motion could shape Country: carving channels, lifting land, producing
storms, water, coloured sky phenomena and, in the speculative retelling,
participating in the origin of life. A later version of that same story
had the Serpent bite a plasma-like Sun, provoke dangerous flaring, and
be forced away by wind and water.</p><figure class="vdiagram"><h4>Diagram 1 · Development spine</h4><svg viewbox="0 0 1100 400"><line class="soft" x1="70" x2="1030" y1="210" y2="210"></line><path class="cyan" d="M80 210 C150 85 245 85 315 210 C385 335 480 335 550 210 C620 85 715 85 785 210 C855 335 950 335 1020 210"></path><path class="violet" d="M80 210 C150 335 245 335 315 210 C385 85 480 85 550 210 C620 335 715 335 785 210 C855 85 950 85 1020 210"></path><g><circle class="node" cx="80" cy="210" r="9"></circle><circle class="node" cx="245" cy="210" r="9"></circle><circle class="node" cx="400" cy="210" r="9"></circle><circle class="node" cx="550" cy="210" r="9"></circle><circle class="node" cx="705" cy="210" r="9"></circle><circle class="node" cx="860" cy="210" r="9"></circle><circle class="node" cx="1020" cy="210" r="9"></circle></g><text x="40" y="175">Feb 2025</text><text class="muted" x="20" y="150">Rainbow Serpent</text><text x="195" y="260">late 2025</text><text class="muted" x="185" y="284">Squatter Man</text><text x="350" y="175">Dec–Feb</text><text class="muted" x="324" y="150">enclosure/caduceus</text><text x="510" y="260">Aug 2026</text><text class="muted" x="475" y="284">one phenomenon</text><text x="660" y="175">JANUS / 8</text><text class="muted" x="642" y="150">gates + lattice</text><text x="810" y="260">Exploration of 8</text><text class="muted" x="810" y="284">state-space method</text><text x="960" y="175">Sep 12–13</text><text class="muted" x="955" y="150">shell/braid</text><text class="muted" x="320" y="365">persistent structure: axis/serpent · enclosure/threshold · activation/reset</text></svg><figcaption>The latest shell model is shown as the endpoint of a much older investigation, not its beginning.</figcaption></figure><p>Already present were three ideas that later became central:</p><ul>
<li>a <strong>serpentine energetic structure</strong> rather than a
static object;</li>
<li>a world whose <strong>land and water are physically
reshaped</strong> by that structure;</li>
<li>a cycle of <strong>activation, danger, water, retreat and
renewal</strong>.</li>
</ul><p>By late 2025 the research had moved toward the recurring
anthropomorphic petroglyph sometimes called <strong>Squatter
Man</strong>. The interest was not simply that an ancient image “looked
like a person.” Its geometry could be read as an axis with a pinched or
hourglass body and paired lateral structures. Anthony Peratt’s
controversial plasma-petroglyph hypothesis supplied one possible
physical vocabulary: high-current plasma instabilities can produce
columnar, pinched and lobed forms. This was never settled archaeology;
it became a visual hypothesis to compare against other axis figures.</p>
