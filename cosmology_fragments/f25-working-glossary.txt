<hr/><h1 id="22-working-glossary">Working glossary</h1><p><strong>Axis</strong> — central geometric connector: rod, tree,
mountain, cross-line or scroll-rod.</p><p><strong>Active braid</strong> — two opposed strands linking
neighbouring stable shell turns.</p><p><strong>Aetio/Atel/Ase branch</strong> — August 2026 role-equivalence
hypothesis around centre/generator/threshold; not an etymological
theorem.</p><p><strong>Convergence throat</strong> — region where surface directions
narrow into an axial state.</p><p><strong>33rd direction</strong> — modern Vardath name for the axis
through a 32-bearing surface network; not a historical compass
point.</p><p><strong>Gate</strong> — privileged position/phase where transition or
coupling is possible.</p><p><strong>Operator</strong> — narrative agent associated with
controlling/crossing a gate; not necessarily identical with the
apparatus.</p><p><strong>Vehicle</strong> — moving interface such as
chariot/wheel/mount; Sleipnir occupies this role in the user’s
model.</p><p><strong>Manifestation</strong> — visible form of the activated
structure: eyes, wings, serpents, hourglass, many limbs, etc.</p><p><strong>Stabilized turn</strong> — world-region after the active
braid reopens and its geography settles.</p><p><strong>Rubble layer</strong> — fractured land material carried and
deformed by the hypothetical substrate.</p><p><strong>Reset</strong> — catastrophic transition involving water/land
displacement and subsequent new stability.</p><p><strong>JANUS core</strong> — frozen audited mathematics.
Symbolic/physical comparisons are explicitly outside the theorem
core.</p><p><strong>Exploration of 8</strong> — state-space/gate research
framework that classifies exact mathematics separately from
cultural/historical interpretation.</p><hr/>
