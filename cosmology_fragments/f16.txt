<h2 id="how-meltology-fits-the-cosmology-if-treated-as-a-hypothesis">How
Meltology fits the cosmology if treated as a hypothesis</h2><p>The shell-lattice event already predicts a period of extreme surface
deformation. A high-energy activation could, in principle, add
thermal/electrical effects to the mechanical movement. Meltology
therefore sits at the <strong>Reset / surface-material</strong> stage of
the event architecture rather than at the Axis/Gate stage.</p><div class="callout good"><p>The strongest version is not “every mountain is a melted building.”
It is a testable narrower proposition:</p></div><blockquote>
<p>Some anomalous masonry-like formations claimed by Meltology may
preserve high-temperature transformation textures. If the Vardath event
involved strong electrical/plasma heating, those samples should show a
coherent, datable mineralogical signature distinguishable from ordinary
geology and ordinary fire damage.</p>
