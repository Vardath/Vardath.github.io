<p>The worlds or layers are successive stabilised turns of a larger growing spiral structure. The inhabited world is one settled turn, with a previous/lower turn beneath it and a next/upper turn forming beyond it. In the present model each inhabited turn is a <strong>broad, materially deep flat world-disc</strong> supported and enclosed by a woven lattice-dome; the larger world-body can still curve, spiral and grow around those locally flat inhabited surfaces. Between and through those turns runs a two-directional woven lattice: one part of the structure can move upward while another moves downward, the strands crossing without needing to be cut.</p>
<p>During a quiet phase the lattice is broad and the worlds appear separated and stable. Under changing tension it can gather toward one or many convergence throats, collapse its many surface directions toward an axial state, and narrow into the scroll-like finger-trap rod. In that contracted phase the geometry can change which layers are adjacent, open temporary gates between them, and carry energy, atmosphere, water, debris or other material through the axial path. When the tension changes again, the structure reopens and a new stable arrangement forms.</p>
<h2 id="paired-rods-narrow-wide-path">Paired rods — narrow path / broad path</h2>
<div class="callout warn"><strong>Provenance check.</strong> The striking rock-art-style image that prompted this comparison is <strong>not being treated here as authenticated prehistoric art</strong>. A surviving web trail records a July 2025 Facebook post in <em>Ancient Wonders of Archaeology, Art History &amp; Architecture</em> that claimed spectacular images of this type came from Fate Bell Shelter in Seminole Canyon, Texas. The later source audit preserving that trail explicitly judged those particular paintings to be fake, while separating them from genuine Lower Pecos photographs. I have not yet established a pixel-for-pixel match between the exact JPEG supplied to this project and the vanished Facebook file, so its precise origin remains unverified. The image is retained only as the visual trigger for the geometric comparison below, not as archaeological evidence.</div>

<h3 id="paired-rods-what-i-see">What I see in the viral image</h3>
<p>What caught my eye is a piece of geometry rather than an archaeological identification. Two dominant vertical anthropomorphic or axial forms occupy the centre of the composition. The left-hand central form is extremely narrow, tapering and cord-like; the larger central form is much broader, patterned and body-like. Long lateral limbs branch away from both axes, while the main forms remain visually continuous in the vertical direction. In the Vardath model I read those two widths as a useful picture of <strong>two states of one finger-trap geometry: a constricted rod and an opened or broadened rod</strong>.</p>
<p>This produces a comparative echo with the narrow-gate teaching in Matthew 7:13–14: the narrow gate and way are associated with life, while the broad way is associated with destruction. I am <strong>not</strong> claiming that Lower Pecos artists encoded Matthew, and the uncertain/likely modern provenance of the viral image means it cannot establish an ancient connection. The useful observation is geometric: <strong>narrow axial state ↔ restricted passage; broad axial state ↔ opened passage</strong>. In a woven finger-trap, narrowing and broadening are mechanically linked states of the same tube, which is why the paired forms immediately suggested the comparison.</p>
<p class="equation"><strong>one woven axis → constricted / narrow state ↔ restricted path → opened / broad state ↔ discharge, rupture or destructive path</strong></p>

<p>A second core part of the present model is the <strong>vapour-canopy / upper-water flood cycle</strong>, and I now place it inside the active-door phase rather than treating it as a permanent background atmosphere. As the door opens and the plasma environment intensifies, the model proposes that the frozen upper-water reservoir is heated: <strong>ice melts → liquid water steams → a dense vapour canopy forms</strong>. During that interval the atmosphere becomes hotter, wetter and more strongly pressurised, with the high-oxygen/plasma-rich biological conditions explored later on this page. As the event weakens, <strong>the world cools → the steam condenses → the canopy falls as floodwater</strong>. In this version, the vapour canopy and the flood are two successive phases of the same water cycle.</p>
<p class="equation"><strong>stable shell → activation → convergence → axial gate / scroll rod → layer contact and transfer → deformation / catastrophe → reopening → stabilised world</strong></p>
<p>The familiar symbols in the archive are read as different views of that changing geometry. Serpents and dragons describe the winding or braided current; trees, mountains, pillars, crosses, ladders and rods describe the gathered axis; wheels and eyes describe nodes or end-on views; arks, baskets, eggs, caves and enclosed worlds describe contracted or protected carrier states; opened heavens, floods, fires and wars of the gods describe the transition; and renewed worlds describe the reopened stable phase.</p>
<p>The three-world intuition therefore becomes three phases of one continuing growth process rather than three permanently independent cosmic objects: <strong>previous/lower stabilised turn → present inhabited turn → next/upper forming turn</strong>, with the active braid as the mechanism joining them.</p>
<p>The shortest statement of the model remains:</p>
<p class="equation"><strong>SERPENT → AXIS → ENCLOSURE → GATE → POLARITY → LATTICE → SHELL → SCROLL ROD → RESET → NEW WORLD</strong></p>
