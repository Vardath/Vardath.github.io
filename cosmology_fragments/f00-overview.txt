<h1 id="current-cosmological-overview">Current cosmological overview — read this first</h1>
<p>This is the present working shape of Vardath Cosmology. The rest of the page preserves how the idea was reached, including earlier versions, tests, failed branches and later refinements.</p>
<p>The worlds or layers are successive stabilised turns of a larger growing spiral structure. The inhabited world is one settled turn, with a previous/lower turn beneath it and a next/upper turn forming beyond it. Between and through those turns runs a two-directional woven lattice: one part of the structure can move upward while another moves downward, the strands crossing without needing to be cut.</p>
<p>During a quiet phase the lattice is broad and the worlds appear separated and stable. Under changing tension it can gather toward one or many convergence throats, collapse its many surface directions toward an axial state, and narrow into the scroll-like finger-trap rod. In that contracted phase the geometry can change which layers are adjacent, open temporary gates between them, and carry energy, atmosphere, water, debris or other material through the axial path. When the tension changes again, the structure reopens and a new stable arrangement forms.</p>
<p class="equation"><strong>stable shell → activation → convergence → axial gate / scroll rod → layer contact and transfer → deformation / catastrophe → reopening → stabilised world</strong></p>
<p>The familiar symbols in the archive are read as different views of that changing geometry. Serpents and dragons describe the winding or braided current; trees, mountains, pillars, crosses, ladders and rods describe the gathered axis; wheels and eyes describe nodes or end-on views; arks, baskets, eggs, caves and enclosed worlds describe contracted or protected carrier states; opened heavens, floods, fires and wars of the gods describe the transition; and renewed worlds describe the reopened stable phase.</p>
<p>The three-world intuition therefore becomes three phases of one continuing growth process rather than three permanently independent cosmic objects: <strong>previous/lower stabilised turn → present inhabited turn → next/upper forming turn</strong>, with the active braid as the mechanism joining them.</p>
<p>The shortest statement of the model remains:</p>
<p class="equation"><strong>SERPENT → AXIS → ENCLOSURE → GATE → POLARITY → LATTICE → SHELL → SCROLL ROD → RESET → NEW WORLD</strong></p>
