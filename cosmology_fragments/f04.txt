recalled branch</h2><p>The Christian cross entered the investigation through the
hanging-figure geometry. The comparison is not “Jesus was a plasma
discharge.” It is structural: a human body fixed to a vertical axis
crossed by a horizontal member produces the same basic silhouette that
the Squatter-Man/hourglass/plasma discussions kept returning to.</p><p>The mature interpretation adds perspective: the cross can function as
an <strong>axis plus transverse plane</strong>, while the hanging body
personifies the point where those dimensions meet. In a fourfold
upper-world model, the cross also resembles four directions/rivers
around a centre. These are Vardath comparisons, not claims about
historical Christian doctrine.</p><h2 id="buddha-beneath-the-bodhi-tree--user-recalled-branch">Buddha
beneath the Bodhi tree — user-recalled branch</h2><p>The independent source motif is firm: Buddhist tradition locates
awakening beneath the Bodhi tree at Bodh Gaya, and early Buddhist art
could use the tree itself as a symbol of the Buddha and enlightenment.
The Vardath comparison treats this as an <strong>axis-tree + human
witness + revelation</strong> configuration.</p><p>Unlike Odin, Buddha is not hanging from the tree. That difference is
important. The shared structural element is not body position but the
relationship between <strong>human figure, central tree/axis and
transformative knowledge</strong>. In the event architecture, this
belongs mainly to the <em>Witness</em> and <em>Axis</em> roles rather
than catastrophe.</p><h2 id="odin-and-yggdrasil--partly-recovered-directly">Odin and
Yggdrasil — partly recovered directly</h2><p>The August conversations already connected Odin and Sleipnir to the
recurring phenomenon. The source tradition adds a strong axis/hanging
motif: in Hávamál, Odin says that he hung on a windy tree for nine
nights, wounded by a spear, sacrificing himself to himself, then takes
up the runes and gains knowledge.</p><p>For Vardath Cosmology this is a remarkably dense structural
package:</p><p><strong>world tree / axis → suspended figure → wound/spear/rod →
ordeal → downward gaze → acquisition of symbols/knowledge → return
transformed.</strong></p><p>That does not prove a physical event. It does explain why Odin became
a central comparison once the Squatter-Man and hanging-axis patterns
were already in play.</p><h2 id="loki-and-sleipnir--partly-recalled-partly-recovered">Loki and
