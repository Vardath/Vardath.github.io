# WNG Master Plan Append — Late Rebuild Reconciliation + Asuran Statue Traps

Date: 2026-09-16  
Design authority: Vardath  
Status: **ACTIVE CONTINUITY + PLANNED CONTENT. Newest explicit Vardath instructions override older wording.**

This append exists because a large amount of approved work moved from “future idea” to implemented local rebuild content during the D073–D095 sequence, while several older master-plan appendices still describe those systems as merely planned. It also records new ideas that had only existed in chat.

The purpose is twofold:
1. preserve the newest design decisions whether already implemented or still pending;
2. prevent a future continuation from treating an implemented system as missing, or from losing a valid side branch because of checkpoint/numbering confusion.

## 1. Current implementation authority and branch reconciliation

The local WNGv1 rebuild remains implementation authority. Website material is the durable design/continuity layer and must not be used to overwrite newer local implementation truth.

During the late rebuild, two different branches were both sealed under the label D095:
- **Hybrid stabilisation** — implemented as an adjunct to the Wraith retrovirus/suppression system; it adds a finite safety window against relapse and is not a cure.
- **Wraith feeding independence** — implemented later as risky gene therapy that removes only `WNG_LifeForceMetabolism`, restores ordinary Food need, removes Life-Force feeding abilities and leaves the same pawn otherwise Wraith.

These are both valid implemented systems, but they were produced as parallel branches from the D094 base. They must be merged into one later mainline checkpoint before either can be considered the sole complete continuation of D094.

No future session should silently discard either branch.

## 2. Late-rebuild systems that are already implemented

The following ideas are no longer merely future-plan material in the active rebuild history:

### Full DLC integration direction
WNG is being rebuilt against the full current DLC set where the native systems genuinely fit: Royalty, Ideology, Biotech, Anomaly and Odyssey.

### Ideology
A functional first Ideology layer exists for real WNG actions, including Wraith feeding morality and nanite/synthetic-life reactions. Beliefs are attached to committed gameplay events rather than decorative text.

### Royalty / Wraith telepathy
Wraith telepathy uses Royalty psychic/psycast grammar without pretending Wraith possess Empire Psylinks. Psychic capability is caste-sensitive, with Queens strongest.

### Iratus physical biology
Iratus is an ordinary organic animal. The exact attacking bug can attach to a host like a living implant, feed over time, regenerate while attached, and be removed through dangerous controlled surgery. The attached exact pawn is preserved rather than replaced by an abstract proxy.

### General animal containment and Comparative Xenobiology
Anomaly holding platforms are extended as biological containment for ordinary downed animals without reclassifying them as Void entities. Ordinary animals provide only trace research value; Iratus is the major gateway into Comparative Xenobiology and later xenopharmacology/implant work.

### Replicator sovereign swarm control
A Replicator Queen or Sovereign Neural Lattice bearer can deploy ten real controlled Drones when authority capacity permits. The deployed group shares a saved feral state and can be deliberately released back into autonomous hostile behavior. Child’s Toy retains its consume-matter-to-reproduce behavior and has a deliberate irreversible Go Feral command.

### Goa’uld shuttle fuel fallback
WNG-owned Goa’uld craft use ONAC liquid Goa’uld fuel and related ONAC economy when ONAC is present; without ONAC they remain playable using Chemfuel. Tretonin/tritonin remains ONAC-owned and must not be duplicated by WNG.

### Kassa economy
Kassa is implemented as a farmable, edible, strongly addictive staple. Processed Kassa derivatives and persistent faction-scoped repeat-customer demand are part of the implemented branch. The intended loop remains: useful nutrition and profit create dependence and recurring demand.

### Stargate pharmacology already implemented in the late branch
The rebuild has implemented substantial portions of the planned pharmacology catalogue, including:
- raw/refined Wraith enzyme and staged weaning treatment;
- Roshna as permanent maintenance dependence rather than a conventional stimulant;
- Nish’ta as one-use biological conditioning gas with electrical/EMP deprogramming and post-cure resistance;
- first-generation Hoffan treatment with severe recipient mortality and lethal consequences for a Wraith attempting to feed;
- Iratus gland extract and derived paralytic;
- Iratus Queen restorative treatment;
- Wraith retrovirus + recurring suppression therapy;
- Whispers-hybrid tissue analysis and derived sensory/mist implant technology;
- feeding-independence gene therapy as a high-risk late Wraith treatment.

### Whispers hybrid
The SGA “Whispers” creature is implemented as Michael’s early abandoned Human/Iratus/Wraith-derived experiment, not a normal Hive caste and not a Void entity. The design preserves permanent blindness, exceptional hearing and neck-gill fog used as a predatory tactical tool. Capture, biopsy and research are part of the progression.

## 3. Implemented branch requiring mainline merge — Hybrid Stabilisation

Hybrid stabilisation is valid implemented work but is not present in the later feeding-independence branch until the two are merged.

Intended behavior to preserve:
- only applies to a subject already under Wraith retroviral humanisation/suppression;
- does not initiate humanisation;
- does not cure Wraith ancestry;
- does not cure or convert Whispers hybrids;
- adds a separate finite safety window against relapse/reversion;
- redosing resets rather than stacks the window indefinitely;
- if both suppression and stabilisation expire, the original same-pawn Wraith restoration path resumes.

This system should be merged into the later mainline before additional dependent treatment work is layered on top.

## 4. NEW — Asuran statue infiltration/trap branch

Vardath adds a new Asuran trade/infiltration concept.

### 4.1 Asuran-made trade statues

Add one or more decorative Asuran statues to the trade economy.

Core rules:
- the statues are **Asuran-made objects**; ordinary factions/colonists should not be able to manufacture them through a generic sculpture recipe;
- they can appear through appropriate Asuran trade/supply channels;
- they should look like valuable decorative art rather than obvious weapons;
- their hidden behavior should be save-persistent and survive moving, trading, storing and loading.

### 4.2 Awakening statue

One statue variant is a delayed infiltration weapon.

Behavior:
- after acquisition/spawn, assign a hidden random awakening delay between **5 and 60 in-game days**;
- when the timer expires, the statue comes alive as a **hostile Asuran/human-form Replicator**;
- the event should occur from the physical statue itself rather than from an unrelated map incident;
- the resulting pawn is hostile even if the statue was legally purchased;
- this is intended to make apparently safe Asuran trade goods capable of functioning as sleeper infiltration devices.

Exact pawn kind, trade value, timer visibility and whether rare forensic clues can reveal the trap remain tunable.

### 4.3 Dark feeder statue

A second statue variant **does not awaken into an Asuran**. Instead it behaves as a concealed matter-harvesting nanite organism.

Behavior:
- it only advances its feeding cycle while kept in darkness / sufficiently low light;
- assign a hidden/random feeding interval between **5 and 20 in-game days**;
- when ready, it chooses a suitable nearby pawn and consumes that pawn as matter;
- the victim is genuinely lost/consumed rather than merely injured;
- the statue remains a statue afterward;
- feeding leaves behind recoverable **Asuran nanite sludge/residue/slurry** rather than a normal corpse;
- the product should connect to the existing Asuran nanite resource economy rather than inventing a redundant fuel unless a separate residue Def is mechanically useful.

This is intentionally exploitable by the player in a morally dark way: a player may leave the statue in a prison or other dark holding area and use prisoners/captives as feedstock to generate nanite material. That use is a feature, not an accidental exploit, though balance, Ideology consequences and prisoner-faction consequences may be added later.

### 4.4 Statue branch constraints

- Do not let ordinary colonists manufacture the trap statues simply because they have Art skill; manufacture must be Asuran-restricted or Asuran-source-only.
- Timers must be save-persistent.
- Moving the statue must not reset the hidden timer unless an explicit future design changes that rule.
- The dark-feeder timer should depend on darkness; light should pause or otherwise prevent feeding progress rather than allowing a brightly lit statue to eat someone on schedule.
- The dark-feeder statue should search a bounded nearby area/room rather than selecting arbitrary pawns across the entire map.
- The two trap behaviors are separate variants; the feeder does not later “wake up” and the awakening statue does not also farm prisoners.
- Trade generation should make both variants rare enough that their threat remains surprising rather than routine clutter.
- If Ideology integration is expanded, using prisoners as deliberate statue feedstock should be eligible for colony moral consequences.

## 5. Still-planned late branches not to lose

These remain approved ideas even where they are not yet mainline implementation:
- Reol recognition/memory manipulation, but only once its biological source/acquisition branch is coherent;
- symbiote poison, subject to ONAC/RimGate ownership/compatibility checks;
- broader Wraith diplomacy, client settlements and Queen/Hive favour;
- richer Ideology templates, roles and rituals once the underlying systems warrant them;
- additional Whispers/Michael laboratory sites rather than only the roaming escape incident;
- later refinement of dangerous first-generation medicines such as Hoffan serum and feeding-independence therapy;
- further comparative-xenobiology drugs, implants and organic–synthetic medicine where they have clear source biology.

## 6. Continuity rule from this reconciliation

When chat introduces a new WNG gameplay idea, record it in durable continuity and the appropriate master-plan append **even if it is not implemented immediately**. When an idea is implemented, update its status rather than leaving the plan describing it only as future work.

If implementation branches diverge, preserve both and record the merge obligation explicitly. Do not silently pick one branch and erase the other.

Static/API validation is evidence only. None of the above should be called compiled, Def-loaded, save-tested or live-playtested until RimWorld itself proves it.
