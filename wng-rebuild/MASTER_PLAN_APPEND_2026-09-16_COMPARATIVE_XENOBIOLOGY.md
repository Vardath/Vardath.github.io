# WNG Master Plan Append — Comparative Xenobiology / Animal Study Progression

Date: 2026-09-16
Design authority: Vardath
Status: **ACTIVE PLANNED CONTENT. Newest explicit Vardath instruction overrides older wording where this append conflicts.**

This append extends the Anomaly/Iratus branch and should be read together with `MASTER_PLAN.md`, `MASTER_PLAN_APPEND_2026-09-11_ANOMALY_AND_IDEOLOGY.md`, and `MASTER_PLAN_APPEND_2026-09-11_IRATUS_DIPLOMACY_PHARMACOLOGY_ROYALTY.md`.

## Core idea

WNG should treat biological life itself as something that can be studied, compared and learned from. Ordinary animals are not Void entities and should never be reclassified as Anomaly creatures merely to make the research system work, but biological diversity is still scientifically useful. A muffalo, thrumbo, insect, predator and Iratus bug are different organisms with different adaptations; studying those differences should produce a small but real research return.

The progression should therefore distinguish **containment**, **ordinary biological study**, and **true xenobiological/anomalous study** rather than collapsing them into one category.

## Holding-platform role

Anomaly holding platforms may be used as general biological specimen containment in WNG.

- Ordinary animals can be captured and held as exact pawns when appropriately downed/carryable.
- They remain ordinary RimWorld animals with their normal race/flesh classification.
- Containment must not silently convert animals into Anomaly/Void entities.
- Iratus uses the same physical custody grammar but is a much more important xenobiological specimen.
- Wraith and Asuran science can reasonably regard mundane planetary biology as comparative research material even when the organisms are not metaphysically anomalous.

## Ordinary-animal research yield

Studying ordinary animals should grant a **very small amount** of Anomaly/biological knowledge compared with genuine anomalies or Iratus.

The intended scale is deliberately low: ordinary animals are useful observations, not an exploit for rapidly completing the Anomaly research tree.

Preferred progression behavior:

- first meaningful study of a species should be worth more than repeatedly studying the same species;
- repeated study may still produce trace knowledge but should have diminishing value;
- studying a broader range of species should be more useful than farming one captive animal indefinitely;
- rare, extreme or biologically unusual animals may justify somewhat greater comparative value than common livestock;
- exact values remain balance parameters and should not be treated as immutable design law.

This system should encourage a specimen collection / comparative biology playstyle without turning every animal into a high-yield research object.

## Iratus as the gateway specimen

The Iratus bug is the intended bridge from ordinary zoology into WNG xenobiology.

The colony should first encounter, capture and study Iratus through the physical attachment/removal and holding-platform systems. Iratus research teaches the colony that apparently "animal" biology can contain mechanisms far outside ordinary terrestrial expectations: aggressive parasitic attachment, life-energy feeding, extreme regenerative response and unusual host interaction.

A dedicated **comparative xenobiology / animal research** project should therefore remain locked until the player has completed the relevant Iratus study or Iratus-gated research milestone.

The exact project name is not locked; working concepts include **Comparative Xenobiology**, **Exotic Zoology**, or **Xenobiological Survey Methods**.

## Comparative Xenobiology research node

Once Iratus has been sufficiently studied, a new research project should unlock the ability to systematically extract useful knowledge from contained ordinary animals.

The node should formalize:

- safe biological specimen handling;
- cross-species comparative anatomy and physiology;
- unusual immune, regenerative and metabolic mechanisms;
- neural and behavioral response mapping;
- adaptation to hostile environments;
- comparative parasitology and symbiosis.

This is not simply another generic research-speed bonus. It should enable actual downstream WNG content.

## Downstream late-game biology branch

Comparative animal research should become one prerequisite stream for later WNG biological technology, alongside the relevant Wraith, Iratus, Asuran and Anomaly discoveries.

Potential late-game branches include:

### Xenopharmacology

Drugs and treatments derived from comparative animal, Wraith, Iratus and synthetic-biological observations. Possible functions include controlled regeneration, trauma stabilization, temporary metabolic suppression, resistance to parasitic attachment, improved recovery from Life Force drain, or specialized combat/medical preparations.

These should have real costs, side effects and lore-consistent ingredients rather than functioning as free stat potions.

### Adaptive biological implants

Implants inspired by naturally occurring structures and Wraith/Iratus physiology: enhanced healing, sensory adaptation, toxin or disease resistance, circulatory support, neural stabilization, environmental adaptation, or controlled metabolic modification.

These should remain distinct from Asuran nanite implants. A biological implant and a synthetic nanite augmentation may interact, but they are not the same technology.

### Life-force / host-interface medicine

Late research may combine Iratus feeding biology with Wraith Life Force knowledge to develop better diagnostics, stabilization procedures, protective implants or medical countermeasures. This should not trivially duplicate Wraith feeding powers or remove their narrative danger.

### Organic–synthetic comparative medicine

Once Asuran/human-form Replicator biology is understood, comparative xenobiology can help explain the boundary between living tissue and nanite-maintained synthetic bodies. This may support specialized interfaces, medicines, reconstruction aids or compatibility technology without erasing the distinction between Wraith biology and Asuran nanites.

## Progression principle

The intended arc is:

**capture ordinary life -> encounter Iratus -> study Iratus -> unlock systematic comparative xenobiology -> study diverse animal biology for small cumulative gains -> combine that foundation with Wraith / Asuran / Anomaly discoveries -> unlock advanced drugs, implants and biological technologies.**

The system should reward curiosity and breadth rather than repetitive grinding.

## Implementation constraints

- Do not classify ordinary animals as Anomaly entities just to satisfy vanilla holding-platform code.
- Preserve exact animal pawn identity through capture, holding, transfer and release.
- Ordinary animal study yields must remain small relative to genuine anomaly research.
- Prefer species-first-study bonuses and diminishing repeat returns if practical and save-safe.
- Research gates should use real completed study/discovery state, not hidden arbitrary flags.
- Iratus must remain the first major gateway into this branch.
- Downstream drugs/implants should require relevant Wraith/Asuran/Anomaly research as appropriate rather than becoming available from animal study alone.
- Numbers, exact project names, ingredient costs and final tech effects remain tunable until live gameplay testing.
