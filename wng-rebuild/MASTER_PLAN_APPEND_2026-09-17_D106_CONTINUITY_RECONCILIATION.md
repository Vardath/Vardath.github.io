## 3. Conversation-feature reconciliation — implemented and preserved

The following ideas introduced or refined in the late rebuild are no longer merely chat notes. They are represented in durable continuity and, where stated, implemented in the current clean rebuild line.

### Iratus / Anomaly / comparative xenobiology
- Iratus bugs are real biological animals rather than abstract anomaly entities.
- Their signature attack is a persistent living attachment around the neck/upper body, with worsening severity over time, feeding/regeneration while attached, paralysis/immobilisation, and meaningful medical removal.
- The exact attaching Iratus pawn is preserved rather than replaced by a proxy effect.
- Ordinary animals can use Anomaly holding-platform containment without being reclassified as Void/Anomaly entities.
- Ordinary animal study gives only trace biological/anomaly research value; Iratus study is the gateway into Comparative Xenobiology.
- Comparative Xenobiology supports later xenopharmacology/implant branches rather than turning ordinary livestock into high-yield anomaly research farms.
- Iratus-derived paralytic/xenopharmacology and rare Iratus Queen restorative treatment are implemented in the clean rebuild line.

### Wraith biology, pharmacology and transformation
- Wraith enzyme harvesting, raw/refined enzyme, weaning treatment, Hoffan countermeasure, retroviral/suppression therapy and feeding-independence gene therapy are represented in the rebuilt progression.
- Feeding-independence treatment is intentionally high-risk and removes only the Life Force metabolism dependency rather than stripping unrelated Wraith identity/caste/telepathy genes.
- Iratus/Wraith restorative treatment remains dangerous rather than a universal healing potion.

### Wraith Royalty/telepathy layer
- Wraith telepathy uses native Royalty psychic/ability grammar where appropriate without making Wraith Imperial nobles.
- Hive Sense, Predator's Presence, Compulsion and Queen's Command have been implemented in the rebuild line with caste restrictions.
- Queen-court/favour/permit mechanics remain a separate deferred expansion unless Vardath advances them.

### Replicator sovereign deployment / Child's Toy
- Replicator Queen and Sovereign Neural Lattice bearers can deploy a real controlled ten-Drone swarm subject to the accepted authority rules.
- The summoned group can be deliberately released into one shared feral autonomous domain.
- Child's Toy retains its consume-target -> two controlled Toys transaction and has an explicit irreversible Go Feral command for deliberate weaponisation on hostile maps.

### Kassa and Stargate pharmacology
- Kassa is a real farmable, nutritious staple crop with severe dependency risk rather than a trade-only drug token.
- Processed Kassa derivatives and persistent dependent-customer demand are part of the implemented economy.
- Roshna and Nish'ta are separate mechanics: Roshna as maintenance dependence; Nish'ta as biological conditioning with electrical deprogramming/resistance.
- Reol chemistry and other Stargate pharmacology remain subject to coherent source/ownership implementation rather than being generic reskinned drugs.
- Tretonin/tritonin remains ONAC-owned and WNG must not duplicate it.

### Goa'uld shuttle fuel fallback
- Player-owned WNG Al'kesh / Death Glider paths use ONAC's native Naquadah/liquid-fuel economy when ONAC is present.
- When ONAC is absent, WNG craft remain usable through the accepted Chemfuel fallback rather than becoming hard-disabled by an optional dependency.
- This fallback does not authorize inventing a standalone WNG Goa'uld capital-ship resource economy where no accepted route exists.

### Whispers / Michael hybrid branch
- The `Whispers` creature branch is treated as a Michael experiment, not a normal Hive Wraith caste.
- It is blind, has exceptional hearing, hunts at close range and produces a fog/smoke field from neck-gill biology.
- It has a dedicated encounter/research branch and derived biological implant technology in the rebuilt progression.

### Asuran trap-statue family — final normal-progression authority
Three distinct artifacts exist and must remain distinct:

1. **Sleeper / awakening statue**
   - traded through appropriate Asuran/Quiet Lattice stock;
   - first real map placement starts one hidden save-persistent 5–60 day deadline;
   - when mature and physically placed it becomes one hostile human-form Asuran/Replicator;
   - minification preserves the deadline and pauses execution.

2. **Dark-feeder statue**
   - never awakens into a pawn;
   - first real placement starts a 5–20 day biological feeding cycle;
   - only sufficiently dark installed time advances the cycle;
   - it may consume a nearby living flesh pawn and leave existing Asuran nanite slurry/residue;
   - deliberate placement in a dark prison with captive feedstock is intended gameplay;
   - it remains a statue and begins another cycle afterward.

3. **Replicator reliquary**
   - separate artifact;
   - first real placement starts a hidden 5–60 day deadline;
   - when mature and placed it releases exactly five real hostile autonomous Replicator Drones sharing one autonomous domain;
   - transactional failure must preserve the statue rather than half-release the group.

Normal/default manufacture of all three is **Asuran-only**. D100 implements this using the project's canonical Asuran/human-form nanite identity predicate rather than a second ad-hoc list. Trade availability remains valid. The OFF-by-default universal-crafting escape hatch is a separate user setting and does not redefine the normal progression rule.
