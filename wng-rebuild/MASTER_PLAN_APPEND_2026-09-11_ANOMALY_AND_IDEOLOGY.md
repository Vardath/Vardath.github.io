# WNG MASTER PLAN APPEND — ANOMALY + IDEOLOGY

Date: **2026-09-11**  
Author/design authority: **Vardath**

## STATUS

**PLANNED CONTENT ONLY. DO NOT IMPLEMENT THIS APPEND YET.**

This append records the intended use of RimWorld **Anomaly** and **Ideology** inside Wraith & Nanite Gravtech. It supplements `MASTER_PLAN.md` and does not replace existing Wraith, Replicator, Asuran, Stargate, Goa'uld, gravship, hunger, feeding, captivity or Queen contracts.

WNG is intended to require the full current RimWorld DLC set and may use native DLC systems where they suit the design: **Royalty, Ideology, Biotech, Anomaly and Odyssey**.

The central design principle is:

**Anomaly supplies containment, study, xenobiological horror and laboratory consequences. Ideology supplies the colony's moral, religious and political interpretation of those same systems.**

Wraith-derived biological anomalies are not automatically Void entities in lore. WNG may use Anomaly's containment/study grammar because it fits the gameplay, while still treating Wraith experiments as biological/xenobiological phenomena rather than pretending they are supernatural Void organisms.

---

# 1. BROADER WRAITH EXPERIMENTATION / XENOBIOLOGY BRANCH

Do **not** reduce this branch to a single generic "Michael hybrid" enemy type.

The branch should cover Wraith/Iratus-derived biological experimentation broadly, with Michael's research program as one major source of specimens and sites.

The player should be able to encounter, capture, contain and study selected Wraith-derived experimental organisms. The purpose is not simply to add tougher enemies; the content should create a progression from fear and uncertainty into biological understanding, treatment/countermeasures and potentially dangerous player experimentation.

Current intended categories:

## 1.1 Iratus-derived organisms

Iratus bugs and other directly relevant Iratus-derived biological material are a natural low-level xenobiology entry point.

Potential roles:
- live capture/containment where mechanically appropriate;
- biological study;
- tissue/genetic sampling;
- link into understanding Wraith feeding biology and hybridisation;
- laboratory escape/infestation risks where appropriate;
- research prerequisite for later hybrid physiology/retrovirus work.

Do not automatically convert these into generic bioferrite livestock. They are biological Stargate organisms, not Void creatures merely because Anomaly containment mechanics may be useful.

## 1.2 Early feral hybrid experiments — "Whispers" type

The abandoned forest settlement / fog encounter from **Stargate Atlantis: "Whispers"** is a required design reference.

These are an **early hybrid experiment class distinct from the later intelligent Human-Wraith hybrids**.

Canonical functional traits to preserve as design targets:
- derived from human captives subjected to hybridisation experiments;
- earliest human/Iratus-line experimentation also incorporating traits from several other life forms;
- no longer meaningfully ordinary human subjects;
- blind / eyes covered;
- hunt primarily through exceptional hearing, sound and vibration;
- make clicking or similar locating sounds;
- fast, feral and predatory;
- capable of climbing/scaling surfaces;
- gill-like structures in the neck emit a dense mist/fog;
- fog is a **predatory tool**, not merely cosmetic weather;
- the fog obscures prey and should create meaningful tactical danger;
- prolonged exposure may be hazardous/uncertain;
- source fiction also depicts the fog interfering with radio communication;
- even their creator could not reliably control them;
- suitable for stasis-pod / abandoned-laboratory encounters;
- capture and study should be possible when the player can incapacitate one without killing it.

Current encounter concept:

**Abandoned Fog Village / Hidden Hybrid Laboratory**

A forested settlement appears deserted. The player finds signs of vanished inhabitants, hidden tunnels/laboratory space and stasis chambers. Some chambers may be intact, some opened, some empty. The fog may begin before the player understands its source. Surviving experiments hunt through the settlement/lab by sound.

Possible encounter contents:
- exact surviving villagers or other human captives;
- sealed and broken stasis pods;
- live feral specimens;
- corpses / failed subjects;
- laboratory records;
- tissue/genetic samples;
- gas masks or environmental clues;
- missing or partially transformed people;
- evidence that additional specimens escaped the site;
- later follow-up encounter if some specimens were not accounted for.

This should become a genuine Anomaly-style containment/study encounter, not just a fog-themed raid.

## 1.3 Advanced Human-Wraith hybrids

Later, more stable/intelligent Human-Wraith hybrids are a separate class from the feral "Whispers" organisms.

Intended gameplay:
- can be fought and incapacitated;
- can be captured alive;
- can be contained/studied as Wraith-derived anomalies;
- should retain individual identity where they are humanlike individuals rather than disposable monster proxies;
- intelligence/social capability should distinguish them from feral early experiments;
- some may be hostile soldiers;
- some may be escaped/test subjects or unwilling victims;
- some may be candidates for treatment, stabilisation or rare rehabilitation/recruitment depending on later design decisions.

Do not collapse every hybrid into one xenotype/PawnKind/entity. Preserve meaningful experimental stages and behavioural differences.

## 1.4 Bug People / engineered combat organisms

Michael's deliberately bred combat organisms remain a possible separate entity class rather than merely a stat-upgraded hybrid.

They belong to the same broad Wraith-experimentation branch but should retain their intended role as purpose-built combat creatures.

## 1.5 Unstable / partial transformations

Potential content includes humans or Wraith caught part-way through experimental treatment, retroviral suppression, reversion or failed hybridisation.

These can support:
- partially transformed rescue subjects;
- hidden/latent hybridisation;
- unstable containment patients;
- treatment decisions;
- reversion events;
- apparent human survivors whose condition later becomes obvious;
- laboratory moral choices rather than every encounter ending in extermination.

This should not simply copy Anomaly Metalhorror mechanics. If WNG uses concealed transformation, it needs its own biological logic and reveal conditions.

# 2. CAPTURE, CONTAINMENT AND STUDY

Vardath's current direction: **Wraith hybrids are anomalies that can be captured and studied.**

Anomaly's native containment/study loop should be reused where practical rather than inventing a parallel laboratory minigame.

Potential captured-specimen actions to design later:
- Study specimen;
- Extract tissue/sample;
- Stabilise condition;
- Attempt humanisation / Iratus suppression where canonically justified;
- Attempt controlled reversion where relevant;
- Investigate feeding physiology;
- Investigate regenerative biology;
- investigate telepathic/psychic traits where relevant;
- rehabilitate/recruit in rare suitable cases;
- euthanise/destroy dangerous specimens.

Not every entity should allow every action.

Containment risk should depend on the actual specimen:
- feral Whispers-type entities: sound-sensitive, fast, escape-prone, fog-producing;
- advanced intelligent hybrids: planning, social manipulation or coordinated escape potential;
- Iratus organisms: biological escape/infestation risk;
- unstable partial transformations: mutation/reversion/treatment failure risk.

Containment failure should produce the **same exact contained specimen** where identity matters; do not silently substitute a newly generated proxy.

# 3. XENOBIOLOGY STUDY PROGRESSION

Current conceptual progression:

**Wraith Xenobiology -> Iratus Genome -> Hybrid Physiology -> Retroviral Manipulation -> Controlled Wraith-Gene Suppression / treatment countermeasures**

Names are placeholders until implementation design is approved.

Study can reveal, depending on specimen/source:
- human ancestry;
- Wraith genetic components;
- Iratus-derived traits;
- feeding/Life Force physiology;
- regenerative processes;
- sensory modifications;
- fog-producing biological structures;
- experimental stabilisers/drugs;
- failed and successful hybridisation approaches;
- retroviral suppression mechanics;
- why some subjects are irreversible while others may be treatable;
- countermeasures useful against Wraith-derived creatures.

Research/data should come from actual specimens, laboratory evidence, samples and recovered records rather than appearing as a giant free technology dump.

Potential biological resources, if later approved:
- Wraith tissue;
- Iratus genetic material;
- hybrid tissue cultures;
- experimental serum/retroviral samples.

Do not default these resources to bioferrite unless a later explicit design gives a genuine reason.

Planned encounter families include:

- Michael laboratory with multiple generations of test subject;

- hybrid transport/recovery mission;
- laboratory archive/sample recovery;
- colony containment failure after bringing a live specimen home;
- delayed reveal that not every rescued test subject was fully human/stable.

The player should sometimes face a choice between destroying a dangerous site immediately and preserving valuable specimens/data for study.

# 5. IDEOLOGY — GENERAL PRINCIPLE

Ideology should not merely add faction-coloured symbols. WNG should expose meaningful **issues/precepts, memes, rituals and roles** that change how colonists interpret Wraith feeding, hybrids, containment, synthetic life, Goa'uld symbiosis and precursor technology.

A major cross-DLC goal is that **the same captured hybrid can mean different things to different colonies**:

- a victim who must be cured;
- a dangerous organism that should be destroyed;
- a scientific subject whose biology must be understood;
- a valuable source of technology;
- a legitimate new form of life;
- the first step toward deliberate transhuman/xenobiological transformation.

Ideology should influence mood/opinion/ritual/research decisions where native mechanics support it, but exact gameplay effects are not yet locked.

# 8. HUMAN / ATLANTIS-STYLE IDEOLOGY CONCEPTS

Human colonies should not all react identically to Wraith-derived biology.

Example ideological combinations:

## Extermination-oriented survivor colony
- Wraith: Exterminate;
- Feeding: Forbidden/abhorrent;
- Hybridisation: Abomination;
- Xenobiological study: limited or acceptable only for countermeasures.

## Atlantis-style scientific stewardship
- Wraith: Distrust/Tolerate according to circumstances;
- Hybridisation: Cure when possible;
- Xenobiological study: Essential;
- Ancient technology: Stewardship;
- Containment: Cautious study.

## Morally dangerous experimental colony
- Hybridisation: Improvement;
- Xenobiological study: Essential;
- Genetic modification: Celebrated;
- Containment: Aggressive study;
- technological dominion: strong.

The same Anomaly mechanics should therefore support very different player stories without hard-coding one moral answer.

# 11. CROSS-DLC DESIGN RULE

The preferred relationship is:

**Biotech** — genes/xenotypes/biological identity where appropriate.  
**Anomaly** — containment, specimen study, hazardous laboratory entities/events.  
**Ideology** — moral/religious interpretation, precepts, rituals, specialist roles.  
**Royalty** — native high-tech/psionic/orbital systems where they genuinely fit.  
**Odyssey** — gravships, shuttles, orbital/world-layer systems.

Do not recreate a parallel WNG mechanic where a native DLC system provides the correct underlying behavior, but do not force lore-inappropriate Void/supernatural explanations onto biological Wraith content merely to use Anomaly mechanics.

# 12. OPEN DESIGN QUESTIONS — DO NOT RESOLVE BY IMPLEMENTATION YET

Before this branch is coded, explicitly design/review:
- which hybrid classes are humanlike pawns, Anomaly-style entities or both through integration;
- exact containment platform/cell requirements;
- exact study yields and research pacing;
- how fog affects sight, targeting, sound, communication and exposure;
- whether sound can deliberately lure Whispers-type entities;
- whether high-frequency sound becomes a countermeasure;
- whether walls/climbing requires a bespoke pathing mechanic or a simpler faithful approximation;
- treatment/cure/reversion success rules;
- whether some advanced hybrids can be rehabilitated/recruited;
- how latent/partial transformation is revealed without copying Metalhorrors;
- sample/resource names and uses;
- ideology mood/opinion consequences;
- ideology availability by faction/xenotype;
- ritual mechanics and rewards;
- how player ideology choices interact with existing Wraith hunger/feeding without merging those separate systems;
- exact art/audio/VFX requirements for fog, containment and specimens.

No implementation should be treated as approved merely because it is technically convenient. Vardath remains final design authority.
