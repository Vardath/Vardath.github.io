## Why this validation was run

The first ladder/sky pilot conditioned retrieval directly on `masonic + ladder`, leaving only 15 matched pairs and admitting unrelated hook-and-ladder material. V2 preserved that pilot as a failed/underpowered first attempt, then used broad Masonic symbolic-art retrieval with no ladder term in the search queries. Ladder/ascent, celestial, lattice-structure and axis vocabulary were scored only after acquisition.

## What the result actually teaches

This run tested **catalogue descriptions**, not the pixels of the art. The exact hit list shows the problem directly. `The Masonic Ladder` is an explicit historical Masonic ladder image, but its Commons metadata does not describe the visible composition using the frozen celestial or lattice vocabulary. Other ladder-positive hits are books, notices, buildings or stair/step references rather than clean symbolic-ladder plates.

Thus the corrected run successfully removes the original query contamination but reveals a different limitation: broad museum/file metadata is too sparse to resolve the proposed **visual** ladder ↔ sky relationship. The hypothesis is not confirmed by this test; it is also not fairly falsified at image level by absent catalogue words.

The next discriminating test, if pursued, should freeze visual criteria and score the images themselves: visible ladder geometry, Sun/Moon/stars/sky field, rails/rungs or repeated transverse crossings, pillars/axis, and their spatial relation.
