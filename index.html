About

Vardath is a brand-new, one-person sonic laboratory that began transmitting in January 2025. Using generative-AI tools as both muse and instrument, Vardath stitches together glitchy percussion, crystalline pads and unexpected melodic detours to create music that is at once strange, cinematic and quietly beautiful.

A self-described “truther,” he draws conceptual fuel from alternative-history ideas, turning fringe perspectives into shimmering, other-worldly soundscapes.
