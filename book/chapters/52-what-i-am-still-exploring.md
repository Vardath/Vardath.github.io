# Chapter 52 — What I Am Still Exploring

A cosmology can become a prison if every question has already been answered.

I do not want Vardath Cosmology to become that.

The whole project began because I kept finding connections that did not fit comfortably inside the picture I had before.

If I now pretend the current picture is finished, I would be repeating the same mistake at a larger scale.

So I want to end the main body of this book with the parts I am still exploring.

Not as a list of failures.

As the places where the world is still open.

## The primordial current

The deepest question is still the current.

I think the current is fundamental.

I think the serpent imagery, Peratt-like plasma morphology, the axial forms, the world-tree, the gate and the heat-altered surface all fit better if a persistent energetic current runs through the larger world-body.

I also think the earliest pulse may have been vastly stronger than later ones.

Perhaps strong enough to organise or create the physical matter that now encloses the world-space.

Perhaps the matter accumulated around the current.

Perhaps later pulses are damped by that matter.

Perhaps Squatter Man, Meru and the great axial sky forms are smaller later descendants of the original world-forming fire.

That picture connects many branches beautifully.

What I am still exploring is the exact physical nature of the current.

How is it sustained?

What carries it?

What closes the circuit?

How does it interact with the world-disc, the dome, the shell-turns and the surrounding matter?

How much of Anthony Peratt's plasma work can be carried across directly, and where does my cosmological extension leave the territory his work actually covered?

Those are not reasons to abandon the current.

They are the questions that tell me where to look next.

## The matter around the world-set

The idea that the primordial current may have created or organised the matter that now encloses the world-space is one of the strongest newer parts of the cosmology.

It may explain why the earliest world was more energetic.

It may explain why later pulses are smaller.

It may explain why the world-machine becomes increasingly enclosed.

It may explain why the active sky of older ages looks so different from the quiet sky now.

But the material itself needs a clearer picture.

What is it?

How is it arranged?

How does it grow?

Does it form the lattice dome directly?

Does it collect around the shell-turns?

Does it thicken between worlds?

Does it absorb or redistribute the current?

Does it behave differently during contraction?

This is one of the places where Meltology and the primordial-current branch may eventually meet at much larger scale.

The surface may contain small examples of what the world enclosure itself once did.

## The flat world and the larger growth

I am comfortable keeping the flat world-disc and the larger shell/world-tree growth together.

I no longer see them as competing pictures.

The inhabited world can be a broad flat or disc-like surface with real depth and a lattice dome above it.

That local world can still belong to a larger structure whose turns curve, spiral or grow like shell chambers.

Flat locally.

Curved or spiral in the larger growth.

The relationship makes sense to me.

What I still want to understand better is the exact transition between those scales.

How large is one disc relative to one shell-turn?

Does each turn contain one dominant inhabited disc?

Can several local discs belong to one larger turn?

How does the dome connect to the wider world-tree?

What does the world look like from outside the inhabited level?

Those are geometric questions still waiting for a fuller picture.

## The upper ocean

The upper-water branch remains central.

I think a water-bearing boundary between present and next world can explain a great deal.

Flood.

Waters above.

Opening heaven.

World sea.

Upper reservoir.

The first waters of the next world.

The idea that one boundary can be ceiling from below and floor from above remains one of the most elegant relationships in the model.

What I am still exploring is the physical support.

How does the water remain in place during the quiet world?

How thick is the boundary?

What happens to pressure across it?

How does it interact with the lattice dome?

How much of the water belongs to the next world rather than the present one?

What happens during partial openings?

Can local nodes leak without a global reset?

The architecture feels right to me.

The detailed behaviour still has room to grow.

## The breathing sky

The sky branch is also still developing.

I think the quiet sky and waking sky may be two states of one larger structure.

The broad field is the ordinary state.

Nodes can wake.

The field can converge.

An axial rod-body can appear.

The pulse can create Squatter-Man-like or Meru-like manifestations.

Then the sky can reopen.

What I do not yet want to force is the identity of every celestial object.

Sun.

Moon.

Planets.

Stars.

Some may be nodes.

Some may be bodies moving through the field.

Some may be local manifestations.

Some may be something I have not yet separated conceptually.

The early paired-endpoint Sun/Moon idea may still have a place at some scale or phase.

The Moon-projector idea may still connect to a local optical or lattice phenomenon rather than the entire sky.

I want to keep those possibilities alive without insisting on one answer too early.

The sky has more to teach me.

## The pulse and the rod-body

The large axial manifestation is another major open branch.

Squatter Man is one visual clue.

Mount Meru is another axial image.

World trees, pillars, caduceus forms and central mountains all sit around the same family.

The current model says these may be different views or scales of convergence.

That connection feels strong.

What I am still exploring is the exact relationship among them.

Is Squatter Man the visible plasma morphology around a deeper rod?

Is Meru the same event experienced from the world surface?

Does the world-tree become luminous only at strong pulse?

Does the primordial current create one giant axial structure through the entire world-set or many nested axes?

Can local nodes create smaller versions of the same form?

I suspect scale is the key.

The same geometry may repeat from local gate to world-axis to primordial creation current.

## Meltology

Meltology remains one of the areas I want to pursue most deeply.

The cosmology gives it a natural place.

The current concentrates.

The surface heats.

Material softens, vitrifies, foams, fuses, recrystallises or slumps.

Water arrives.

The altered material cools rapidly.

Sediment buries it.

Later people inherit forms that lie somewhere between architecture and geology.

I think that possibility is important.

I also think the category is probably broad.

Different sites may have different histories.

Some may be ordinary geological formations.

Some may be conventional ruins altered by fire or weathering.

Some may belong to processes not yet recognised.

Some may preserve exactly the kind of reset damage I am describing.

The interesting work is in seeing whether the strongest cases share a coherent material signature and event context.

I do not want Meltology reduced to one sensational claim.

I want to understand how it fits the world-machine physically.

## Moving lands

The moving-land branch is equally important.

The crust is cargo.

The gate changes adjacency.

The time door can involve world-level movement.

That makes it possible for land to shift vertically through the world-tree as well as laterally across the surface.

This may connect to lost civilisations, buried architecture, strange cultural discontinuities and lands that appear displaced in history.

What I still want to understand is the scale of coherence.

How large a region can cross without disintegrating?

Can a city move intact?

Can a whole island?

Can continents exchange pieces?

What determines whether the movement is mainly lateral, vertical or both?

How do the S-shaped land bends relate to world-level transfer?

How much deformation happens during transport rather than afterward?

These questions connect geology, history and time in a way I find extremely promising.

## Tartaria and the Old World

Tartaria and the wider Old World material remain open areas of exploration for me.

I do not need one simplified global empire story.

What interests me is the larger discontinuity.

Monumental architecture.

Buried lower levels.

Repurposed structures.

Melted or mountain-like forms.

Mud-flood imagery.

Reset stories.

Populations inheriting cities.

Historical compression.

Possibly moved land.

Possibly moved time.

The Vardath model can contain all of those without forcing them into one rigid chronology.

The question I want to keep asking is whether some Old World structures belong to a previous world-state that was physically inherited into the present one.

That is a much larger idea than one disputed map.

## The Maya and world-level movement

The Maya interest me for a different reason.

The landscape, chronology, abandoned centres, layered construction and deep mythic time all create another kind of discontinuity.

Perhaps some regions were moved.

Perhaps populations crossed.

Perhaps much of the story is ordinary cultural change.

Perhaps several processes overlap.

The point is not to decide too early.

The world-machine gives me a new category to explore:

**a civilisation can be divided between levels of the world rather than merely rising and falling on one unchanged surface.**

That possibility deserves more thought.

## Giants and active-world life

The waking-world biology is still wide open.

Different atmosphere.

Different pressure.

More moisture.

Different electrical conditions.

Different heat.

Different radiation.

Different available energy.

Biological transfer among world-turns.

All of those could change life.

The old stories of giants, long-lived beings, monstrous animals and extraordinary fertility may fit that environment in ways I have not yet understood.

Some giant imagery may be plasma projection rather than biology.

Some may be literal large beings.

Some may be cultural exaggeration.

Some may be different categories entirely.

I do not want to collapse them.

The active world may have been biologically stranger than the quiet world.

That is enough to keep the branch alive.

## Other beings

The operator question is even more open.

Who or what occupies the central role in some of these stories?

A god.

A rider.

A king.

A radiant being.

A giant.

A watcher.

A guide.

A survivor.

A natural process personified.

A real intelligence using the world-machine.

I do not know.

I am comfortable leaving that open.

The apparatus can exist whether or not the operator is a conscious being.

But some traditions speak about agency so strongly that I do not want to remove the possibility.

The cosmology has room for natural process, intelligence and cultural personification at the same time.

Future work may separate them more clearly.

## The time door

The time door is probably the largest open question in the entire model.

I think it follows naturally from the world-turn structure.

Previous, present and next become past, present and future.

The gate changes adjacency.

Therefore the gate can connect world-times.

That logic is internally simple.

The physical consequences are enormous.

Can a person meet another version of themselves?

Can one event appear at several historical times?

Can a city arrive from an older world?

Can a land move into a younger world?

Can the same traveller return to a future in which centuries have passed?

Can history branch?

Can it loop?

Can a world-turn be visited before it becomes dominant?

How is destination selected?

Does the gate connect only neighbouring turns or can stronger convergence bridge farther?

I do not know yet.

The connection between world-gate and literal time travel remains one of the most exciting unresolved parts of the cosmology.

## The recurring event

I also want to understand the recurrence better.

Is there one great event that cuts across multiple times?

Are there many repeated events?

Are local gate openings smaller versions of the same process?

Does every world-turn eventually pass through one major reset?

Can partial pulses occur without total world succession?

Are fairy-tale gate events local echoes while flood myths preserve global transitions?

Can a large pulse create many historical appearances because the time door connects different turns?

I suspect the answer may involve more than one of these.

The world-machine can recur physically and still produce temporally distributed memories.

That is the kind of layered answer the project repeatedly points toward.

## The role of consciousness

Consciousness sits at the edge of the model in a way I have not yet fully integrated.

A witness matters because observation is perspectival.

Mythic figures repeatedly gain knowledge by crossing boundaries.

Dreams, visions, trance, death-and-return and revelation often accompany world-crossing stories.

Perhaps those are only narrative forms.

Perhaps consciousness interacts with the gate differently from ordinary matter.

Perhaps altered states make local structure perceptible.

Perhaps the relationship is entirely cultural.

I do not know.

But I do not want to ignore the pattern simply because it is harder to mechanise.

The cosmology began with visible geometry and has gradually moved toward time.

Consciousness may be another layer waiting beyond that.

## The plenum

The plenum is also still growing as an idea.

A full world.

No meaningless void between levels.

Current, water, matter, medium and structure filling the larger body.

The tree woven through it.

The gate changing relationships inside it.

This may eventually help me understand gravity, pressure, support and motion more clearly.

Instead of asking how isolated objects act across nothing, I can ask how regions of one full structure influence one another.

That feels closer to the world I am building.

## What I do not want to do

I do not want to reach the end of this book and pretend the cosmology now explains everything.

It does not.

I also do not want to solve every open question by removing the strange part.

That would defeat the purpose of the project.

Flat world-disc geometry remains.

The lattice dome remains.

The upper ocean remains.

Meltology remains.

Moving lands remain.

Tartaria and Old World inheritance remain areas of exploration.

The time door remains.

The primordial current remains.

The possibility of different world-level biology remains.

These are not embarrassing leftovers.

They are the living edges of the model.

## What has become clearer

At the same time, the cosmology is much more coherent than when I began.

The serpent became current.

The current gained a lattice.

The lattice became woven.

The weave became fingertrap.

The fingertrap produced the axis.

The axis produced the throat.

The throat became the gate.

The gate changed adjacency.

The world-turns became past, present and future.

The gate became a time door.

The crust became cargo.

Land could move.

Heat produced Meltology.

Carriers preserved life.

The old world became inheritance.

The next world became successor.

Myth became camera angle and memory.

Those connections now form one structure in my mind.

That is what this book has been trying to show.

## The method continues

My method remains the same.

Look.

Compare.

Find the strongest similarities.

Keep the differences.

Ask whether the pieces can belong to different scales, phases or viewpoints of one larger process.

Do not force certainty where there is only possibility.

Do not throw away an idea merely because it does not fit the first version of the model.

Change the model if the larger pattern becomes stronger.

That is how Vardath Cosmology grew.

That is how I expect it to keep growing.

## The world that opens

The title of this book comes back to me here.

The world opens in several senses.

The boundary opens.

The lattice opens and closes.

The time door opens.

The next world opens into habitability.

The old world opens into memory.

The mythology opens when different camera angles are placed beside one another.

And the cosmology itself remains open.

That last part matters most.

I do not want the final page to close the world.

I want it to leave the reader standing where I am still standing:

looking at a serpent that became a current,

a current that became a tree,

a tree that became a gate,

a gate that became a door through time,

and wondering what else the world might become when it opens again.
