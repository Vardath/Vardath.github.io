# Introduction — A World That Changes Its Adjacencies

Imagine the ordinary world first.

Not an apocalypse. Not a giant tree filling the sky. Not a serpent of fire, a rolled firmament, a gate between realms or a flood covering the land.

Just the world as it appears now.

Land is land. Oceans have coastlines. The atmosphere circulates. The sky is broad. The Sun, Moon, planets and stars follow the regular motions we have learned to expect. A mountain stays where it was yesterday. Rivers still know which way is downhill.

That is where Vardath Cosmology begins.

At the centre of the model is one further idea that runs through this entire book: **the great gate is a natural door through time**.

I do not picture history as containing hundreds of unrelated supernatural portals. I picture one class of natural world-event recurring at different times. When the larger structure enters the gate-state, normally separated world-states can become temporarily adjacent. People living in different ages encounter the same underlying event from different places, cultures and stages of the cycle.

That is why the record is scattered.

One age remembers a world tree. Another remembers a fairy road, hollow hill or impossible journey. Another remembers a flood, a bridge, a mountain opening, a shining wheel, a serpent, a descent into an underworld, a visit to another realm, a meeting with someone who should belong to another time, or a land that seems to have moved from where history says it should be.

In my cosmology these are not automatically separate phenomena. They can be different human records of the same natural door appearing again and again through history.

I call the ordinary condition the **quiet world**.

Quiet does not mean motionless.

Everything can be moving and the world can still be quiet in the sense I mean. Weather moves. Water moves. Crust moves. Life grows. The sky changes hour by hour. The deeper current proposed by the model can also remain active.

Quiet means the larger structure is not forcing neighbouring world-states into contact.

The present world is stable enough to behave like one world.

That distinction is the foundation of everything that follows.

## The vapour canopy is part of the world-machine

One part of the model needs to be stated early because it changes almost everything that comes after it.

The **vapour canopy is not a decorative cloud layer and not an abandoned early idea**. In Vardath Cosmology it is a major environmental component of the enclosed world. It belongs with the upper waters, the lattice boundary and the waking atmosphere.

When that canopy is present in its strong form, I picture the inhabited world beneath it as a **high-pressure, oxygen-rich, water-rich environment**. The canopy helps contain and load the atmosphere. The larger electrical and plasma environment is also more active. Pressure, oxygen, moisture, heat and electrical activity therefore change together rather than as unrelated variables.

That environmental package is central to my biological picture of earlier or waking ages. In the model it supports giant plants, giant animals and giant or giant-like people. It also supports much longer biological persistence. I connect the pressure-and-oxygen regime with continued tissue repair and with the possibility that telomeres are maintained or regrown rather than steadily shortening under quiet-world conditions. Taken to its full consequence inside the model, organisms can remain alive and growing for extraordinary periods while the regime persists.

The same principle makes the biology stranger. A stronger plasma and electrical environment can increase mutation and developmental variation. Tissue that would normally fail quickly may remain viable much longer. In the most extreme version of the model, even separated or abnormal tissue can continue growing: a severed finger, a removed tumour or another fragment of living tissue may persist instead of simply dying because the surrounding environment continues to feed the processes of growth.

That is not a side note to the cosmology. It is one of the ways the atmosphere, the canopy and the world-cycle become biology. It is also why old images of enormous plants, oversized animals and creatures such as giant snails remain interesting to me as possible visual memories of a different environmental regime.

The quiet world is what remains when that regime is no longer dominant. Pressure falls. The atmospheric balance changes. The canopy state changes. The plasma environment becomes less intense. Biological scale, lifespan and mutation pressure change with it.

## One world inside a larger body

In Vardath Cosmology, the world we inhabit is not the entire structure.

It is one stabilised turn of something larger.

Behind or beneath it is an older turn: the previous world-state retained inside the larger growth.

Beyond or above it is another populated turn: the next world-state, developing in relation to us but already a world in its own right within the same body.

I use words like *above* and *below* because the model is layered, but the relation is also temporal.

The older turn is the inherited past.

The present turn is the world-state we currently inhabit.

The next turn is the developing future.

They are not three unrelated universes.

They are successive parts of one continuing structure.

One point needs to be explicit here: **all of these world-turns are populated**.

Previous, present and next do not mean dead world, inhabited world and unborn world.

They describe the worlds' relationship to the world I am standing in.

A world above or below can have its own inhabitants, ecology, history and stable order while remaining separated from us. The gate changes adjacency between populated worlds. It does not populate an empty one.

The closest ordinary image is a growing shell.

A shell keeps its earlier turns while adding new ones. The older material does not vanish merely because growth continues. The new turn develops in continuity with what came before.

That is the broad relation I am proposing.

The model does not require everyday geography to stop being geography. A city still has a location on the present surface. A road still goes from one place to another. A river still follows the terrain.

The larger world-body adds another kind of relationship: how the present surface sits among the neighbouring turns and what happens when those normally separated regions begin to approach one another.

That is where the lattice enters.

## The hidden lattice

I use the word **lattice** for the woven structure connecting the larger world-body.

I do not mean a rigid metal cage sitting visibly over the sky.

I mean a connected field of paths, crossings and nodes.

A woven structure can distribute movement.

It can carry tension.

It can contain local junctions.

It can be broad in one state and narrow in another.

That last property became the key.

The mature Vardath model contains paired, counter-wound movement inside the larger weave. One family of paths can have one axial sense while another has the opposite sense.

That is the mechanical descendant of the old paired-serpent image.

The serpent did not survive because I decided every mythic snake was secretly cosmic machinery.

It survived because a serpent is an extraordinarily natural image for a moving line.

Two serpents winding around one centre are an equally natural image for opposed movement organised around a shared axis.

Most of the time, the lattice is broad.

That broad state is the quiet world.

Paths remain distributed.

Local nodes exist without becoming full gates.

The sky remains an extended field instead of collapsing toward one dominant centre.

The neighbouring turns remain effectively separate.

Then the state begins to change.

I call that change **activation**.

## The current and the pulse

The model proposes a persistent deep current.

The exact physical carrier of that current is not settled. I am not pretending I have finished the physics simply because the structural idea is clear to me.

What matters at this stage is the difference between the current and the **pulse**.

The current persists.

The pulse is the active condition in which the current begins driving the world-machine into a different state.

This same distinction appears all through the cosmology.

The lattice exists before it contracts.

The boundary exists before it becomes a gate.

The node exists before it opens.

The world tree exists before it becomes traversable.

The event is therefore not the sudden appearance of a completely foreign mechanism.

It is a state change in something already present.

During activation, local preferred paths strengthen.

Nodes become more important.

The broad structure begins to organise.

Then comes **convergence**.

## From the broad world to the through-route

In the quiet world, direction is spread.

Movement around the surface dominates ordinary experience.

During convergence, more of the structure begins to gather toward an axial route.

This is not a claim that every compass bearing literally collapses into a pole.

It is a change in which geometry dominates.

The stable world is broad.

The active world becomes increasingly axial.

That means the world can move through a continuous visual sequence:

**field → weave → narrow strip → ladder-like form → rod → throat**

The great question was how one structure could pass through those states without becoming a completely different object.

The fingertrap supplied the best answer I have found.

## Why the fingertrap matters

A woven fingertrap is wide in one state and narrow in another.

Pull it along its length and the weave changes angle. The sleeve becomes longer and thinner. Release it and it can broaden again.

The strands remain the same strands.

That simple behaviour gave me a mechanical bridge between symbols that had previously looked unrelated.

The broad lattice did not need to disappear so that a rod could replace it.

The rod could be the lattice under contraction.

The ladder could be an intermediate view of the narrowed weave.

The throat could be the most concentrated state of the same structure.

That changed the cosmology from a collection of objects into a transformation.

A tree, ladder, pillar and rod no longer had to be competing answers to the question of what the world-axis was.

They could be different appearances or states of one family.

A wheel, eye, ring or star could be what a radial node looked like end-on.

A serpent could be one moving strand.

A paired-serpent image could be two opposed strands around the same centre.

The important claim is not that the symbols all historically meant the same thing.

The important claim is that the geometry can generate several of them.

## The gate

At maximum convergence, the throat takes on its most important function.

It changes **adjacency**.

That is the mature definition of the gate.

I do not imagine a permanent portal standing open between worlds.

I imagine normally separated regions becoming temporarily capable of direct exchange because the geometry that kept them apart has changed.

During the quiet state, the previous, present and next turns are effectively separate.

During the gate interval, some part of that separation weakens.

The present world can become connected to a region of the next world above or the previous world below.

The gate therefore does not need to be a doorway installed in the sky.

It is a phase of the structure.

This also explains why it closes naturally.

When the contraction ends, the geometry that created the unusual adjacency ends with it.

The lattice widens.

The throat loses dominance.

The neighbouring turns separate again.

The gate disappears because the world has changed state.

## What crosses

Once the gate exists, the next question is unavoidable.

What crosses it?

Water is the most obvious material because water already belongs to the boundary structure of the cosmology.

The mature model proposes that an upper watery region may be the lower boundary of the forming next world-turn. From the present world it appears above. From the next world it would be below.

During the quiet phase, the interface is stable.

During activation, it can become part of the transfer.

That gives flood a very different role from ordinary weather.

The flood is no longer the whole explanation.

It is one visible consequence of a boundary becoming active.

Atmosphere can cross as well.

Pressure, vapour and heat become part of the event.

Fine material can be carried.

Sediment can move.

Rock can become debris.

The crust itself can deform while water is moving over it.

Living material can survive if it remains protected or if the crossing conditions allow it.

This is why carriers became so important to the model.

## The enclosure principle

An ark, basket, chest, cave, egg, shell or womb all solve one basic problem.

They create an **inside** that stays coherent while the outside becomes dangerous.

That is the enclosure principle.

The carrier does not have to understand the gate.

Its job is to preserve the cargo.

This can happen while the medium does the moving.

A basket floats.

An ark rides the water.

A cave remains still while the world outside changes.

An egg protects life until it can emerge into another environment.

At world scale, the same logic applies.

The stable world itself is an enclosure.

During a reset, smaller enclosures can preserve continuity while the larger one changes.

That is why so many creation and flood stories became interesting to me.

Not because every one of them proves the cosmology, but because the same functional problem appears again and again:

**how does life cross a world transition without ceasing to be life?**

## The land becomes cargo

The land is not the lattice.

That distinction is essential.

The lattice is the deeper organising structure in the model.

The crust is material riding above it.

I sometimes call the crust **rubble on the lattice** because the phrase keeps the hierarchy clear.

Land can bend, shear, break, rotate, rise, sink and be buried.

The deeper structure can remain continuous while the visible map changes.

This is where the large bends and arcs of the world became interesting to me.

The Americas, the Mediterranean and Africa, Arabia, India, Southeast Asia, Indonesia, New Guinea and eastern Australia all show large curved relationships at different scales.

I do not claim that a single drawn line on a map proves the lattice.

The cosmological idea is simpler.

If the deeper driver contains organised shear and changing directions, the crust above it should not be expected to move only in straight lines.

It can bend.

Water then outlines the new relief.

The final coastline becomes the combined result of land deformation and where the redistributed water settles.

## The sky is also part of the machine

The same principle applies upward.

The sky is not empty background in Vardath Cosmology.

It is part of the larger structure.

That does not mean I have solved the exact physical nature of the Sun, Moon, planets and stars inside the model.

I have not.

The stronger claim is architectural.

The celestial field belongs to the same changing geometry as the gate.

During the quiet phase, the field is broad and ordinary.

During activation, nodes can become more prominent.

During convergence, the sky can become increasingly organised around the axial structure.

At maximum contraction, the broad field can be dominated by what I call the **rod-body**.

During reopening, the field spreads again and the ordinary-looking sky returns.

This is one reason myths containing eyes, wheels, stars, rods and radiant figures can sit so close together in the Vardath reading.

They may be different visual responses to an active geometry.

Again, that is a comparison, not proof.

## Mythology comes after the machine

Only after the physical structure is stated do I want to compare mythology.

That order matters.

The myths are not allowed to decide freely what the cosmology should become every time a new symbol appears.

The model first defines roles.

Then I ask where those roles appear elsewhere.

The main roles include:

- **axis** — the central connecting geometry;
- **gate** — the temporary state of changed adjacency;
- **operator** — a figure associated with directing, ordering or activating the event;
- **vehicle** — an active means of crossing;
- **carrier** — an enclosure that preserves cargo;
- **guide** — one who knows the route;
- **guardian** — one who controls or marks the threshold;
- **manifestation** — the visible form of the active structure;
- **medium** — water, wind, cloud, fire, radiance or current surrounding the transition;
- **witness** — the observer who experiences only one view of the event;
- **reset** — the reorganisation that turns catastrophe into a successor world.

Different traditions can preserve different roles.

One may be dominated by the tree.

Another by the flood.

Another by a guarded bridge.

Another by a world-serpent.

Another by a rolled sky.

Another by a child carried through water.

Another by an old body becoming the material of a new cosmos.

They do not need to be the same story to become structurally interesting beside one another.

That is the approach I take throughout the book.

## Reopening

The gate cannot stay open.

If it did, the worlds would not regain separate environments.

The event ends by reversing the contraction.

The throat loses dominance.

The braid widens.

Nodes close.

Water and atmosphere settle.

Sediment is deposited.

The land retains the scars of movement.

Life emerges from protected pockets and surviving lineages.

The next stable state begins.

This is where the word **reset** can be misleading.

I do not mean rewind.

The world does not return to the exact arrangement it had before.

A reset in Vardath Cosmology is a **handoff**.

The new world inherits material and life from what came before while settling into a different arrangement.

The world survives by changing.

## The full cycle

The complete movement can be written simply:

**quiet world → activation → convergence → throat → changed adjacency → transfer → deformation and reset → reopening → renewed quiet world**

That is the central machine of this book.

Everything else grows around it.

The serpent is the moving line.

The lattice is the broad connected form.

The fingertrap explains contraction.

The rod is the concentrated state.

The gate is the temporary contact.

Water, atmosphere and land reveal the consequences.

The carrier preserves continuity.

The world tree holds the long connection.

The myths give us different human views of the event.

And the renewed world becomes quiet enough that the event itself can fade into memory.

Vardath Cosmology is therefore not fundamentally a story about the end of the world.

It is a story about **how one world can become the next without the larger world-body losing continuity**.
