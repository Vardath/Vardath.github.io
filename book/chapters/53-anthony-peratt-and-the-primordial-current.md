# Chapter 53 — Anthony Peratt and the Primordial Current

Before I go farther into mythology, religion, fairy tale and story, there is one physical bridge I want to stop and examine properly.

Anthony Peratt.

I have already introduced him earlier in the book because the Squatter-Man figure and the current beneath the world became important very early in my thinking. But those earlier chapters were doing several jobs at once. Here I want to give the Peratt connection one chapter of its own, because it may be one of the clearest places where the physical and mythic sides of Vardath Cosmology touch.

After this chapter I return to the stories.

This chapter is the exception.

The reason is simple.

If I am going to keep asking whether old myths, gods, world trees, serpents, mountains, wheels, eyes and fires may preserve different views of one recurring natural event, then I need to explain why I think an enormous electrical or plasma-like event is physically worth considering at all.

Peratt gives me that starting point.

He does not give me the whole Vardath Cosmology.

He does not give me my world-disc, my growing sequence of worlds, my time door, my moving lands, my upper ocean, my Meltology interpretation or my idea that one primordial current may have helped create the material enclosure itself.

Those are my extensions.

But his work gives me something I needed long before I understood how much I needed it:

**a large current can have anatomy.**

It can filament.

It can pinch.

It can form lobes.

It can organise around an axis.

It can develop repeated shapes.

And under extreme conditions, something that is fundamentally a current can become something a human witness might remember as a figure in the sky.

## What I take from Peratt

I want to keep three layers separate.

The first is ordinary plasma physics.

Birkeland currents are electric currents flowing along magnetic-field-aligned paths in plasma. Plasma does not have to behave like a smooth featureless glow. Currents can become filamentary. Magnetic fields created by the current can act back on the plasma. In a Z-pinch, an axial current and its magnetic field can drive plasma inward toward the axis, creating a narrow high-energy configuration.

That basic pinch physics is not mythology.

It is plasma behaviour.

The second layer is Peratt's ancient-sky proposal.

Peratt compared high-current plasma morphologies with recurring petroglyph forms and argued that at least some ancient images may have recorded an extreme auroral or plasma phenomenon. The "Squatter Man" is the most famous form associated with this work: a shape that looks anthropomorphic because a pinched central structure and lateral features can resemble a body with arms and legs.

Peratt's proposed ancient event goes much farther than ordinary modern aurora.

That is his ancient-sky hypothesis.

Then there is the third layer.

Mine.

I look at that proposed ancient sky event and ask whether even it may have been only a **later and smaller descendant of something much older**.

That is where Vardath Cosmology begins to move away from Peratt's own claim.

## The figure is not the current's only form

The Squatter Man matters to me because it breaks the habit of thinking that a physical phenomenon must have only one visual identity.

Imagine a large current system viewed from the ground.

At one stage it is a column.

At another it narrows through a pinch.

At another it produces wider structures above and below.

Filaments separate and rejoin.

Bright concentrations form.

Transverse structures appear.

The human eye is very good at turning geometry into bodies.

A bright upper concentration becomes a head.

A narrow central region becomes a waist.

Lateral forms become arms.

Lower structures become legs.

The event becomes a person.

But turn the geometry or change the phase and the same system may no longer look human at all.

It may look like a rod.

A tree.

A mountain.

A torch.

A ladder.

A pair of serpents.

A wheel seen end-on.

A star or rosette.

A many-eyed thing.

That is one of the reasons Peratt became so important to the projection grammar I use later in the book.

The useful lesson is not "the Squatter Man was definitely this one exact plasma form."

The useful lesson is:

**one physical structure can produce several symbolic families depending on phase and viewpoint.**

That is exactly what I need if mythology is going to behave like many cultural camera angles rather than one universal copied story.

## The current before the enclosure

My larger idea begins by moving the current backward in time.

I keep wondering whether the current may be older than the present world arrangement.

Older than the sky as we know it.

Older than the physical enclosure around the inhabited world.

Perhaps the current came first.

Perhaps the world grew around it.

That reverses the ordinary picture.

Instead of starting with a finished world and asking how an electrical disturbance entered it, I start with a tremendous current moving through a less enclosed state and ask whether matter could have gathered, condensed, fused, organised or otherwise settled around the pathways created by that current.

I am deliberately leaving the exact matter-formation mechanism open.

I do not know whether "created matter" will turn out to be the right phrase.

It may be that matter already existed and was concentrated or reorganised.

It may be that plasma cooled into increasingly stable material.

It may be that multiple processes operated at different stages.

I do not need to decide that yet.

The important possibility is the direction of inheritance:

**current first → material organised around current → enclosure grows → later current pulses through inherited enclosure.**

That sequence does a lot of work for me.

It means the current is not merely one event inside the world.

It may be part of the reason the world has the structure it does.

## The fire that may have built the worlds

I call this the primordial fire because "current" can sound too quiet.

What I am imagining at the beginning is not a small modern discharge.

It is enormous.

A vast Birkeland-like current or family of currents passing through the developing world-set.

The first major pulse may have had far more room to expand than any later pulse.

There may have been less settled matter around it.

Less enclosure.

Less shielding.

Less thermal mass.

Fewer inherited barriers.

The current could therefore have occupied an immense volume.

It could filament at huge scales.

It could pinch.

It could heat material.

It could create or organise cavities.

It could establish preferred pathways.

It could leave behind a structural skeleton that later worlds continue to inherit.

This is where my serpent, braid and world-tree ideas begin to converge.

The serpent is the moving line.

The paired serpent is the counter-wound current.

The braid is the interaction of those paths.

The world tree is the enormous architecture that eventually grows around them.

The rod is the contracted state.

The Squatter Man is one visible later morphology.

These do not have to be separate inventions.

They may be different stages of one long history.

## A growing set of worlds

The second part of my extension is the geometry of the worlds themselves.

I do not picture an endless stack of identical floors.

I picture growth.

Previous, present and next are neighbouring stages within a larger structure that keeps expanding.

The natural image that keeps returning is the spiral.

An ammonite.

A coiled shell.

A world tree whose newer growth contains and extends what came before.

A fingertrap-like weave changing width and length while preserving continuity.

I often use Fibonacci-like growth as the visual shorthand because the important thing is not a mystical number.

It is **scaled succession**.

Later turns are related to earlier turns but need not be the same size.

The system can widen.

Older worlds remain part of the body.

A newer world grows beyond the older one.

The current passes through a structure whose dimensions have changed because the structure itself has grown.

That immediately gives me a reason later events may not look like the first event.

The same family of current is now moving through a different machine.

## Matter as memory

This is one of the ideas I find most useful.

Matter may be memory.

Not memory in the psychological sense.

Physical memory.

The material surrounding a later pulse records everything that previous pulses already did.

Every layer that formed changes the conditions of the next event.

Every boundary adds load.

Every body of water changes conductivity and heat transfer.

Every mass of rock adds inertia.

Every lattice segment changes where current can concentrate.

Every previous reset leaves pathways, weaknesses, barriers and altered material.

The world becomes the accumulated history of the fire.

That means a later pulse is never starting from the same initial condition as the first.

The first great current may have helped organise the enclosure.

The next one has to move through that enclosure.

The next has still more inherited matter around it.

The current may remain related while its visible expression becomes progressively constrained.

This gives me a possible reason for a hierarchy of events.

The primordial event can be world-forming.

A later event can be world-resetting.

A later local event can be regional.

A smaller node event can be local.

Same family.

Different scale.

Different amount of inherited world around it.

## Why the later pulse may be smaller

This is where the idea becomes physically intuitive even before I know the full physics.

Put more material around a current pathway and the system changes.

Energy can be absorbed.

Heat can be distributed.

Current can divide among more paths.

Matter can provide inertia.

Boundaries can constrain expansion.

Conductive and non-conductive regions can redirect where energy goes.

Water and atmosphere can change how a discharge couples to the environment.

The pulse is no longer moving through an empty or simple domain.

It is moving through the accumulated architecture of older worlds.

So I keep coming back to this possibility:

**the worlds themselves damp their creator.**

Not necessarily by switching the current off.

By forcing it to express itself inside a more complicated enclosure.

That is how I can imagine an original current enormous enough to help form worlds and a later current still enormous enough to terrify human observers without requiring both events to be equal.

The later event is a reduced descendant.

Still the fire.

But a fire with walls around it.

## Squatter Man as a reduced descendant of creation

This is where Squatter Man changes meaning for me.

Instead of being the great original event, the ancient sky figure may be a diminished descendant of the original world-forming current.

That possibility makes the anthropomorphic form much more interesting.

Human beings may have witnessed a later pulse moving through pathways established long before humanity existed.

The current rises.

It pinches.

Bright regions form.

Filaments become visible.

The sky acquires an axial body.

The observer sees a giant figure.

That image is then carved, drawn, remembered and eventually absorbed into stories of gods, giants, beings in the sky, celestial trees, ladders, pillars or mountains.

If that happened, then the petroglyph is not merely a record of a strange aurora.

It may be a record of the world-machine briefly revealing one of its old pathways.

That is my extension.

I am not putting it into Peratt's mouth.

## Meru and the same axial event

Mount Meru became important to me for the same reason.

I no longer need to choose between "giant figure in the sky" and "cosmic mountain."

Those can be scale and viewpoint problems.

A strongly concentrated axial current can look like a figure when transverse structures dominate the view.

The same axial event can look like a central mountain when the whole vertical scale dominates.

The same structure can look like a rod when the side detail disappears.

It can look like an eye or wheel when seen toward the axis.

This gives me a family:

**current → pinch → rod → figure → mountain → tree**

Not as synonyms.

As possible appearances or cultural descriptions of one changing axial system.

Meru then becomes especially interesting because it is not merely a mountain in ordinary geography.

It is cosmological organisation.

A centre.

An axis.

A relation among levels.

That is exactly where a later reduced world-pulse would appear in my model.

## The fires and Meltology

The current also has to touch matter somewhere.

That is where the sky story meets Meltology.

If an intense current becomes visible in the sky, concentrates through the lattice, opens nodes and interacts with the surface, then I would expect the material world to remember that interaction.

Heat.

Electrical alteration.

Vitrification.

Glazing.

Foaming.

Fusion.

Recrystallisation.

Sudden thermal stress.

Softened or distorted surfaces.

Material that looks as though it was exposed to conditions very different from the quiet world.

This does not mean every melted-looking wall or unusual mountain was made by the same event.

I do not need that claim.

What interests me is the continuity of mechanism.

The sky-form and the surface scar may belong to different regions of the same active state.

Peratt's current gives me the upper morphology.

Meltology gives me a possible material consequence where current and matter meet.

One is the fire seen.

The other is the fire remembered in stone.

## Water and fire are not opposites

This also helps me with a problem that appears again and again in old stories.

Flood and fire often sit uncomfortably close together.

If the catastrophe is water, why is there fire?

If the catastrophe is fire, why is there flood?

In my cosmology they do not have to compete.

They can be different effects of the same world-state.

The current energises the structure.

The lattice contracts.

The boundary changes.

Upper waters become mobile.

Atmosphere becomes loaded.

At the same time, current concentration can produce intense heating and luminous plasma-like effects.

So the world can be wet and burning.

The sky can be bright while water is moving.

The current can heat one region while floodwater overwhelms another.

A single event family can produce both.

That is much more useful to me than trying to force every catastrophe story into one element.

## A current inside a woven machine

Peratt's plasma forms also help me picture why the lattice matters.

A current does not move in abstraction.

It follows fields and conductive pathways.

In Vardath Cosmology, the larger world structure is woven.

I separate the two ideas:

**lattice = structure**

**current = movement through structure**

The distinction is important.

The lattice can remain when the current is quiet.

The world can sleep without losing its architecture.

Then the current strengthens.

Inherited pathways become active.

Nodes brighten.

The weave tightens.

The same old roads inside the world become visible again.

That gives recurrence a physical memory.

The event does not have to rediscover its route every time.

The world already contains the path.

## Pinch and fingertrap

This is also where plasma pinch and fingertrap geometry sit beside one another without needing to be the same phenomenon.

The fingertrap explains how a woven structure can become narrower and more axial.

The Z-pinch gives me a real plasma example in which current and magnetic field can drive plasma inward toward an axis.

I do not simply equate the two.

I place them beside one another.

One is my geometric image for the lattice.

The other is a physical current behaviour that shows how strong axial concentration can happen in plasma.

The combination is powerful.

A woven world-structure narrows.

The current inside it also concentrates.

Geometry and energy reinforce the same axial state.

That state becomes the rod.

The throat.

The gate.

The visible giant.

The mountain.

The tree.

Different descriptions begin clustering around the same moment.

## Why the event can recur

If the current is persistent rather than created anew each time, recurrence becomes much easier to imagine.

The world does not need a fresh cosmic machine for every reset.

The same structure can cycle between quiet and active states.

Quiet current.

Pulse.

Convergence.

Pinch.

Transfer.

Release.

Quiet current again.

The later world inherits both the pathway and the scars.

That means each cycle can be related to earlier cycles without being identical.

The current is old.

The world around it is new.

This is exactly the relationship I need for repeated myths.

Similar enough to recognise.

Different enough to produce different stories.

## The oldest event may be the largest

The growth model gives me another possibility.

Perhaps the farther back I go, the less enclosed the system becomes.

If so, then the earliest major current may also have been the least constrained.

That would make the oldest event the largest.

The first fire could be enormous.

Later pulses become smaller as worlds accumulate around the pathway.

This reverses a common intuition that ancient events should be weaker, simpler or more primitive.

In this model, the earliest event can be the most physically extreme because the structure had not yet accumulated all of the matter that later limits it.

That gives creation a very different character.

The world is not born quietly and later subjected to catastrophe.

Creation itself may be the greatest catastrophe.

Later catastrophes are smaller repetitions of creation.

That idea may explain why creation stories and apocalypse stories so often borrow each other's imagery.

Waters.

Fire.

Serpent.

Mountain.

Tree.

Division.

Darkness.

Light.

Opening.

New land.

The end of one world and the beginning of another can resemble each other because they may belong to the same family of process at different scales.

## The current and time

This is the part I am not ready to pretend I understand.

The time door has become central to Vardath Cosmology.

Previous, present and next may be neighbouring world-states that become temporarily adjacent.

People may cross.

Lands may move.

A region may appear in another age.

Stories may preserve changed return times, mirror selves, repeated people, fairy time or places that exist beside ordinary history without being continuously accessible.

The current is clearly involved in the active gate-state in my model.

But I do not yet know whether the current causes the time relationship or merely activates a geometry that already contains it.

Maybe the growing world-tree is inherently temporal.

Maybe each turn is both a place and a time.

Maybe the current contracts the weave until those times become adjacent.

Maybe the gate is spatial first and temporal only because the neighbouring world-turns correspond to different ages.

Maybe there is another layer I have not yet understood.

I leave that open.

The important thing is not to use "plasma" as a magic word that supposedly solves time travel.

It does not.

Peratt gives me a physical bridge into the current and the sky morphology.

My time-door idea goes farther.

The connection is one of the things I am still exploring.

## What Peratt changes for the rest of the book

Peratt changes the way I read the stories that follow.

Not because every god becomes plasma.

That would be useless.

Not because every myth becomes a disguised aurora.

That would flatten the very differences I am trying to understand.

What changes is that I now have a physical reason to take changing morphology seriously.

A current can be one thing and look like many things.

An axial event can produce a body-like form.

A pinch can create a narrow waist.

Filaments can produce branches.

A radial structure can look like an eye or wheel.

A large luminous axis can become a mountain, tree or pillar in human memory.

Once that possibility exists, the old stories become more interesting rather than less.

I can ask about role.

Viewpoint.

Sequence.

Scale.

What is the gate?

What is the operator?

What is the vehicle?

What is the carrier?

What is the medium?

What is the witness seeing?

What changes before and after the event?

I do not need every answer to be "plasma."

I only need the physical world to be capable of producing strange enough forms that mythic description remains worth investigating.

Peratt gives me that permission.

## The bridge, not the destination

That is the place I want to leave this chapter.

Anthony Peratt is a bridge in Vardath Cosmology.

His plasma work gives me real current, pinch and filament behaviour.

His ancient-sky interpretation gives me the possibility that people once recorded an extreme electrical or auroral form in repeated images.

My cosmology then extends the idea into a much larger architecture:

a primordial current;

a growing sequence of worlds;

matter accumulating around the current;

later pulses damped by inherited enclosure;

Squatter Man as a reduced descendant of the original fire;

Meru as another scale or view of the axial event;

Meltology as one possible material memory;

and a gate whose relationship to time remains open.

That is as far as I want the plasma chapter to go.

From here I return to mythology, religion, legend, fairy tale and story.

I am not going to turn every chapter that follows into plasma cosmology.

I want to do the opposite.

I want to let each tradition speak in its own language first.

Then I want to ask whether, beneath that language, the same strange architecture keeps appearing.

The current is one possible physical bridge.

The stories are the rest of the journey.
