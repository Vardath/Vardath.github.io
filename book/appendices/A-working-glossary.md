# Appendix A — Working Glossary

This glossary is not a claim that every term below names an established physical structure.

It is a plain-language guide to how I use the main terms inside **Vardath Cosmology**.

Where a word also has an ordinary scientific, religious or mythological meaning, the definition here describes **my use inside this book** unless I say otherwise.

The model remains speculative. The glossary is meant to keep the internal language consistent.

## Active world

The phase in which the normally quiet larger world-structure is strongly changing state.

In the active world, current, atmosphere, water, lattice geometry, local nodes or boundaries may behave very differently from their ordinary quiet condition.

The active world is not a separate universe. It is another phase of the same larger structure.

## Activation

The beginning of departure from the quiet state.

Activation is not yet maximum convergence or full gate formation. It is the stage in which currents, nodes, preferred pathways or environmental conditions begin shifting toward the active regime.

## Adjacency

What is effectively next to what.

I use this word more broadly than ordinary physical distance.

Two regions can be far apart in everyday geometry yet become effectively adjacent if the structure connecting them changes state.

The gate is fundamentally a change in adjacency.

## Apparatus

The structure through which a transition occurs.

The apparatus may include lattice, boundary, throat, axis, node or route.

In mythic comparison, I distinguish the apparatus from the operator, traveller, guardian and vehicle.

## Axis

A dominant through-direction that appears when the broader structure becomes concentrated.

The axis can be represented comparatively by a rod, pillar, tree, mountain, ladder, spear or similar vertical form.

I do not treat the axis as one permanent rigid pole that must always dominate the world. In the mature model, it is primarily a **state of the wider network**.

## Boundary

The condition that keeps one region or world-state distinct from another.

A boundary is not necessarily a solid wall.

It can be an interface, layer, membrane-like relation, water boundary, atmospheric transition, lattice state or other form of maintained separation.

Stable worlds require working boundaries.

## Braid

The paired or multiple winding pathways inside the larger lattice.

The braid developed from the earlier paired-serpent image.

It allows opposed or differently directed strands to remain part of one connected structure.

## Broad lattice

The distributed form of the world-structure during the quiet state.

Instead of one dominant axial route, pathways and nodes remain spread across a wider field.

The broad lattice is the state from which convergence can occur and to which reopening returns.

## Camera angle

My shorthand for the way one underlying form or event can look very different depending on viewpoint, scale, phase and medium.

A broadside view may suggest net, weave or braid.

A side view may suggest rod, tree, pillar, ladder or mountain.

An end-on view may suggest eye, wheel, ring, star or rosette.

This is a comparative tool, not a claim that every such symbol historically came from one object.

## Carrier

An enclosure or structure that preserves cargo through dangerous transition.

An ark, cave, shell, basket, house, island or other protected inside can occupy the carrier role.

A carrier is not necessarily a vehicle. It can remain relatively passive while protecting what is inside.

## Cargo

Anything transferred, preserved or redistributed during transition.

Possible cargo in the model includes water, gases, sediment, stone, biological material, people, structures, information and cultural memory.

Different cargo may respond differently to the same gate.

## Changed adjacency

The core gate condition.

Normally separated regions become capable of direct interaction or transfer without needing to be understood as simply crashing together metre by metre.

This remains a hypothesis inside the physical model.

## Connector

A route or relation joining regions without necessarily carrying a traveller bodily.

Examples in the comparative atlas include chain, fishing line, bridge, ladder or other narrow relation.

A connector can transmit force, access or information even when it does not function as a full transport route.

## Convergence

The phase in which the broad world-structure gathers toward one or more narrow through-routes.

Convergence increases the importance of axial geometry and can lead toward throat or gate formation.

## Current

The persistent deeper flow proposed by the model.

The exact physical nature of this current remains unresolved.

I use Birkeland-current and plasma morphology as important comparison points, especially through Anthony Peratt's published work, but the full world-forming current is my own speculative extension.

## Deep current

Another name for the persistent current beneath or through the larger world-system.

It is distinguished from a temporary pulse.

The current may persist quietly while the world remains stable.

## Distributed support

The idea that the world need not depend on one permanent central pillar.

A network of many relationships, strands or supports can maintain the quiet geometry.

A dominant axis can then emerge only during particular phases.

## Dome

The proposed woven or structured upper boundary of an inhabited world-disc.

The dome is not meant as a simple rigid glass ceiling.

In the mature model it is more like a broad lattice or vault that can change state and may participate in convergence.

## Enclosure

A protected inside separated from a changing outside.

Egg, cave, ark, basket, shell, womb, chamber and similar forms belong to this family.

Enclosure can preserve life, development, material or identity through transition.

## Event

The full active transition rather than one isolated catastrophe.

The event can include build-up, activation, convergence, gate formation, transfer, atmospheric and surface change, reopening, settling and renewed stability.

## Event gate

A temporary route that exists because a specific active condition forms.

A cyclone in *The Wizard of Oz* is a fictional example of this structural category.

The gate is not permanently present as an open passage before or after the event.

## Field

The wider distributed region in which nodes, paths and relationships exist.

I sometimes use field language when the broad world-structure is more useful to imagine as a continuous environment than as a visible set of lines.

## Fingertrap

The main mechanical analogy for how one woven structure can change from broad to narrow without becoming a different object.

A fingertrap changes radius, length and strand angle together.

That gave me the bridge from broad lattice to ladder, rod and throat.

## Gate

A temporary condition in which normally separated regions become unusually connected or traversable.

The gate is not necessarily a permanent physical doorway.

It is a phase or local state of changed adjacency.

## Gate capacity

The amount and type of transfer a gate can support.

A gate may be able to pass gas but not large rock, one traveller but not a city, or information before bulk matter.

This is a physical question the model still needs to solve.

## Gate duration

How long the changed-adjacency state remains active.

Duration matters because the amount of water, matter, heat or life that can cross depends on how long a usable route exists.

## Gate window

The period in which a local or larger route is available.

Fairy-time stories often represent this through special dates, sunset, seasonal moments or other phase conditions.

In the model, the general principle is **place + phase**.

## Guardian

A figure associated with watching, controlling or testing access to a route.

Heimdallr at Bifröst is a useful mythic example of the role.

Guardian and gate are not the same thing.

## Guide

A figure who knows or mediates the route.

Gabriel in the Miʿrāj comparison, Niamh in the Tír na nÓg material and various fairy guides occupy this kind of role.

Guide and vehicle are not automatically the same.

## Handoff

The transfer of continuity from an old world-state into the one that follows.

The handoff can include material, water, atmosphere, life, structures, survivors, knowledge and cultural memory.

A reset becomes succession only if something is handed forward.

## Inheritance

Anything the successor world receives from what came before.

Inheritance does not have to remain intact.

It can arrive as fragments, sediment, ruins, biological remnants, altered land, myth or memory.

## Lattice

My main word for the proposed woven relational structure connecting the larger world-body.

The lattice can be broad during quiet conditions and more concentrated during convergence.

It contains paths, crossings and nodes rather than functioning as one rigid cage.

## Local gate

A small-scale threshold event affecting one place, traveller or limited region rather than the whole world.

Fairy mounds, wells, tunnels and similar stories are useful comparative images for this category.

## Local node

A particular place in the larger network where paths meet, concentrate or become capable of unusual coupling.

A local node is not necessarily active as a gate all the time.

## Many-throat model

The mature view that the world can contain many possible local or regional gate sites rather than one permanent physical portal at one pole.

A larger axial state may still occur under stronger convergence.

## Manifestation

The visible or experienced appearance of the active structure.

A manifestation may be remembered as serpent, eye, wheel, rod, wings, giant figure, radiant body or other form depending on viewpoint.

The manifestation is not automatically identical with the deeper mechanism.

## Medium

The material or environmental domain through which another process occurs.

Water, atmosphere, sea, cloud or plasma-like conditions can function as media.

A medium can separate regions and also connect them.

## Meltology

My working branch concerning melted-looking, vitrified, glazed, fused, foamed, recrystallised, distorted, enlarged or mountain-like built and geological forms.

Inside Vardath Cosmology, Meltology is explored as possible **material memory of intense reset conditions**, including electrical, plasma-like and thermal effects.

I do not assume every claimed Meltology site has the same origin.

## Memorial threshold

A place, object or architectural feature that remembers a transition without functioning as an active gate in ordinary conditions.

The sipapu is a strong comparative example.

The event ends; the cultural address remains.

## Moving lands

The possibility that a sufficiently strong transition can shift not only individuals but coherent pieces of surface material, settlements, regions or larger land masses.

This is one of the more speculative parts of the model and remains physically unresolved.

## Natural time door

My current hypothesis that a recurring natural gate-state may sometimes connect world-states that are also different **times**.

The idea grew from the previous-present-next model and from fairy-time, Otherworld and return stories.

The exact mechanism remains open.

## Negative space

Structure defined by what is absent rather than by bright or solid objects.

The Emu in the Sky helped sharpen this idea for me.

In a network, gaps and closed regions can be as informative as nodes and strands.

## Neighbouring world

A world-turn structurally related to the present one.

A neighbouring world is not necessarily nearby in ordinary surface distance and does not have to be a duplicate of the present world.

All world-turns in Vardath Cosmology are populated in their own right. **Previous, present and next describe relationship and adjacency, not whether a world has inhabitants.**

## Next world

The populated world immediately above a chosen present world; it is future-relative to the world below while remaining present to its own inhabitants.

It remains populated in its own right before, during and after any change in its relation to the present.

## Node

A crossing, concentration point or local centre inside the lattice.

Nodes can remain quiet, become active, form local throats or participate in larger convergence.

## Operator

The agent or process that changes the state of the apparatus.

In comparative mythology, Tāne separating sky and earth is a clear operator example.

An operator is not necessarily the axis, gate, guardian or vehicle.

## Otherworld

A comparative category for a region represented in myth, legend or story as outside ordinary human access.

I do not assume every Otherworld corresponds to a physical Vardath world-turn.

The category is useful because it preserves the structural problem of unusual access, different rules and difficult return.

## Phase

A distinct operating condition of the larger system.

Quiet, activating, converging, gate, reopening and recovery are examples of phases.

The same structure can perform different roles in different phases.

## Place + phase

The idea that a gate address may require both a location and the right system state.

A node can exist permanently while the gate window is temporary.

This is one of the clearest lessons from fairy-threshold stories.

## Plenum

My working idea of the larger world as filled rather than separated by meaningless empty void.

Current, water, matter, medium and structure all participate in one larger body.

The plenum remains a developing concept rather than a completed physical theory.

## Previous world

The populated world immediately below a chosen present world; it is past-relative to the world above while remaining present to its own inhabitants.

Its route or relation to the present can become lower, hidden, sealed, ancestral or mythologised, while the previous world itself remains populated and ordinary to its own inhabitants.

## Primordial current

The speculative first or deepest world-forming Birkeland-like current in the model.

My current idea is that early pulses may have been vastly stronger and may have organised or created much of the material that now encloses the world-system.

This goes beyond Anthony Peratt's published claims and is explicitly my own extension.

## Projection grammar

The comparative method of asking how one geometry changes appearance under different viewing angles.

Broadside, side-on and end-on views can produce very different cultural images without requiring one-to-one symbol identity.

## Pulse

A stronger active episode of the deeper current.

The current may persist.

The pulse is the condition in which the larger world-machine is driven strongly away from quiet equilibrium.

## Quiet world

The ordinary stable phase.

Quiet does not mean motionless.

Water, atmosphere, crust, life and deeper currents can all remain active.

The world is quiet because neighbouring world-states are not being forced into strong abnormal contact.

## Receiver

A figure, structure or region that accepts a traveller or cargo arriving through a threshold.

The animals receiving Sky Woman are a clear comparative example.

## Reconnection

The restoration of a needed coupling after it has been blocked or interrupted.

The hidden Sun and Moon material provides a useful comparative image.

Reconnection is different from global gate opening.

## Recovery

The post-transition period in which the world settles.

Cooling, drainage, pressure equalisation, sedimentation, biological recovery, atmospheric stabilisation and renewed boundary function can all belong to recovery.

Recovery may last far longer than the critical transition.

## Reopening

The phase in which the concentrated axial or throat state relaxes and broader world geometry returns.

Reopening restores local directions and separation between neighbouring regions.

It is not the same as "opening the gate." In this book, reopening usually means **the world opening back out after convergence**.

## Reset

The transition from one stable world-order into another.

Reset can involve catastrophe, but it does not mean the world returns to its exact previous configuration.

Reset is **forward reorganisation**, not rewind.

## Residual state

A consequence of the active event that remains after the external gate or transition has ended.

Altered material, biological change, memory, cultural knowledge, sediment or Meltology can all function as residual states.

## Rod

The narrow concentrated axial appearance of the lattice during strong convergence.

Rod does not mean one permanent solid cosmic pole.

It is primarily a geometric state.

## Route

The path actually used during crossing.

The route can be distinct from the larger apparatus and from the gate condition that makes it available.

## Shell

A broad analogy for the larger growing world-body.

A shell keeps earlier turns while adding new ones, which makes it useful for thinking about previous, present and next worlds as parts of one continuing structure.

I do not mean that the cosmos must literally be a biological shell.

## Stabilisation

The process by which the active transition gives way to a workable world again.

Stabilisation includes more than simple gate closure. The environment must settle into ranges compatible with ordinary life.

## Stabilised turn

A world-region after reopening and recovery have produced a new quiet state.

The present world is treated as one such stabilised turn in the model.

## Substrate

The deeper supporting or organising level beneath visible surface material.

In the model, the proposed lattice or deeper structure is not identical to continents, buildings or sediment.

The visible surface can behave as cargo riding or responding to the deeper substrate.

## Surface cargo

Land, sediment, water, structures and living material treated as material resting on or responding to deeper support.

This is not meant to deny ordinary local geology. It is the larger speculative relationship proposed by the model.

## Throat

The narrow high-connectivity state produced by convergence.

The throat is where transfer capacity becomes concentrated.

A throat can be local, regional or part of a larger axial state.

## Time door

Short form of **natural time door**.

A gate whose changed adjacency may involve different world-times rather than only different locations.

## Time-state

The temporal position or regime associated with a world-turn or region.

I use this idea when discussing stories in which returning to the same geographical place does not mean returning to the same historical world.

## Transfer

Movement or exchange across a temporarily changed boundary.

Transfer can include matter, heat, atmosphere, water, life, information or cultural pattern.

## Transition

The larger process connecting one stable condition to another.

Transition includes but is not limited to gate crossing.

## Traveller

The being or person who crosses.

The traveller is not automatically the operator, carrier, guide or vehicle.

## Two-way transfer

Exchange in more than one direction through an active relation.

The Haudenosaunee Sky Woman / earth-diver sequence is a useful comparative image: life moves downward while material is recovered upward.

In the physical model, different cargo can move differently according to gradients and gate conditions.

## Upper waters

The proposed water-bearing region or boundary above the present world in the Vardath model.

Its exact physical nature, support and pressure conditions remain unresolved.

The idea is compared with traditions of waters above, heavenly oceans and water-bearing world boundaries without claiming those traditions teach the same mechanism.

## Vehicle

A moving interface that carries a traveller through an exceptional route.

Sleipnir, al-Burāq and other extraordinary mounts occupy this role in the comparative atlas.

A vehicle is distinct from the gate itself.

## Vapour canopy

A major environmental component of Vardath Cosmology: the water-rich upper atmospheric boundary associated with the upper waters and lattice enclosure. In its strong form it creates a high-pressure, high-oxygen, moisture-rich atmosphere beneath it and participates in the more active electrical/plasma regime of the waking world.

The canopy is central to the model's giant-life, longevity, telomere-regrowth, mutation and persistent-tissue branches.

## Waking atmosphere

The atmospheric regime of the active or canopy world. It is characterised in the model by higher pressure, higher oxygen availability, heavy moisture, strong electrical/plasma activity, heat and rapid movement of water and gases.

This atmosphere is one of the main mechanisms connecting the world-machine to giant biological scale, extreme longevity, mutation and unusual tissue persistence.

## Waking world

Another name for the active-world phase.

The world has not become a different universe. Its deeper architecture and environment have entered another operating state.

## Water as tracer

The principle that large water movement may reveal deeper changes rather than being the deepest cause.

Water follows pressure, slope, channels and boundaries.

Flood can therefore be evidence of structural change without being the mechanism that initiated it.

## World-body

The larger continuing structure of which individual world-turns are parts.

The shell, tree and living-body analogies all point toward this idea.

## World-disc

The proposed local inhabited world geometry in which the ordinary lived surface can be broad and disc-like, with real material depth beneath it.

This local world-disc can coexist, in the model, with a larger shell or world-tree architecture at another scale.

## World-state

A particular stable or active configuration of the world-system.

The same larger structure can occupy different world-states without becoming unrelated universes.

## World succession

The recurring pattern in which populated worlds remain in a continuing vertical chain while gate events temporarily increase exchange among a present, its past-relative world below and its future-relative world above.

No world becomes the one universal present. Each world remains its own present. Succession describes repeated destructive-and-renewal cycles, transfer and inheritance across the chain, followed by new-beginning periods on the affected worlds.

## World-tree

The tree image used for the larger connected architecture of levels or world-turns.

Yggdrasil is the most important comparative example in the book.

In my mature model, the world-tree is not only spatial. It may also represent a woven relationship among past, present and future world-states.

## World-turn

One stable region or stage within the larger growing shell/tree structure.

A turn is not necessarily a visible spiral drawn on the local ground.

It is a way of describing successive parts of one larger continuing world-body.

## Woven time structure

My mature interpretation of the world-tree as a network connecting not only different regions but potentially different world-times.

This is the structural basis of the natural time-door hypothesis.

## Final shorthand

The full Vardath cycle can be compressed to:

**quiet world → activation → convergence → throat / gate → changed adjacency → transfer → deformation / heating / redistribution → reopening → recovery → renewed quiet world**

And the time-door extension adds:

**previous world-time ↔ temporary gate relation ↔ present / next world-time**

Those two sequences are the core of the language used throughout this book.
