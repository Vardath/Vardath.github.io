# Vardath Cosmology — Book Manuscript

**Working title:** *Vardath Cosmology: The World That Opens*  
**Author:** Stephen Michael Hawton / Vardath  
**Status:** In development

This directory is the canonical working manuscript for the long-form Vardath Cosmology book.

## What this book is

The book is a continuous first-person explanation of my cosmology. It is not a copy-and-paste compilation of the website or WordPress series.

The website and WordPress archive are the research reservoir. The book reorganises that material into one narrative: how the world-picture formed, how the world-machine works, how the gate opens, what crosses, how one world becomes the next, and why mythology can be read as different camera angles on related structural roles.

## Scope

Included:
- the development of the cosmology where it helps the reader understand the present model;
- current world structure;
- current, lattice, braid, shell/world-turns and gate mechanics;
- upper-water, atmosphere, crust, sediment and living-cargo branches;
- mythology and comparative role grammar;
- land, sky and waking-environment branches;
- Yggdrasil, time and world-switching;
- unresolved parts of the physical picture.

Deliberately excluded:
- JANUS and number research;
- numerology;
- phonetics;
- research tests, corpus runs and test logs;
- assistant commentary;
- long methodological audit sections.

## Manuscript rules

1. **Write in Vardath's voice.** First person is the default when explaining the cosmology or how I came to it.
2. **Do not paste the article archive into chapters.** Rebuild the material as book prose.
3. **The current cosmology is the destination.** Earlier ideas appear only where they help explain how the current picture formed.
4. **Mythologies keep their own identities.** I compare roles, relations and sequences; I do not claim every god or symbol is secretly one thing.
5. **Speculation stays visible as speculation.** The book can be confident about what the model proposes without pretending unresolved physics has already been solved.
6. **No numbers-research detours.** Ordinary descriptive quantities can appear when needed, but number-pattern research is outside this book.
7. **No test-report prose.** A result may have changed my thinking, but the book tells the resulting story rather than reproducing the experiment.
8. **Save substantial work chapter by chapter.** The repository remains the canonical manuscript so progress survives chat or connection failures.
