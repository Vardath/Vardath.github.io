## Working book architecture

This outline is deliberately book-sized rather than a repackaging of the 453-post article bank. The articles supply the research reservoir; the book reorganises them into one continuous argument.

# Part I — Finding the Hidden Shape

### Chapter 1 — The Serpent and the Question
The Rainbow Serpent as the earliest recoverable intuition: a moving current that shapes land and water. Why the project began with motion rather than a static world-picture.

Squatter Man, Z-pinch morphology, anthropomorphic projection and the discovery that viewpoint can turn one structure into person, eye, wheel, cross or tree.

### Chapter 3 — The First World-Machine
The December 2025 enclosure: inhabited floor, upper fourfold geometry, central sea, spike, hanging sphere, and the transition into the February 2026 caduceus room.

### Chapter 4 — One Phenomenon, Many Gods
Operator versus apparatus; vehicle, manifestation, medium, witness and gate. Why role separation stopped the mythology from collapsing into “everything is the same god.”

### Chapter 5 — Numbers at the Door
7, 8, 11, Aetio/Atel/Ase and the symbolic threshold period, presented as research history rather than universal etymology.

### Chapter 6 — JANUS: Where Symbol Became Mathematics
The exact audited core: 1→3→7, 11, Q² = −8Q, 3–4–5, 33, 137, and the hard boundary between theorem and cosmology.

### Chapter 7 — Exploration of 8 and the Discipline of Gates
Finite inventory, order, coordinates, product spaces, symmetry, gates and allowed paths. How the method changed the whole project.

### Chapter 8 — The Models That Had to Fail
Literal polar geography, spiral longitude, Moon projector, Sun/Moon serpent ends, outward-pressure gravity, simple three-beat clock and exact φ chronology. Why failure improved the cosmology.

### Chapter 13 — Thirty-Two Around, One Through

### Chapter 14 — Many Throats, Not One Pole
Distributed convergence nodes, local and regional gates, closed/open node states, eyes, wheels, stars and the difference between local threshold and global reset.

### Chapter 15 — The Gate Is a Change in Adjacency
The mature definition of gate. Effective connectivity, finite capacity, duration, return windows and why extraordinary travel is topological in the model rather than merely very fast.
