# Ezekiel 21 — one incoming route reaches a two-road fork and a decision operator selects one branch

The chapter turns the sword into a directional operator.

The key spatial instruction is unusually explicit:

- Ezekiel is told to mark out **two roads** for the sword/king of Babylon;
- both routes begin from the same source-country;
- a signpost is made at the head/parting of the road;
- one branch points toward Rabbah of the Ammonites;
- the other points toward fortified Jerusalem;
- the Babylonian king stands at the fork and performs divination;
- the Jerusalem branch is selected.

The divination set includes:
- shaking/consulting arrows;
- consulting teraphim/household gods;
- inspecting the liver.

The secure geometry is therefore:

**one incoming route**
→ **junction**
→ **two available branches**
→ **selection procedure**
→ **one branch activated**
→ **siege machinery advances toward the selected destination**.

This is one of the cleanest explicit **branch-selection** sequences in the corpus so far.

It is not a fingertrap by itself.

It is better treated as a model of:
**route choice at a bifurcation after convergence to a junction**.

For the Vardath map this matters because a gate event need not merely open a throat; it may also require a **routing state** that decides which neighbouring world/region becomes effectively adjacent.

## Ezekiel 21 — the fork is a control point, not merely a crossroads

The king does not randomly wander from one road to another.

He halts at the branching point and queries multiple information channels before motion resumes.

That gives:

**motion**
→ **pause at junction**
→ **information acquisition**
→ **branch state selected**
→ **motion resumes**.

This resembles a switching node more than a passive crossing.

The Vardath relevance is not that ancient divination was a physical routing technology.

The relevance is the narrative process:
**junction → query → selection → directed continuation**.

## Ezekiel 21 — high/low inversion is stated directly

The prince's turban/crown is removed and the oracle announces a reversal in which:

It belongs beside earlier biblical reversal structures, but it should not be forced into literal vertical world-transfer.

Its value is that the chapter contains both:
1. **two-branch route selection**;

Those are separate operations in the text.

# Ezekiel 22 — distributed population is gathered into one bounded furnace-state

The strongest mechanical image is the smelter/furnace sequence.

The people are compared with mixed metal/dross:
- silver;
- bronze/copper;
- tin;
- iron;
- lead.

The explicit process is:

**distributed material**
→ **gathered into Jerusalem**
→ **placed inside one smelter/furnace**
→ **air/blast applied**
→ **temperature rises**
→ **materials melt within the enclosure**.

This is important because it cleanly distinguishes:
**gathering into a central enclosure**
from
**fingertrap contraction**.

Both begin with distributed material moving toward a bounded centre.

But here connectivity is not preserved.
The contents are thermally transformed.

So Ezekiel 22 gives a strong **negative control**:

> **broad→centralized does not equal broad→fingertrap.**

The state variable that distinguishes them is:
**continuity/integrity of the participating structure**.

Mechanically abstracted:

# Ezekiel 23 — Oholah and Oholibah are an explicit paired system from one mother

The chapter deliberately begins with:

**two women**
→ **daughters of one mother**.

Then it identifies them:
- Oholah = Samaria;
- Oholibah = Jerusalem.

This is one of the strongest explicit paired-figure structures in Ezekiel.

The crucial point for the project is not their names.

It is the process relation:

**common origin**
→ **two differentiated branches**
→ **branch A follows one trajectory**
→ **branch B observes A**
→ **branch B repeats/exceeds comparable behaviour**
→ **both enter destructive consequences**.

This is a true **paired historical trajectory**.

It is not evidence that Samaria and Jerusalem are literally two strands of one cosmic braid.

But structurally it is exactly the kind of pair the project is tracking:
**one origin → two linked expressions → interactions/parallel outcomes**.

---

## Ezekiel 23 — images on a wall trigger remote attachment

For the Vardath information/material distinction:

**image/information transfer can precede physical movement.**

## Ezekiel 23 — pair control: parallel does not mean identical

Oholah and Oholibah share:

This is an important role-discipline control.

# Ezekiel 24 — the pot sequence is a full containment/process/discharge experiment

The city becomes a metal cooking vessel.

Initial sequence:

**pot set in place**
→ **water added**
→ **selected pieces added**
→ **bones/meat contained**
→ **fuel piled beneath**
→ **contents boiled**.

Then the image changes:

**contents removed piece-by-piece**
→ **pot becomes empty**
→ **empty copper pot remains on coals**
→ **metal heats/glows**
→ **internal deposit/filth is supposed to melt/burn away**.

This is a remarkably explicit distinction between:

1. **container**;
2. **contained cargo**;
3. **processing medium**;
4. **heat source**;
5. **residue attached to the container**.

That separation is extremely useful for the Vardath transport model.

It prevents:
**carrier**
from being confused with
**cargo**
or
**medium**.

---

## Ezekiel 24 — cargo can leave while the carrier remains under processing

The pot is first full.

Then:
**piece after piece is removed**.

After the cargo is gone:
**the vessel itself remains** and is heated further.

So:

**cargo-state transformation**
and
**carrier-state transformation**
are temporally separable.

That is an excellent addition to the carrier/cargo model.

In abstract form:

**loaded enclosure**
→ **cargo processing**
→ **cargo discharge**
→ **empty carrier persists**
→ **carrier itself undergoes further state change**.

This is not the fingertrap mechanism.

But it is a very strong **transport-system decomposition**.

## Ezekiel 24 — destructive cleansing can fail even under extreme energy

The final heating is intended to consume the pot's deposits.

Yet the text says the thick crud/corrosion does not depart even by fire.

Therefore:

**greater energy input**
does not necessarily produce
**successful reset/cleansing**.

This is useful as a failure mode.

For the physical analogy:

**energy intensity alone is not sufficient**.

Outcome depends on:
- material properties;
- adhesion/residue;
- structure;
- duration;
- chemical/physical state.

This is an important check against a vague “more plasma fixes/changes everything” model.

# SECTION 53B2A textual / historical / scholarly controls

1. Ezekiel 21 has a chapter/verse-numbering offset between common English chaptering and the Hebrew tradition; comparisons must identify verses by content as well as number.
2. The Babylonian king's liver inspection belongs to historically attested ancient Near Eastern extispicy; the road fork is a real narrative decision point, not evidence of a physical cosmic switch.
3. Ezekiel 22's furnace is judgment imagery. Its mechanical value is the explicit gather→contain→blow→melt sequence.
4. Melting destroys or radically changes prior material form, so it is a **negative control** for the connectivity-preserving fingertrap.
5. Ezekiel 23 explicitly interprets Oholah and Oholibah as Samaria and Jerusalem.
6. The sexualized violence of Ezekiel 23 is rhetorically and ethically difficult; feminist/postcolonial scholarship treats the female-city bodies as part of political/colonial polemic. Preserve the paired-city topology without converting the violence into neutral mechanics.
7. Ezekiel 24 explicitly dates the beginning of Nebuchadnezzar's siege of Jerusalem to the sign's date.
8. The pot allegory distinguishes vessel, contents, water/process medium and fire. The comparison is strongest at that role/process level.
9. The persistent corrosion/crud in the pot shows failed purification/reset even under heating; do not equate destructive heat with successful renewal.
10. Ezekiel's wife/bereavement episode is an enacted prophetic sign. The secure process is observed body-state → interpretation → later survivor-message → restored speech.
11. None of Ezekiel 21–24 explicitly describes a broad woven field physically contracting into a narrow axial braid and reopening.
12. The strongest direct Vardath contributions are therefore:
   - branch selection;
   - convergence classification;
   - paired branch trajectories;
   - carrier/cargo separation;
   - message-carrier state switching.
