# Jeremiah 25 as textual hinge — MT and LXX do not put the same material in the same place

This is especially important for the reconstructed 88-text Bible.

In the Masoretic form:
- the Oracles Against the Nations appear near the end of Jeremiah, chapters 46–51.

In the Old Greek/LXX form:
- the nations material occurs in the middle of the book, after the Jeremiah 25 hinge;
- the individual national oracles also have a different order;
- the Greek Jeremiah is substantially shorter overall.

### Research consequence

For the final transformation timeline:
we must distinguish:

1. **event/process order within each oracle**;
2. **book/editorial order in MT**;
3. **book/editorial order in LXX/Greek**;
4. **Ethiopic ordering where recoverable**.

The position of a text in one edition cannot by itself establish historical chronology.

# SECTION 50B2 textual / literary / historical controls

1. Jeremiah 21's life/death routes concern Babylonian siege/surrender.
2. Jeremiah 23's storm and word-as-fire/hammer are prophetic judgment/information metaphors.
3. Jeremiah 24 directly maps two fig baskets to two political/exilic populations; its value is in paired outcome structure, not literal botanical mechanics.
4. Jeremiah 25 is one of the major textual hinges where MT and LXX literary architecture diverge.
5. In LXX/Old Greek the Oracles Against the Nations occur mid-book after the Jeremiah 25 hinge; in MT they occur later in chapters 46–51.
6. The individual nation-oracle order also differs between editions.
7. The Greek Jeremiah is substantially shorter; Qumran evidence supports ancient Hebrew pluriformity, but individual readings still require case-by-case judgment.
8. The 70 years has no single uncontested chronological explanation in scholarship.
9. Mark Leuchter's Esarhaddon/Babylon 70-year proposal is a significant historical-literary hypothesis, not a settled fact.
10. Jeremiah 27–28's yoke imagery is explicitly political/imperial submission imagery and belongs to broader ancient Near Eastern imperial discourse.
