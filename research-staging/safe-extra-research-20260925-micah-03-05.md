# SECTION 57C2 — Micah 3–5
## Micah 3 attacks corrupted “head” and message-control subsystems: rulers who should know justice are portrayed as flaying, breaking and cooking the people; prophets switch between “peace” and “war” depending on whether they are fed; their information channel then undergoes night/darkness/sunset and receives no answer, while Micah contrasts himself as a functioning message channel. The consequence is materialized as Zion plowed into field, Jerusalem reduced to heap, and the temple mountain becoming a wooded height. Micah 4 deliberately reverses that same node: in days to come the temple mountain is established/raised above the hills; many peoples “stream” uphill to it, while instruction flows outward from Zion. Weapons are reworked into agricultural tools, private vine/fig-tree shelter becomes a stable non-fear state, and lame/cast-off populations are gathered into a remnant and strong nation. Daughter Zion then enters labor, leaves city for open country and Babylon, and is rescued there; hostile nations unknowingly converge on Zion like sheaves gathered to a threshing floor, after which Zion receives iron-horn/bronze-hoof imagery and threshes them. Micah 5 adds siege/rod imagery, a small Bethlehem source-node producing a ruler “from ancient days,” labor/birth timing before kindred return, shepherding/security, seven/eight defender-number control, and the remnant’s striking dual projection as both dew/showers and lion/predator. The block ends by removing horses, chariots, strongholds, sorcery, images and sacred poles—stability is produced partly by dismantling old dependence/control subsystems. Strong node-state reversal, uphill convergence, outward information flow, material repurposing, remnant gathering, birth/transfer, hostile-convergence and successor-security correspondences; strict spiderweb→fingertrap geometry remains absent.

# SECTION 57C2 textual / historical / scholarly controls

1. Working base text: Micah 3–5 NRSVUE.
2. Micah 3’s skin/flesh/bone/cooking imagery is political exploitation metaphor in context. It is vivid layered-body imagery but not direct anatomy/cosmology.
3. Micah 3:6–7’s night/darkness/sunset is tied directly to failure of vision/revelation. Keep information-darkness distinct from physical atmospheric darkness elsewhere.
4. Micah 3:8’s “spirit/power/justice/might” is prophetic empowerment language; do not force one physical-energy interpretation.
5. Micah 3:12 is quoted in Jeremiah 26:18 as a remembered Micah oracle, making it a particularly clear example of warning tradition preserved and reused.
6. Micah 3:12 and 4:1–2 form a striking literary state contrast: temple mountain decommissioned/wooded, then future temple mountain elevated and attracting nations.
7. Micah 4:1–3 has very close wording with Isaiah 2:2–4. The literary direction/common-source relationship is debated. Marvin Sweeney (2001) argues that the passages have distinct literary/political functions rather than simply solving the dependence question.
8. “Peoples shall stream” is human pilgrimage/convergence imagery; it is intentionally flow-like but should not be read as literal uphill hydrology.
9. Swords→plowshares and spears→pruning hooks are material-tool repurposing. This is genuine functional transformation but not reversible fingertrap deformation.
10. Vine/fig-tree security is household/agricultural stability imagery, not evidence for a global canopy.
11. Micah 4:6–8 explicitly gathers lame/driven-away populations and makes them a remnant/strong nation, an active successor-building sequence.
12. Micah 4:10’s first verb has a Hebrew uncertainty footnoted by NRSVUE; the secure process is labor-like pain, city departure, open-country transit, Babylon and rescue there.
13. Micah 4:11–13 reinterprets hostile national convergence as source-level gathering to a threshing floor. This is an agricultural processing metaphor.
14. Horn iron/hoof bronze is poetic strengthening/hybrid imagery, not a literal metallic creature.
15. Micah 5:1 has uncertain Hebrew wording and differing verse numbering; do not base exact wall/gate geometry on it.
16. Micah 5:2’s “from old/ancient days” has extensive Jewish/Christian reception history. Preserve primary temporal/origin language separately from later doctrinal conclusions and from any time-travel hypothesis.
17. Micah 5:3 repeats childbirth/labor timing before remnant/kindred return. Childbirth metaphor remains a phase-transition analogue, not proof of physical throat mechanics.
18. Micah 5:5’s seven/eight leaders are often treated in commentary as an abundance/sufficiency x/x+1 expression. Exact identity of the leaders is debated.
19. Micah 5:7–8 deliberately gives the same remnant two radically different analogical roles: dew/showers and lion. This strongly supports projection-role separation.
20. Micah 5:10–14 removes military, divinatory and cultic systems. Stable successor state is therefore subtractively reconfigured, not merely restored wholesale.
21. Strict fingertrap status remains negative.
