# 2,300 evenings/mornings — preserve the scholarly calculation dispute

The text gives:
**2,300 evenings and mornings**.

Interpretation is debated.

Options in scholarship include:
- 2,300 full days;
- counting evening/morning offerings in a way yielding a shorter day count;
- other chronological reconstructions.

A 2022 peer-reviewed study by Michael Segal and Shlomo Wadler argues that 2,300 can be understood as a count of missed daily offerings under a reconstructed early halakhic scheme and maps it to approximately the 3.5-year tradition elsewhere in Daniel.

## Permanent research rule

Record:
**2,300 evenings/mornings**.

Do not silently replace it with:
- 1,150 days;
- 2,300 days;
- 3.5 years

without labeling the interpretive calculation.

This will matter when Daniel 9 and 12 timing systems are compared.

# SECTION 55B textual / literary / historical controls

1. Daniel 8 returns to Hebrew after Daniel 7's Aramaic ending.
2. The Ulai location has a real textual/translation issue: NRSVUE prints “Gate” but notes “or river,” with ancient-version support for river wording. Do not build portal mechanics on “gate.”
3. The text explicitly identifies the ram horns with Media/Persia and the goat with Greece. This interpretation has priority.
4. The great horn is interpreted as the first Greek king, commonly correlated historically with Alexander the Great; the four horns are successor kingdoms rather than a literal fourfold physical fracture map.
5. The no-ground-contact goat image conveys exceptional speed/unimpeded advance in context; it is not evidence of biological levitation.
6. Horn breakage is fracture, not continuous deformation. This makes Daniel 8 a negative control for strict fingertrap mechanics.
7. The exact Hebrew syntax around the small horn's emergence in 8:9 has textual/grammatical difficulty; NRSVUE flags the reading. Do not build exact branching geometry on an uncertain antecedent without witness work.
8. Historical-critical scholarship broadly reads the later aggressive horn/ruler against the Antiochus IV Epiphanes crisis and temple desecration of the second century BCE. The vision is coded political resistance/apocalypse before physical cosmology.
9. “Host” and “stars” function within apocalyptic symbolism; star-casting should not be treated automatically as literal stellar impact.
10. The 2,300 evenings/mornings calculation is genuinely disputed. Michael Segal and Shlomo Wadler (2022) propose an offering-count reconstruction mapping the figure to the 3.5-year tradition; other scholars calculate differently. Preserve the raw expression.
11. The chapter itself supplies named interpretation through Gabriel. External Vardath mapping must never replace it.
12. Daniel's falling/trance/sickness are narrative observer-state data, not direct evidence of a physical field unless independently supported.
13. “Broken not by human hands” is an agency statement; exact mechanism remains unspecified.
14. Strict fingertrap status remains negative.
