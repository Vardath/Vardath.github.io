# SECTION 54B textual / literary / historical controls

1. Daniel 4 explicitly interprets the giant tree as Nebuchadnezzar and his sovereignty. The world-tree/axis comparison is secondary structural analysis.
2. The centre-of-earth / heaven-reaching / whole-earth imagery is royal universalizing symbolism and hyperbole in context; do not treat it as a surveyed physical world axis.
3. Daniel 4 physically severs branches and disperses dependent creatures. That is a negative control against strict same-weave fingertrap contraction.
4. The retained stump/root is explicitly tied to future kingdom reestablishment and is therefore a secure restoration-core image.
5. The role of the iron/bronze band is not explained by the text and has been interpreted differently. Do not assign modern electrical/mechanical function.
6. Peter J. Atkins' peer-reviewed work emphasizes Daniel 4's deliberate crossing/troubling of divine-human-animal category boundaries. This supports treating the human→animal state change as a core literary mechanism rather than an incidental detail.
7. The story gives a 12-month delay and a seven-times altered-state period; they are distinct clocks.
8. Daniel 5 reuses the Jerusalem temple vessels already introduced in Daniel 1. This is genuine cross-chapter cargo continuity.
9. The hand-writing scene depicts fingers/hand without first presenting a full sender body. “Projection” is a useful abstract label only; the source is a divine-judgment narrative.
10. Marian Broida analyzes Daniel 5:25 as written divination/mantic interpretation. This is a strong caution against treating mene/tekel/peres as ordinary engineering measurements.
11. The terms also participate in weight/wordplay traditions. Preserve the linguistic puzzle rather than flattening it into a single literal translation.
12. Donald Polaski's study of Daniel 5–6 emphasizes writing and political power/resistance across the two chapters; this supports treating inscription/decree as control-plane phenomena.
13. Daniel 6's “law of Medes and Persians” is a literary depiction of irrevocable imperial law. Chaya Halberstam's 2024 analysis situates the chapter as a trial story about state-enforced law and competing legal authority.
14. Exact physical reconstruction of the lions' den should remain conservative. The secure features are mouth/opening, stone closure, sealing, downward insertion, upward extraction.
15. A sealed primary interface does not prove complete physical isolation. The previously researched Bel temple hidden-door episode is the project's standing negative control.
16. Daniel 6 and the Bel-and-the-Dragon lions' den are distinct traditions with different durations, transfer mechanisms and narrative settings. Never synthesize them silently.
17. The lions' differential response is a literary/theological miracle pattern, not experimental evidence that biological cargo physically changes a hazard field.
18. Strict fingertrap status remains negative for Daniel 4–6.
