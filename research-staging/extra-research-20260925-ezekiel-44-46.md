# Ezekiel 44:1–3 — the outer east gate preserves memory of a completed passage

After the glory has entered from the east in Ezekiel 43, Ezekiel is brought back to the **outer east gate**.

It is shut.

The explanation is causal:

**YHWH entered through it**
→ therefore
**it remains shut**.

This is not just a static wall.

The present gate state depends on a previous event.

## Process abstraction

**gate open / traversable in earlier phase**
→ privileged transit occurs
→ transit changes later permitted state
→ gate remains closed.

This is a genuine form of **state memory / hysteresis**.

For Vardath modelling, this is important because a gate need not return immediately to its pre-event state once a transfer finishes.

Possible variables now include:
- previous transit history;
- current access state;
- privileged carrier identity;
- reopening rules;
- whether closure is reversible or permanent within the represented cycle.

### Prince exception is not ordinary passage

The prince may sit in the gate to eat before YHWH and reaches it by the vestibule route, but the text does not simply reopen the east outer gate for ordinary through-transit.

Keep separate:
- **presence in / access to gate complex**;
- **crossing through the gate from one domain to the other**.

This distinction becomes essential in chapter 46.

# SECTION 53C3b-1 textual / historical / scholarly controls

1. Ezekiel 44–46 belongs to the visionary temple/community program, not a technical manual for cosmic transport.
2. The **outer east gate** of Ezekiel 44 and **inner east gate** of Ezekiel 46 must be distinguished. The apparent “always shut / periodically open” tension is largely resolved once the gate levels are kept separate.
3. Julia Rhyder's peer-reviewed analysis of Ezekiel 40–48 independently supports reading the gates as a differential access system organized by **level, direction and timing**.
4. The exact route and stopping point of the prince around the vestibule/posts in Ezekiel 44:3 and 46:2 has interpretive uncertainty. Preserve the secure result — restricted access differs from ordinary population access — without forcing a floor-plan reconstruction beyond the text.
5. Ezekiel 44's priest/Levite distinctions are cultic and historical-theological categories. Their systems use here is an abstraction about access classes, not a claim that the text describes physical particle sorting.
6. Garment change is explicitly ritual/holiness logic. It supplies a structural analogue for carrier-state conversion, not proof of atmosphere or field-state conversion.
7. Ezekiel 45:1 contains a textual measurement issue reflected directly in the NRSVUE footnote: Greek supports the 20,000-width rendering while Hebrew has ten. Exact geometry must remain witness-aware.
8. Ezekiel 45's honest-measure and anti-dispossession rules show that the restored order is ethical/administrative as well as spatial.
9. Ezekiel 46:9 explicitly imposes directional through-flow on ordinary festival worshippers. This is a secure routing rule, but it is ritual choreography rather than evidence of a physical one-way portal.
10. The four corner kitchens are functional processing spaces. Fourfold geometry alone is not evidence for a four-force or four-world mechanism.
11. No connected broad weave contracts into a narrow throat in 44–46. Strict fingertrap status remains negative.
