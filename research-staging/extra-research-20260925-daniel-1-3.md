# SECTION 54A textual / literary / historical controls

1. Daniel 1–6 is conventionally analyzed as a set of court tales about Daniel and his companions living under foreign imperial rule.
2. Daniel 1 begins in Hebrew; Daniel 2:4b through 7:28 is Aramaic in the Masoretic form.
3. Modern critical scholarship generally places the final form of Daniel in the second-century BCE Maccabean crisis, despite the sixth-century BCE narrative setting; individual court tales may preserve older materials.
4. Daniel 2 explicitly interprets the statue's material zones as successive kingdoms. Any Vardath physical mapping is therefore secondary comparative geometry.
5. The iron/clay feet are explicitly described as divided, partly strong/brittle, mixed yet not holding together. This makes material-interface incompatibility a secure textual abstraction.
6. Statue destruction is total fragmentation and wind dispersal. It is not same-material geometric reshaping.
7. The stone/mountain is a separate successor image; do not treat it as reassembled statue material.
8. Daniel 3's gold image is an imperial cult/idolatry narrative device. Its 60×6 dimensions are source data, not automatic cosmological dimensions.
9. The musical sequence functions as an explicit social command signal. Synchronized falling can therefore occur without physical linkage—a crucial control for any “coordinated movement proves lattice” argument.
10. The furnace story is miracle/theological narrative. Differential fire effects are literary facts of the story, not laboratory evidence for real high-temperature shielding.
11. The MT/Aramaic text does not explain the exact micro-mechanism by which the three become unbound.
12. The identity of the fourth figure is described from Nebuchadnezzar's perspective as divine/godlike; later he calls the deliverer an angel. Do not turn the fourth into an independently identified physical operator.
13. Ancient Greek Daniel contains the Prayer of Azariah and Song of the Three inserted within the furnace story. That material has already been researched in Section 43 and must remain textually labeled rather than silently merged with MT Daniel 3.
14. Old Greek and Theodotion are major distinct Greek Daniel traditions. The final 88-text edition/research should maintain witness labels wherever the furnace interior depends on one of them.
