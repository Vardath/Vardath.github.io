# SECTION 56B4 — Joel 3:1–21
## Scattered population and divided land are answered by a reverse topology in which the nations are gathered to one judgment node; displaced people and prior actions are directionally “returned”; agricultural tools are materially/functionally converted into weapons; ripeness/fullness triggers harvest/winepress processing; celestial darkening and earth/heaven shaking recur while one source functions simultaneously as threat and refuge; Jerusalem’s boundary becomes selectively closed; and Joel ends with an abundance/hydrology inversion in which dry streambeds flow and a fountain from the house of YHWH waters a downstream wadi — a strong source→channel→land analogue that remains intertextually and theologically controlled rather than treated as direct Vardath plumbing

This completes **Joel** inside the reconstructed roadmap:

> **SECTION 56 = Hosea, Joel, Amos**

English Joel 3 corresponds to **Hebrew Joel 4**.

That numbering difference is preserved throughout to prevent false duplication.

Active controls:
- gathering populations into a valley is social/political/theological convergence, not strict lattice contraction;
- “return” of deeds or displaced people is directional/retributive restoration language, not literal time reversal;
- sale/trade of people is an indictment of human commodification; recording its transfer mechanics does not normalize or endorse it;
- plowshare/sword and pruning-hook/spear are real functional transformations of tools, but not world-lattice transformations;
- harvest/winepress fullness is threshold metaphor, not automatically plasma pressure;
- “valley of decision” is judicial/divine decision in context, not people standing at a free-choice crossroads;
- sun/moon/star changes are celestial/apocalyptic state language with physical mechanism unspecified;
- fountain-from-temple imagery is a strong source/channel motif but has close intertextual parallels, especially Ezekiel 47 and Zechariah 14;
- strict fingertrap still requires broad connected weave → reorientation/narrowing → axial throat → preserved connectivity → reopening.

# JOEL MASTER textual / intertextual controls

1. Hamborg identifies Joel’s three large literary sections as 1:1–2:27; 2:28–32; 3:1–21.
2. Joel 2:28–32 is numbered Hebrew chapter 3; English Joel 3 is Hebrew chapter 4.
3. The four locust terms are uncertain and recur in different order.
4. Literal locust catastrophe versus military-metaphor emphasis remains debated; the text itself deliberately overlaps the image classes.
5. Joel 2:18 contains tense uncertainty affecting exact placement of the divine-response transition.
6. Joel 2:20 front/rear/east/west removal should not be over-literalized as simultaneous fluid vectors without control.
7. Joel 2:23 has Hebrew uncertainty in the rain phrase.
8. “Restore years” and “restore fortunes” are forward compensation/state restoration, not time reversal.
9. Joel 2:28–32 may represent a later compositional insertion now linking the restoration unit to the final Day/nations unit.
10. Acts 2 later reuses Joel 2:28–32; later reception must be separated from Joel’s original event structure.
11. “Valley of decision” in Joel 3:14 concerns divine judgment/verdict in context, not the crowds choosing.
12. Joel 3:10 reverses the tool-transformation direction familiar from Isaiah 2:4 / Micah 4:3.
13. Joel 3:18’s fountain has close prophetic intertexts, especially Ezekiel 47:1–12 and Zechariah 14:8; shared tradition/literary dependence is a live alternative to independent physical-event reporting.
14. Joel 3:16’s “YHWH roars from Zion / gives voice from Jerusalem” directly links into Amos 1:2 and is recognized as a Book-of-the-Twelve seam.
15. Joel 3:18’s mountain-wine abundance also has a close relation to Amos 9:13, strengthening the Joel/Amos literary comparison.
16. Joel 3:21 has a significant Greek/Syriac vs Hebrew textual variant in the bloodguilt clause.
17. Strict fingertrap status for Joel as a whole remains negative.
