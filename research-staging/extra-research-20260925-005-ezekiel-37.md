# Papyrus 967 process-order control — decisive for Ezekiel 37

Papyrus 967 is a late-second/early-third-century Greek manuscript witness to Ezekiel.

Its best-known large-scale difference here is:

**36 → 38 → 39 → 37 → 40**

rather than the Masoretic:

**36 → 37 → 38 → 39 → 40**.

It also lacks the large expansion commonly cited around **36:23c–38**.

Therefore Ezekiel 37 occupies a radically different process position in this early Greek witness.

### MT process placement

expanded restoration in 36
→ dry-bones reassembly
→ breath
→ two-stick unification
→ Gog/Magog assault
→ cleanup/restoration closure.

### P967 process placement

abbreviated 36
→ Gog/Magog assault
→ destruction/cleanup/restoration closure
→ dry-bones reassembly
→ breath
→ two-stick unification
→ temple.

### Vardath significance

The two orders produce different transformation narratives.

The P967 order is especially important because it places:

**major destructive conflict**
→ **cleanup**
→ **spirit/breath restoration**
→ **structural/social unification**
→ **temple/new ordered centre**.

That resembles a catastrophe→reconstruction sequence far more strongly than the MT order.

But this is a textual-history observation, not grounds for choosing P967 solely because it fits the cosmology better.

Both ancient forms must remain visible.

1. Ezekiel 37 explicitly interprets the dry bones as the whole house of Israel; national restoration controls the primary reading.
2. Later Jewish and Christian traditions developed resurrection readings, but the chapter's immediate internal interpretation is restoration from exile/cut-off national death.
3. The Hebrew **ruach** spans wind/breath/spirit; NRSVUE footnotes preserve this lexical ambiguity.

8. The population interpretation expands the sign into gathering from every quarter and one nation/kingdom.
9. Stable restoration includes land, ruler/shepherd and sanctuary; movement alone is not the endpoint.
10. Papyrus 967 places chapters 38–39 before 37 and lacks the substantial end-unit of chapter 36 found in MT.
11. Ingrid Lilly treats P967 and MT as variant literary editions rather than reducing every difference to scribal accident.
12. Ashley Crane's textual-comparative study likewise emphasizes the macro-order and chapter-36 minus as meaningful textual phenomena.
13. The P967 order must not be preferred merely because its catastrophe→restoration sequence maps more neatly onto Vardath Cosmology.
