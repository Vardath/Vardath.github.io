# SECTION 56B1 textual / historical / scholarly controls

1. Hamborg’s 2023 New Cambridge Bible Commentary treats Joel 1:1–2:27 as one major literary section centered on disastrous locust plague, communal appeal and promised land restoration. SECTION 56B1 is a manageable append subdivision inside that larger unit, not a claim that chapter 1 stands independently in composition.
2. The four Hebrew locust terms in Joel 1:4 are uncertain. Major translations and NET notes differ over species, stages, swarms or synonymous devastation terms.
3. Scholars disagree over whether Joel’s locust material is primarily a literal ecological plague metaphorized as military invasion, a hostile army expressed as locust catastrophe, or deliberately overlapping imagery. Keep both ecological and military projection layers visible.
4. Joel 1:6’s “nation” and lion-teeth imagery do not establish one hybrid creature.
5. Tree stripping is agriculturally explicit. It is not evidence that a world-tree/lattice is being peeled.
6. The ecological crisis clearly propagates into offerings and priestly life, showing resource dependence of institutions; that does not mean the temple itself is physically destroyed in Joel 1.
7. Joel 1:17 contains difficult Hebrew. Fine seed/clod mechanics should not be over-read.
8. The fire/dried-watercourse ending is physically suggestive but underdetermined. The text does not identify plasma, atmospheric canopy failure, or inter-world water motion.
9. The Day of YHWH language gives theological/temporal interpretation to the present crisis; “near” is not an exact recurrence interval.
10. Strict fingertrap status remains negative.
