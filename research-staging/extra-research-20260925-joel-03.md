# SECTION 56B4 textual / historical / scholarly controls

1. NRSVUE/Bible Gateway notes English Joel 3:1–21 is numbered Joel 4:1–21 in Hebrew. Normalize content before chapter numbering.
2. Graham R. Hamborg’s 2023 New Cambridge Bible Commentary treats Joel 3:1–21 as the final literary section: judgment on the nations and blessing/restoration for Israel/Judah.
3. Bradford A. Anderson’s 2024 reception commentary surveys multiple proposed locations for the Valley of Jehoshaphat and notes that modern readers/commentators commonly take it symbolically as the valley of YHWH’s judgment or otherwise leave the geography uncertain.
4. Therefore Valley of Jehoshaphat should be retained as a convergence/judgment node in process mapping, not asserted as a known physical gate site.
5. Joel 3:10’s plowshare→sword / pruning-hook→spear language has strong intertextual relation with Isaiah 2:4 and Micah 4:3. Seung Ho Bang (2015) and other scholarship treat Joel as creatively reusing/reversing these traditions in a holy-war/Day-of-YHWH setting.
6. Intertextual reversal proves literary transformation of a motif, not recurrence of a physical apparatus.
7. Joel 3:13’s harvest/winepress language is judgment metaphor; ripeness/fullness/overflow are secure process relations, but no electrical/plasma/pressure threshold is named.
8. Sun/moon/stars darkening repeats Joel’s celestial event signature, but its physical cause remains underdetermined.
9. YHWH’s roar/voice from Zion and shaking heavens/earth are theophanic/judgment language. Source-location and effect-domain relations may be abstracted; propagation physics cannot be supplied from the verse.
10. Joel 3:18’s house-of-YHWH fountain is a genuine source→outflow image. Scholarly comparison with Ezekiel 47 and Zechariah 14 is well established, including Tova Ganzel’s discussion of the temple-water prophetic cluster.
11. The meaning/location of Wadi Shittim is disputed. Proposed readings include a literal place east of Jordan, “Wadi/Valley of Acacias,” Kidron-related identification, or symbolic/otherwise unidentifiable target.
12. One modern study explicitly compares Joel 3:18, Ezekiel 47:1–12 and Zechariah 14:8 and argues for literal future water using an Eden image; other scholarship reads these waters symbolically or as eschatological abundance. Preserve the interpretive range rather than choosing the result that best matches Vardath.
13. Temple-stream intertextuality is a strong alternative explanation for repeated source→water geometry across prophets; structural recurrence is not by itself independent physical confirmation.
14. Joel 3:21 is translation-sensitive. NRSVUE “avenge their blood / not clear the guilty” coexists with readings such as avenging blood not yet avenged or declaring/holding innocent blood; the Hebrew wording is difficult.
15. Do not build fine cosmological mechanics on the contested Joel 3:21 verb.
16. “Forever / generation to generation” is enduring-state language, not a measurable recurrence interval.
17. Strict fingertrap status for Joel remains negative despite the strong temple-fountain geometry.
