# Textual / scholarly controls — Ezekiel 25–28

1. Ezekiel 26–28 is compositionally layered.
2. Ezekiel 27's canonical form integrates ship and trade-network imagery, though scholars often distinguish an older ship-dirge core from later commercial expansions.
3. Jacqueline Vayntrub's load analysis is especially useful: Tyre's accumulated “weight/glory” becomes dangerous excess load.
4. Ezekiel 28's king/cherub relation differs between MT and Old Greek.
5. Critical scholarship does not require identifying the figure with Satan; royal, primordial-human, priestly, cherubic and mythic readings all matter.
6. Therefore use Ezekiel 28 as high-centre/guardian/downward-fall topology, not as literal cosmological biography.
7. Regathering in 28:25–26 is a historical-theological restoration image before any Vardath comparison.
