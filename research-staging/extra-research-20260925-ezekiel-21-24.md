# SECTION 53B2a textual / historical / scholarly controls

1. Ezekiel 21's road fork is historical military decision imagery, not literal cosmic bifurcation.
2. The Babylonian divination cluster is culturally plausible: hepatoscopy and other mantic procedures were established ancient Near Eastern state practices.
3. The arrow-divination procedure is less completely documented for Neo-Babylonia than hepatoscopy; avoid over-precise ritual reconstruction.
4. Ezekiel 21's high/low inversion is royal/political status language.
5. Ezekiel 22's furnace is metaphorical judgment; humans are represented as mixed/dross metals.
6. Ancient pyrotechnology confirms that airflow, furnace conditions and material composition matter, but do not transfer literal metallurgical temperatures to the biblical population.
7. Ezekiel 22 repeats wall/whitewash/breach vocabulary from chapter 13, strengthening the book-level structural contrast.
8. Ezekiel 23 explicitly identifies the two women as Samaria and Jerusalem; the sexual allegory should be treated as political/theological rhetoric and not literalized.
9. The “same way” statement is nevertheless a strong paired-process marker.
10. Ezekiel 24's pot terminology around rust/scum/corrosion contains lexical uncertainty; the safe result is persistent contamination/residue in the vessel.
11. The chapter intentionally shifts from cooking contents to heating the empty vessel itself.
12. Ezekiel 24's date corresponds to the beginning of the final Babylonian siege in the biblical historical tradition.
13. Exact modern-calendar conversion remains debated within broader 587/586 BCE chronology discussions.
14. The death of Ezekiel's wife is a prophetic sign-act; suppressing mourning is culturally inverted behavior used symbolically.
15. Ezekiel 24:26–27's escapee→message→opened-mouth sequence should be read as historical/narrative information transfer before any Vardath analogy.
16. Ezekiel's MT/Old Greek textual differences remain relevant throughout this section; no single unusual phrase should carry a physical-model claim without witness control.
