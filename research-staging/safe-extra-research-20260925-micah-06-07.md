# SECTION 57C3 — Micah 6–7 + MICAH / SECTION 57 SYNTHESIS
## Micah 6 turns mountains and the “enduring foundations of the earth” from catastrophe objects into witnesses in a covenant lawsuit, then recalls an older route-memory—Egypt → Moses/Aaron/Miriam → Balak/Balaam → Shittim/Gilgal—before rejecting escalating sacrifice quantities in favor of justice, loyal kindness and a humble walk. The chapter then returns to systems failure: short measure, wicked scales, deceitful weights, violence, lies and preserved Omri/Ahab practices generate output loops in which eating does not satisfy, carrying away does not save, sowing does not reach reaping, olive pressing does not reach oil use and grape pressing does not reach drinking. Micah 7 begins after the “harvest” with no fruit left, then depicts social trust failure from officials/judges down through friends, partners and households. The narrator instead enters a watcher/wait state and the book reverses: fall→rise, darkness→light, judgment→vindication, wall destruction→wall rebuilding, narrow/failed territory→far-extended boundary, dispersed populations→return from Assyria/Egypt/River/sea-to-sea/mountain-to-mountain, isolated flock→restored feeding in Bashan/Gilead, and frightened nations emerging from fortresses. The last sea-depth image is deliberately different from Jonah: sins/iniquities—not people—are cast into the deep. Micah as a whole therefore supplies strong mountain-state reversal, topographic deformation, convergence, boundary/gate, remnant assembly, information flow, measurement integrity, labor/exile, dispersal/return and stable-successor architecture, but never the strict same-support-weave broad→narrow→throat→reopening transformation.

This completes:

with:
- SECTION 57A1 — Obadiah;
- SECTION 57B1–B2 — Jonah;
- SECTION 57C1–C3 — Micah.
