# SECTION 53B2C textual / historical / scholarly controls

1. Pharaoh as Nile dragon belongs to royal/political and ancient Near Eastern water-monster imagery; it is not evidence that Pharaoh was literally a physical plasma serpent.
2. Ezekiel 29's hooks + attached fish provide a secure extraction sequence regardless of cosmological interpretation.
3. The reed-staff image explicitly tests support under load and should be retained as an engineering analogue, not a literal material report.
4. Egypt's forty-year desolation/restoration is a prophetic claim and should not be silently equated with modern Egyptian chronology without separate historical testing.
5. Ezekiel 29's 27th-year oracle demonstrates nonchronological placement inside the book.
6. Ezekiel 30's arm language is anthropomorphic political-military metaphor, but its strong/broken/unrepaired/strengthened state transitions are textually explicit.
7. Ezekiel 31 addresses Pharaoh through the Assyria/cedar comparison. The exact referential transitions in the poem have generated scholarly discussion; do not flatten every “tree” reference into one physical object.
8. The cedar is a classic political/world-tree image with water, branches, dependent creatures/nations and descent to Sheol. Its mechanics are felling/disassembly, not fingertrap contraction.
9. Ezekiel 32 repeats Pharaoh as water monster and explicitly uses an external net; monster and mesh are separate roles.
10. “Waters clear / streams like oil” follows removal of disturbing human/animal activity in the judgment sequence. It is a quiet-water endpoint, not proof of a post-gate hydrodynamic state.
11. Cosmic darkening in Ezekiel 32 belongs to prophetic judgment imagery and cannot independently establish astronomical/plasma effects.
12. The Pit catalogue is a literary lower-world/death geography populated by prior powers; it should be compared structurally, not literalized automatically.
13. **Papyrus 967 lacks Ezekiel 32:24–26**, according to published discussion of its significant minuses. Those verses cover Elam and the start of Meshech/Tubal in the MT/NRSVUE sequence.
14. Therefore the exact lower-world catalogue membership/order in this passage has significant textual-witness variation.
15. P967 also famously differs later in the chapter order around 36–40, but that later issue should be handled when the research reaches those chapters rather than imported prematurely.
