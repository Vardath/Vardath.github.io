# SECTION 56A4 textual / historical / scholarly controls

1. Hamborg’s literary division places Hosea 12–14 as the third major block of the book.
2. Goldingay’s 2024 Oxford Handbook chapter emphasizes Hosea 12–13’s ambiguity, allusiveness, wordplay and recollection. This strongly cautions against treating Jacob references as transparent physical descriptions.
3. Scholarly interpretation of Jacob in Hosea 12 varies. Van Ruiten’s work on the Targum shows that later interpretation can make Jacob a positive contrast/role model, while other readings see criticism or ambiguity.
4. Hosea 12:4 has pronoun textual variation (“him/us”); identity mechanics should not be built from that clause.
5. Hosea 12:8 has uncertain Hebrew; wealth/self-justification is secure at broad level, exact wording less so.
6. Hosea 12:11 and 12:12 also contain textual/translation variants.
7. Hosea 13:2 has textual uncertainty in its sacrificial/idol wording.
8. Hosea 13:5–6 has variants around “fed/knew/pasture.” The broad feed→satisfaction→pride→forgetting chain remains visible.
9. Hosea 13:9–10 contains textual variants concerning help/king wording.
10. Hosea 13:12’s “bound up/stored” can be read as retained record language; it is not physical binding evidence.
11. Hosea 13:13 is an explicit birth metaphor. The mouth/opening of the womb creates a strong threshold image, but not a literal cosmic throat.
12. Hosea 13:14 is textually and interpretively difficult. UBS/translation notes and NET discuss rescue-vs-rhetorical-question readings and “where/I will be” variants. The judgment context must remain visible.
13. Paul’s later use of Hosea 13:14 in 1 Corinthians 15 is reception/redeployment and should not be used to erase the ambiguity of Hosea’s own wording/context.
14. Hosea 13:15’s east wind → dry fountain/spring is a genuine environmental causal image but does not independently identify plasma, canopy collapse or world-layer transfer.
15. Hosea 14:2 has “fruit/bulls of lips” textual variation.
16. Hosea 14:5, 14:7 and 14:8 contain several textual/translation variants. The overall restoration chain—dew, growth, rooting, spreading, shade, vine/fruit—remains clear.
17. Hosea’s repeated rapid metaphor shifts require projection control; one physical transforming object should not be invented from them.
18. Strict fingertrap status for the whole book remains negative.
