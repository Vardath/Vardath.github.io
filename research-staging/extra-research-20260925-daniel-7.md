# SECTION 55A textual / literary / historical controls

1. Daniel 7 is the final Aramaic chapter in the Masoretic book; Daniel 8 returns to Hebrew. The language switch is part of the book's literary architecture and must be preserved.
2. The chapter explicitly interprets the beasts as kings and the fourth beast as a kingdom. Monster geometry is therefore political/apocalyptic symbolism before physical comparison.
3. Vision and interpretation differ in source-location language: beasts rise from sea in the vision, while the interpreter says kings arise from earth. This is strong evidence against naive literal geography.
4. Beast 1's wing removal/humanization is explicit but is not identified with Nebuchadnezzar. The Daniel 4 reverse-state comparison is structural, not an assertion of same entity.
5. Beast 2's raised side and Beast 3's four heads/wings have long histories of political interpretation. Do not convert them directly into physical world orientation.
6. Beast 4's iron teeth and bronze claws echo Daniel's material vocabulary, but the text treats these as monster/kingdom features.
7. The Ancient One's fiery wheeled throne strongly overlaps Ezekiel throne/fire/wheel imagery, but Daniel does not reproduce Ezekiel's detailed wheel mechanics.
8. The “one like a human being / son of man” remains interpretively contested. Major scholarly models include collective, angelic/representative and individual heavenly readings.
9. Daniel Boyarin's 2012 survey explicitly notes the two broad scholarly trajectories: collective symbolism and divine/angelic individual interpretation, with representative combinations also proposed. The Vardath project must not choose an identity merely to fit its cosmology.
10. Peter Schäfer's work traces the Daniel 7 human-like figure's later reception into Enochic and other Jewish Son-of-Man traditions. Those later developments should not be retrojected as if all were explicit in Daniel 7 itself.
11. The chapter itself connects final dominion with the holy ones/people of holy ones. Any individual-figure interpretation must remain in dialogue with this collective interpretation layer.
12. “Time, two times, and half a time” should be logged before chronological conversion.
13. The vision's fire stream is theophanic/judicial imagery, not demonstrated plasma physics.
14. Strict fingertrap status remains negative.
