# SECTION 52B textual / historical / ethical controls

1. Chapter 6–8 belongs more securely to the core narrative than much of chapter 9.
2. The eagle, fresh figs, resurrection sign and Jordan sorting are miraculous/etiological narrative devices, not physical reportage.
3. Chapter 8's Samaritan origin story is historically incompatible with Kings and other evidence; Allison explicitly treats it as polemical/etiological fiction.
4. The anti-intermarriage logic reflects ancient sectarian boundary-making and must not be endorsed as a modern social prescription.
5. The Jordan functions literarily as a return/identity threshold.
6. The 15-day interval and Jordan “great seal” are narrative ritual/identity controls, not demonstrated cosmological timing or physical sorting.
7. Herzer argues for a Jewish core with Christian ending; Allison similarly reconstructs a Jewish edition but sees Christian hands in more than only the final verses.
8. Allison states that two-thirds or more of received chapter 9 may derive from a later Christian hand; the original Jewish ending is therefore uncertain.
9. The 477-year prophecy, Jesus, twelve apostles and related chapter-9 transformations must be classified as Christian redactional/reception material.
10. The stone double belongs to the received Longer Version ending and is methodologically valuable for identity/form analysis but weak as independent early-Jewish physical evidence.
11. The Shorter and Longer Versions diverge in wording, chronology details and ending material; exact claims must be recension-tagged.
12. The full work likely reflects post-70 CE Jewish concerns and an early-second-century setting, not direct memory of sixth-century BCE events.
13. Its unusual phenomena are most valuable as **narrative topologies and test ideas**, not as direct empirical observations.
