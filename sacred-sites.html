Scope and accuracy. This is an evolving geographic index. The expanded landmark list draws on public religious and archaeological reference sources and selected community research leads. Candidates without a Wikipedia coordinate are not pinned. Traveller recollections are not archaeological verification. The global landmark layer retrieves coordinates from Wikipedia; regional markers use OpenStreetMap/Overpass. These sources are not complete inventories, and coordinates of large areas can represent a feature centre rather than a ritual focal point. Ancient monuments are candidates for comparative study, not automatic evidence of sacred use.

Sensitive or undisclosed Indigenous places should not be exposed without custodians' consent. Respect site access restrictions.

Research leads: Traveller discussions · Shared Sacred Sites · Sacred Natural Sites Initiative · UNESCO World Heritage. Only public, source-located candidates appear on the atlas.

Sources: OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL) · Wikipedia / Wikidata · Overpass API. The number shown is only the records loaded in this browser session; never a worldwide total.
