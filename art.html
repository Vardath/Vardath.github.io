Vardath Art Archive

One place for the visual material gathered through the Vardath Cosmology research: historical objects, mythic imagery, physical analogues, museum corpus hits, control material and the source-linked images used to explain the evolving model.

Source discipline: every research card links back to its museum, heritage, archive or Wikimedia source record wherever one was recorded. The gallery is an archive of what was actually used or gathered during the research, not a claim that every object depicts the same phenomenon.
