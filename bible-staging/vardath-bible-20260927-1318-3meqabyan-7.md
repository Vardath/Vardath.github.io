## 3 Meqabyan 7

**1.** Those who trust in the LORD have no trembling or fear, as David said: “In the LORD I have trusted; I will not fear. What can a son of man do to me?”

**2.** Again he said: “Let the adversary test me; as for me, in him I have trusted. One thing I asked of the LORD, and that I seek. Whoever trusts in him shall live forever and shall not fear evil tidings.”

**3.** Who has trusted in the LORD and been put to shame? Who has called upon him and been met with silence?

**4.** And who has hoped in him and been put to shame? For he said: “The one who loved me, I love; the one who honored me, I honor; and the one who turned to me, I shelter.”

**5.** Save the soul of the aged woman and deliver them, so that the LORD may save you from everything that rises against you for evil. He will also save your offspring after you, for the generation of the righteous shall be blessed. They shall not conceive evil, but shall prosper and give.
