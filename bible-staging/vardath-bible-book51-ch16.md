## 1 Meqabyan 16

**1.** The people of Tyre and Sidon, those who dwell by the sea and in the regions of the Jordan, Gilead and Harran, the Canaanites, the Jebusites, the Girgashites, Edom, and the people of Amalek—

**2.** all these nations remain in their lands, according to their tribes, their customs, their territories, and their languages, each as the LORD has appointed for them.

**3.** Among them are some whose conduct is good and who know the LORD.

**4.** And among them are others whose deeds are evil and who do not know the LORD who created them; he subjects them according to their deeds, as he did by the hand of Ameneser king of Syria.

**5.** He took the strength of Damascus and divided the spoil of Samaria before the king of Egypt.

**6.** The mountains of Gilead and the plains of Gilboa, all Samaria and Patos of the land of Judah, those who dwell in the mountains of Arabia, the Medes, the Parthians, Siwsigya, and Cappadocia—

**7.** those who dwell throughout these regions are a stiff-necked people who do not know the LORD and do not keep his commandment.

**8.** He will repay them according to the evil of their conduct and according to the works of their hands.

**9.** For the district of Caesarea, the people of Gilead, and Amalek will join together to attack the city of the LORD. But you are filled with righteousness; within you the Holy One of Israel is praised—the God of Israel, the LORD of hosts, who sits above the chariot of the cherubim. A thousand thousands stand before him, and ten thousand times ten thousand serve him in fear and trembling.
