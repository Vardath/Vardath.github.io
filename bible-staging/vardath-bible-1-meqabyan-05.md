## 1 Meqabyan 5

*The Beta maṣāḥǝft XML has a line-numbering defect after verse 4: the continuation of verse 4 is labeled “7” before the sequence resumes at verse 5. The continuation has been restored to verse 4 here; no additional verse has been created.*

**1.** Raʾayitawi grew proud. By one small affliction God caused his body to swell from his feet to his head, and by that slight thing he died.

**2.** Again Nimrod grew proud. Because of the greatness of his strength he made for himself a bed of iron, but God also overthrew him.

**3.** Again Nebuchadnezzar grew proud and said, “There is no other king besides me. I am the one who makes the sun rise over the earth.” Because of the abundance of his pride he spoke in this way.

**4.** The LORD drove him away from mankind for seven years and made his portion with the wild beasts and the birds of heaven, until he knew that the LORD was the one who had created him.

**5.** When he came to know him, the LORD restored him again to his kingdom. Who has resisted and exalted himself against the LORD his Creator and has not been brought down to dust?

**6.** Who has transgressed his law and his ordinance without the earth swallowing him up?

**7.** And you, Tsiruts-Saydan, desire to exalt yourself against your Creator. Yet what befell them awaits you also: he will strip away your pride and bring you down into the dust of death.

**8.** He will bring you down still farther into Sheol, into the deepest darkness, where there is weeping and gnashing of teeth, into the lowest depth, into the great burning pit from which there is no escape forever.

**9.** You are a man, and you are dust, though you boast. You will die like the proud kings who were before you and who left behind the possessions of this world.

**10.** Therefore we say to you: you are a man and not the LORD, for the LORD is the Creator of heaven and earth and the Creator of you also.

**11.** He brings the proud low and raises up the humble. He gives strength to the weak, and he gives weakness to the strong.

**12.** He gives death to the living, and he raises the dead who lie in the grave and have become dust.

**13.** He releases servants into life from the bondage of sin.

**14.** O King Tsiruts-Saydan, why do you boast in your unclean gods, which have no power?

**15.** The LORD made the heavens, established the earth, and created the great sea. He made the sun and the moon, and he appointed the seasons and ordered the years.

**16.** A man goes out to his work, labors and serves until evening; and the stars of heaven are established by his word.

**17.** He counts them all by their names, and nothing exists that the LORD does not know.

**18.** He commands the angels of heaven as they are sent forth, and they praise his holy name. Angels are sent everywhere on behalf of those who are to inherit life.

**19.** Raphael the angel was sent to Tobit, and he delivered Tobias in the land of Raguel.

**20.** An angel was sent to Gideon to instruct him how he should destroy the Philistines; and an angel was sent to Moses the prophet when he led Israel across the Red Sea.

**21.** For the LORD alone led them, and no strange god was with them.

**22.** He brought them up onto the heights of the land.

**23.** He fed them from the produce of the fields and nourished them with honey from the rock, because he loved them greatly.

**24.** He also made you king over his people and gave you authority over four kingdoms, so that you might guard the people, shepherd them rightly, and do the will of the one who created you.

**25.** He exalted you above others and made you king so that you might love the LORD your God, for he raised you from among all and appointed you to rule.

**26.** Therefore it is right that you should greatly love the LORD your God, just as he loved you and entrusted all his flock to you. Do the will of the LORD so that your days may be prolonged upon the earth and he may remain with you.

**27.** He will stand for you against all your enemies, establish you upon your throne, and spread the shadow of his wings over your head.

**28.** If you understood, you would know that the LORD chose you just as he chose Saul from among the children of Israel and made him king over Israel his people while he was tending his father’s donkeys; and Saul sat upon the throne with Samuel the prophet.

**29.** He gave Saul a great portion above his brothers and said to him, “Behold, from this time the LORD has made you king over his people, his great people.”

**30.** Therefore repay those who do good with good, and those who do evil with evil, for the LORD has appointed you over them with authority to put to death and to preserve alive.

**31.** Repay those who do good with good and those who do evil with evil, for the LORD has entrusted you with authority over what he has appointed to you, whether in chastening or in preserving alive, so that you may carry out his will.

**32.** Do the will of the LORD at every time and in every hour, so that he may accomplish your desire in all that you purpose and in all that you ask while you humble yourself before him; for you too are a servant of the one who rules everything above.

**33.** He who has no one to judge him judges all.

**34.** He whom no one appoints appoints all.

**35.** He whom no one can remove removes all.

**36.** He whom no one can set free sets all free.

**37.** He whom no one can compel causes all to serve, and no one can snatch anything from his hand, for the judgment of heaven and earth belongs to him.

**38.** Nothing is hidden before his eyes; everything is open before him.

**39.** He sees all, though there is none who sees him. He hears the prayer of the one who prays to him and receives that prayer, for he created mankind in his image and in his likeness.

**40.** He feeds all from his treasury and receives nothing in return, for he is the King who lives forever.
