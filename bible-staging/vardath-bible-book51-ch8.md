## 1 Meqabyan 8

**1.** Concerning the resurrection of the dead, listen and I will tell you. People plant a tree; it puts forth branches and shoots of the vine, and from it they make wine, for the LORD brings fruit from where there was nothing.

**2.** Look: the tree that you planted was small, yet it comes forth with branches, leaves, and fruit.

**3.** The LORD gives its roots drink from two sources, from water and from the earth.

**4.** What is above he nourishes from wind and sun; the root gives water to the leaves, and the earth gives strength to the tree.

**5.** The Spirit of the LORD gives fruit among them. So also is the resurrection of the dead.

**6.** He said, “From the four winds gather the souls—from water and earth, from the sun and from the wind—for each part went to its own place from which it came when it departed from the flesh.”

**7.** The earth remained in its own part and became dust, and the water remained in its own part and became dew.

**8.** The wind remained in its own part and became wind, and the fire remained in its own part and became the heat of the sun.

**9.** The spirit returned into the hand of its Creator, and he keeps it wherever he wills until the appointed time when he wills to bring it back.

**10.** He establishes the souls of the righteous in houses of light, but the souls of sinners he shuts in houses of darkness until the time when he wills to bring them forth.

**11.** He said to the prophet Ezekiel, “Call from the four winds, so that the souls may gather and become one spirit.”

**12.** When he spoke this with one word, they gathered from the four winds.

**13.** The water brought dew, and the sun brought heat.

**14.** The earth brought dust, and the wind brought breath.

**15.** The LORD brought the soul from the dwelling where it had been shut away, and at one word they were gathered together, and there was resurrection.

**16.** Again I will show you an example that is near you. Evening comes and you sleep; morning comes and you rise. When you sleep, you are as one dead.

**17.** When you awake, it is a likeness of resurrection. The night is a likeness of this world, whose whole condition is darkness, and all the world sleeps in it because the darkness of night covers them.

**18.** The light of morning is a likeness of the resurrection of the dead: darkness departs, light fills the whole world, and a person rises and goes out to his work.

**19.** So also is the renewal of the world and the resurrection of the dead, for this world passes away like the night.

**20.** When the sun comes and gives light, it is a likeness of the world, just as David said, “In the sun he set his dwelling.”

**21.** When he comes in the new world, just as the sun now shines when it rises, the LORD himself will shine over it in the new world, for he said, “A sun that does not set and a lamp that is not extinguished”; the LORD himself is its light.

**22.** Again he will raise the dead in the blink of an eye. I will also bring you another example from the food that you sow and by which you live: whether a grain of wheat, a grain of barley, a lentil, or any seed that a person sows in the earth, nothing lives unless it first dies.

**23.** When it dies, its body is consumed in the dust together with its covering, like the human body that you see.

**24.** After its body has been consumed by the earth, it sprouts to a certain measure … [The Geʽez source is incomplete here.]

**25.** See: if it had not died, it could not live; but when it germinates, it brings forth abundant grain.

**26.** The LORD gives fruit to that grain which has grown, and he clothes it with a body, wrapping it in its covering.

**27.** The leaf, blossom, stalk, and ear are not counted as the seed itself; rather, see how greatly the seed that you sowed has multiplied.

**28.** Do not be foolish. Look at your seed and see how it has multiplied. In the same way, consider the resurrection of the dead and how each one will be repaid according to his deeds.

**29.** Again listen and I will tell you: a seed does not change into another kind of seed. If you sow wheat, will it yield barley?

**30.** If you sow barley, will it become cumin? If you sow cumin, will it become flax? And among the trees, if you plant a fig tree, will it produce almonds? If you plant a nut tree, will it produce grapes?

**31.** If you plant sweet fruit, will it grow bitter? And if you plant a bitter tree, can it become sweet?

**32.** In the same way, if a sinner dies, can he become righteous at the resurrection? And if a righteous person dies, can he become a sinner at the resurrection? No. Each one will be repaid according to his deeds and according to the fruit of his work, and no one will be judged for the sin of another.

**33.** A cedar of Lebanon is planted and puts forth long and great branches; but if the dew of Hermon does not fall upon it, its leaves will not flourish, but will dry up and fall.

**34.** A tree is uprooted from its root if the rain of the rainy season does not descend upon it.

**35.** In the same way, if the dew of life from the LORD does not descend upon them, the dead will not rise.
