## 3 Meqabyan 9

**1.** Whether what is in heaven, or what is on earth, or what is of spirit, or what is of flesh, all belongs to him, and everything stands according to his order.

**2.** Nothing transgresses the boundary or commandment of the Creator of all. He traces the path of the eagle in heaven and directs its course wherever he wills.

**3.** He directs the path of the serpent upon the rocks, and the path of a ship in the midst of the sea; no one knows its course except the LORD alone.

**4.** And who knows the journey of a soul after it departs from its body except the LORD alone, whether it is the soul of a righteous person or the soul of a sinner?

**5.** Wherever it is carried—whether into the wilderness, or into the mountains, or like a bird, or like the dew of Hermon that descends upon the summit of the mountain—

**6.** Whether like a storm wind, or like lightning that makes straight its path—

**7.** Or like the stars that travel through the firmament, or like the sand that the waves of the sea cast into the deep—

**8.** Or like a stone of the horizon buried in the depths of the sea, or like a tree planted beside streams of water and giving good fruit—

**9.** Or like a tree of the sea that the heat of the sun dries, and the wind takes it away to another place where it neither grows nor is its trace found—

**10.** Or like the fading of a cloud whose trace cannot be found: who knows the way of the LORD? Who is his counselor? With whom did he take counsel?

**11.** Who can follow his track? For the thought of the LORD is hidden from the children of humankind.

**12.** No one can comprehend the wisdom and counsel of the LORD. He founded the earth upon the waters and established it without a support; by the work of his wisdom he set the heavens upon the wind and spread them out like a canopy over the waters.

**13.** He commanded the clouds to pour rain upon the earth, so that grass and innumerable fruits might spring up for the nourishment of the children of humankind, that we might rejoice together in the LORD.

**14.** All blessings, abundance, and fullness the LORD pours out upon the children of men, so that they may be satisfied and give thanks to the LORD who gives them from the fruit of the earth.

**15.** He clothes them with beautiful garments and gives every desirable blessing, delight, and abundance to those who do the will of the LORD.

**16.** In heaven also he gives them glory, favor, and joy, in the dwelling he has prepared for their fathers, for those who keep the commandment of the LORD.

**17.** Walk according to his judgment and his justice, and do not transgress the commandment of the one who preserves you, feeds you, sustains you, and exalts you, so that you may keep his law and seek his ordinance. I have seen upon the earth what the LORD does for those who love him: he weakens their enemies and preserves their souls.

**18.** He gives them all that they ask and does their will. Do not transgress the commandment of the LORD, but do the will of the LORD.

**19.** Do not stray from his law or from his commandment, lest he become angry with you, destroy you in wrath, and consume you in his anger, so that you are driven out from the land that was formerly the inheritance of your fathers and your dwelling becomes the fire of Gehenna, from which there is no escape forever.

**20.** Before your soul departs from your body, keep the will of the LORD your God, so that your deeds may be pleasing when you stand before the LORD.

**21.** For the kingdom of heaven and earth belongs to him alone; might and kingship belong to him, and mercy and compassion belong to him alone.

**22.** For he enriches and makes poor; he humbles and exalts.

**23.** David also prophesied concerning him, saying: “A man is like vanity, and his days pass like a shadow.”

**24.** “But you, O LORD, remain forever, and your remembrance from generation to generation.”

**25.** Again he said: “Your kingdom is a kingdom for all ages, and your dominion is from generation to generation.” You took the kingdom from Saul and returned it to David.

**26.** No one appoints you, and no one is able to see you fully; but you yourself see everything.

**27.** Your kingdom shall not be removed from generation to generation and forever and ever. There is no one who judges him; rather, he judges all and sees all.

**28.** He examines the heart and the inward parts, for he himself made man in his image and likeness, that man might be for his praise and know how to worship him without doubt, with an upright heart, and not become a worshiper of foreign gods, but of the LORD alone, who created, fed, sustained, and raised them.

**29.** But they refused to worship the one who created them and bowed down to wood and stone, to gold and silver, the work of human hands.

**30.** They sacrifice to them until the smoke of their sacrifice rises toward heaven, as though it could stand before the LORD, and they seek atonement from them for all the sins they commit in the worship of their idols.

**31.** They did not keep the commandment of the LORD that they had been taught, but learned to bow down to idols and to practice every unclean deed that is not pleasing: divination by stars, omens, idols, lusts, and everything that does not please the LORD.

**32.** They do these things in sin because they did not desire the praise of the LORD, by which their souls might be saved from lawlessness and sin and by which they might be commended before the LORD and his angels.

**33.** Their souls will stand naked when all those who slept in the dust rise together and their bones are joined again; the souls of the good will dwell in houses of light.

**34.** But the souls of sinners are in darkness. When the houses are opened, the bodies will rise and the souls will return into the bodies from which they had formerly departed.

**35.** They will stand naked before the LORD as they were born, and their deeds from their youth until that time will be revealed.

**36.** They will bear their sins upon their shoulders and will be repaid according to the deeds they have done, whether small or great.

## 3 Meqabyan 10

**1.** Listen, you who do not believe in the resurrection of the dead. As seeds rise without being born anew in the rainy season, so the Spirit of the LORD rests upon them as he breathed at the beginning.

**2.** By his word he first commanded them to die.

**3.** And according to his will they shall rise, though their flesh had decayed, and it shall be restored again.

**4.** Likewise, when rain descends and waters the earth, living things revive and walk again according to the order established at the beginning.

**5.** For it was said that every living creature that moves upon the earth, and every living soul that drinks water, has life because the Spirit of the LORD hovers over the waters; and through his Spirit and his word the spirit of life is given to them.

**6.** See, O blind of heart, you who say that the dead do not rise. By his Spirit and his word they come to be without mother or father. If there is knowledge and wisdom in you, how can you say that the dead will not rise by the word of the LORD their Creator?

**7.** Repent and return to your faith, for by the word of the LORD the dead who have become dust and earth within the ground shall live.

**8.** They shall rise by the dew of mercy that comes from the LORD, according to the word he spoke from the beginning. That word surrounds all things and raises the dead according to his will.

**9.** Know that you shall rise and stand before him. Do not imagine in your thought that you will remain in the grave.

**10.** It is not so. You shall rise and be repaid according to what you have done upon the earth, whether good or evil, for it is a day of recompense.

**11.** At the resurrection you shall be repaid for every sin you have committed, and the book of your debt, written from your youth until that time, shall be opened. You will have no excuse that the affairs of this world caused you to sin so that you might deny your sin.

**12.** Nor will you be able to present your false word as truth before you, or make the falsehood you spoke into righteousness.

**13.** There will be no excuse there, for the Spirit of the LORD will testify against you, since it knows every evil deed you have done and will reveal it to you before the LORD its Creator.

**14.** There you will be ashamed because of the sins you committed. Before that reaches you, hasten to practice repentance here, so that you may not be put to shame before angels and men on the day of judgment, but may be praised among those who are praised for their good deeds.

**15.** They shall receive their reward from the one who created them without shame, together with the angels who praise the LORD, and they shall rejoice. But if you have done no good in your life while you were in your body, you will have no portion with the righteous.

**16.** Your repentance will then be in a place where it cannot profit you. For here you should have acted while you possessed understanding: buying and selling, and while you possessed goods, feeding the hungry.

**17.** While you possessed clothing, you should have clothed the naked; while you possessed authority, you should have delivered the oppressed.

**18.** While you possessed knowledge, you should have instructed the sinner so that he might turn and repent and the LORD might forgive his sin. You yourself formerly sinned through ignorance; and while you possessed strength, you should have struggled against those who opposed you so that you might overcome.

**19.** While you possessed strength, you should have fasted and prayed to weaken the power of your bodily passions and subject yourself to righteousness rather than favor the flesh of this world.

**20.** The adornment of this world is not sweet foods and fine drinks, nor fine clothing or ornaments of gold and silver.

**21.** Nor is it precious stones, topaz, or emerald. These are not the adornment fitting for a person.

**22.** Rather, a person's adornment is purity, wisdom, understanding, pure love without jealousy, mercy without doubt or hatred, and loving your companion as your own soul.

**23.** Do not repay evil to the one who has done evil to you, so that you may enter the dwelling given to the meek and be rewarded with those who inherit the holy land through humility, understanding, discernment, and hope in the kingdom of heaven at the resurrection.

**24.** Do not say, “The dead will not rise.” Those who speak and think this have been anointed with the hope of the devil so that they may not be saved at the resurrection. They will know when it reaches them, and they will grieve and mourn because they did not understand what they had done, and because evil will be reckoned to them at the resurrection, since they did not believe that they would rise on that day.

**25.** Therefore they will be accused according to the evil deeds they committed upon the earth, and those who denied the resurrection of the body will behold it together.

**26.** Then they will weep because they did not make their way good. It would have been better for them to weep upon the earth, while it was still within their power, than to become mourners there.

**27.** For if we do not weep here of our own will, we shall weep there against our will. If we do not prepare repentance here, we prepare weeping and crying there, where it will not profit us.

**28.** Prepare good works, so that you may pass from death into life, from this passing earth to the heavenly realm above, and from this earthly place into the light that is in heaven.

**29.** Earthly abundance is great beyond measure, but there you shall rejoice with joy that has no end in the kingdom of heaven, together with those who believe in the resurrection of the dead, from now and forever and ever. Amen.
