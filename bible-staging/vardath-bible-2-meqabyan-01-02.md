# Book 52 — II and III Maccabees / 2–3 Meqabyan

*This book contains the distinct Ethiopic Second and Third Books of Meqabyan, not Western/Greek 2 or 3 Maccabees. Fresh Vardath English translations are prepared directly from the Geʽez texts in Beta maṣāḥǝft LIT5840SecondEthioMaccabees and LIT5839ThirdEthioMaccabees. The Beta maṣāḥǝft source transcriptions are licensed CC BY-SA 4.0; these translations derived from those transcriptions are provided under CC BY-SA 4.0. Source chapter and verse structures are retained, and damaged or uncertain readings are marked rather than silently supplied from a modern English translation.*

## 2 Meqabyan 1

**1.** This concerns how Meqabis came upon the Jews in Mesopotamia of Syria and slew them throughout their districts, from Jabbok as far as the precincts of Jerusalem, and devastated the holy land.

**2.** They had spread from Samaria as far as the precincts of Jerusalem and throughout all its regions, striking with the edge of the sword until only a few escaped. The Edomites, the Syrians, and the Amalekites had joined themselves to Meqabis the Moabite who devastated the land of Jerusalem.

**3.** When the children of Israel sinned, the LORD sent against them Meqabis the Moabite, and he struck them down with slaughter.

**4.** Because of this, the enemies of the LORD became bold against his holy land and gloried in their warfare.

**5.** The Edomites and the Philistines encamped there and began to take vengeance upon the land of the LORD, for he himself had sent them because the people had treated the word of the LORD with contempt.

**6.** Meqabis was from Ramoth of Moab. He arose from his land with strength and joined himself with those who were with him.

**7.** They encamped in the valleys of Gilead, from Mesopotamia as far as Syria, intending to destroy the land of the LORD. From there he sought the help of the Philistines and the Amalekites and gave them much gold, silver, horses, and chariots so that they would join him in battle.

**8.** They came together, assaulted the city, and poured out the blood of those who lived in it like water.

**9.** They made Jerusalem like the hut of a keeper in a field. Within it they committed filth, abomination, and every deed that does not please the LORD, and they defiled the land of the LORD which had been full of righteousness and holiness.

**10.** They made the corpses of your servants food for the birds of heaven and the flesh of your righteous ones food for the wild beasts of the wilderness.

**11.** They oppressed widows and orphans and tore the unborn from pregnant women, until the LORD who examines heart and inward parts was provoked to anger, for without fear of the LORD they acted according to what Satan had taught them.

**12.** They returned to their own territories rejoicing over the evil they had done to the people of the LORD, taking with them the captives and plunder they had carried from the holy land.

**13.** When they entered and returned to their dwellings, they made celebration, singing, and festivity.

## 2 Meqabyan 2

**1.** Thus the prophet Reʾay said to him: “For now rejoice only a little, for a brief hour of light. The LORD, the Holy One of Israel, is ready to take vengeance upon you in a way you have not imagined.

**2.** “You say, ‘My horses are swift, and therefore I will escape by flight.’

**3.** “But I tell you: swifter still will be those who pursue you, and you will not escape from the punishment and wrath of the LORD that are upon you.

**4.** “You say, ‘I will clothe myself in iron, and the shot of an arrow or the thrust of a spear will not overcome me.’ It will not be so. ‘I will bring an evil sickness into your heart,’ says the LORD, the Holy One of Israel, ‘worse than the shot of an arrow, the thrust of a spear, or wasting disease and affliction.

**5.** “I will bring an evil sickness into your heart. The one who would help you will disappear and you will not find him, for I have given you over to my wrath. You will not escape from my hand until I erase your memory from the earth.

**6.** “Then you will know that I am the LORD your Creator, when I do this thing swiftly, in the blink of an eye. You stiffened your neck and raised your head over my city, but before me you are like grass before the wind, devoured by fire before my eyes, and like dust swept from the face of the earth by winds and whirlwind.

**7.** “For I have given you over to my wrath because you did not know your Creator. I will not spare anything that is yours, nor leave one who can bear witness throughout your territories.

**8.** “But even now, repent from all the evil you have done. If you repent and humble yourself before the LORD with tears and weeping, and petition him with a pure heart, the LORD will forgive your sin and all in which you have offended before him.”

**9.** At that time Meqabis wept before the LORD because of his sin and covered himself with dust, for the LORD was angry with him.

**10.** He understood that the LORD would not disregard the evil he had spoken through the mouth of his prophet, for his eyes are watchful and do not sleep, his ears are open and do not grow deaf, and he does not make false the word he has spoken but performs it in a single moment, like the blink of an eye.

**11.** Meqabis cast off his garments, clothed himself in hairy sackcloth, placed dust upon his head, and wept and cried before the LORD his God because of the evil he had done.
