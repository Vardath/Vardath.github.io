## 2 Meqabyan 3

**1.** The prophet spoke, for Ramoth of Moab was near Syria.

**2.** Meqabis dug into the earth and entered the pit up to his neck. He wept with bitter weeping and repented for the sin he had committed before the LORD.

**3.** Then the LORD said to the prophet, “Return from the land of Judah to the land of Ramoth, where Meqabis the ruler of Moab is, and say to him: Thus says the LORD: Do not say that you did this by yourself, nor that by the strength of your power and your might you destroyed the holy city. I am the LORD, your ruler; by my own will I sent you to destroy my city,

**4.** “because it provoked me by all its greed, fornication, and violence.

**5.** “I rejected it and handed it into your hand. Therefore do not stiffen your neck and say, ‘By the strength of my hand I subdued the city.’ And now, for the sake of the children whom you have begotten, the LORD has passed over your sin.

**6.** “Now repent with all your heart. Be steadfast and do not be double-minded, for the double-minded do not stand firm in repentance.

**7.** “Blessed are those who repent with all their heart and do not return to fornication and sin after repenting of their evil.

**8.** “Let them persevere with weeping, tears, much supplication, and prostration before the LORD who created them. For he says to those who repent: ‘After you went astray and repented, you are mine.’

**9.** “And to the proud one who returns to him in repentance after going astray he says: ‘Because of your trembling and your fear, I pass over your sin. For I am the LORD your God, who visits the sin of the fathers upon the children to the third and fourth generation; but to those who love me and keep my commandment, I show mercy to … generations.’” *The numeral before “generations” is damaged in the digital Geʽez source and is not silently reconstructed here.*

**10.** “And now, because of these children whom you have begotten, I will keep my covenant with you and receive the repentance you have made concerning your evil deeds,” says the LORD, the Holy One of Israel, who rules over all.

**11.** At that time Meqabis came up out of the pit and bowed before the prophet, saying, “May the LORD do with me according to all that you have spoken. Let him not cast me away from you, but let him make me according to what you desire, for I have provoked the LORD and have not walked in his commandment as my fathers did. We had no law, and you yourself know that we worshiped idols as our fathers taught us.”

**12.** “Until now I did not hear the word of the LORD’s prophets. I am a sinner who walked in my sin, in the pride of my heart and the stiffness of my neck. I provoked the commandment of the LORD and did not walk in the commandments and judgments he commanded me.”

**13.** The prophet answered him and said, “I know that now your repentance has been accepted, for none among your kindred before you confessed his sin.

**14.** “From this time onward, abandon the worship of idols and return to the knowledge of the LORD, so that your repentance may be true.” Meqabis bowed at the feet of the prophet. The prophet raised him up and instructed him in all that was fitting for good conduct.

**15.** Meqabis did according to what the LORD had commanded him, and he returned to his house.

**16.** Meqabis set his life in order and removed from his house all the idols, images, cult objects, and carved figures.

**17.** He questioned the Jewish captives whom he had brought from Jerusalem, evening and morning, concerning all the commandments of the LORD, his law and his ordinances, and how their fathers had practiced them.

**18.** He appointed some of the wise men from among the captives within his household.

**19.** He also appointed wise young men as attendants in his chamber, to watch over his young sons and their companions and teach them the way of the LORD, according to the law, ordinances, judgments, justice, and prohibitions observed by the children of Israel. Thus the Moabites heard these things from the captives.

**20.** He abolished their forbidden cult practices, all their images and idols, their ritual vessels and sacrifices, the offerings from their herds, and the slaughter of animals that they had offered to their gods morning and evening.

**21.** For they had sacrificed to them and worshiped them at every work, time, and hour; they petitioned them, trusted in them, and did whatever those false gods told them.

**22.** They did not turn aside from their words in anything they told them, because it seemed to them that those gods had saved them.

**23.** But Meqabis abandoned their way.

**24.** After he heard the words spoken by the prophet Reʾay, he made his way good through repentance, and all his household made their ways good. He had learned from the children of Israel in those days: at one time they provoked the LORD, and when he chastened them they knew him and cried out to the LORD.

**25.** When they saw themselves humbled beneath the hands of those who afflicted them, they cried before him. Then he had mercy upon them, remembering the covenant of their fathers when they cried to him because of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob their fathers.

**26.** But after he delivered them, they forgot the one who had delivered them and returned to the worship of idols.

**27.** Then he sent nations against them to afflict them. When they oppressed and tormented them, they cried out to the LORD; at that time he had mercy and compassion on them because they were the work of his hands.

**28.** Again, when relief came to them, they returned to provoking him through the worship of the idols around them and through the evil works of their hands.

**29.** Then he raised against them the peoples of the Philistines, Moab, Midian, Syria, and Egypt. They wept and cried when their enemies overcame, afflicted, enslaved, and subjected them; and he raised up judges for them so that the judges might deliver them.

## 2 Meqabyan 4

**1.** There was a deliverer in the days of Hosea,

**2.** another in the days of Gideon,

**3.** and others in the days of Samson, Barak, Deborah, and Judith. Whether through women or through men, he raised up judges for them to deliver them from those who afflicted them and from the hand of all their enemies.

**4.** According to his will, the LORD delivered them from those who oppressed them.

**5.** They rejoiced greatly in all the good he gave them: in the produce of their land, the multiplication of their animals, and all the offspring in field and wilderness.

**6.** He blessed their animals and their plants, and everything they possessed at home and in the wilderness. He did not diminish their livestock, for his eyes watched over them and he loved them greatly because they were the generation of the righteous.

**7.** But when they themselves became evil in their deeds, he handed them over into the hands of their enemies.

**8.** When their enemies began to kill them, then they sought him, returned, and went to the LORD their Creator.

**9.** When they returned with all their heart, he forgave them and did not remember their former sins, for he knew that they were flesh and blood and that the mind is tempted by this world and by demons.

**10.** When Meqabis heard this account which the LORD had established upon the mountain of his sanctuary, he struggled earnestly in repentance.

**11.** He did not turn away from what he had seen and heard concerning all the goodness that the LORD showed the children of Israel: when they transgressed the commandment and the LORD chastened them, they wept and cried out; then he again showed them compassion, and they kept his commandment.

**12.** In the same way Meqabis made his ways straight, kept the commandment, and walked according to the commandment of the LORD, the God of Israel.

**13.** From what he had heard, and from all the things in which the children of Israel boasted, he too boasted in keeping the commandment of the LORD.

**14.** He ordered his household and his children to walk in all the paths and commandments of the LORD.

**15.** The prohibitions observed by the Jews he also observed, and the commandments kept by the Jews he practiced according to what he had heard. He also abstained from the foods from which the Jews abstained, although he himself was of another people, a Moabite.

**16.** He gave tithes, the firstborn of his sheep, his cattle, and his donkeys, and from everything he acquired he gave. He also offered sacrifices as the Jews did, turning his face toward Jerusalem.

**17.** He offered sacrifices for vows, for sin, freewill offerings, offerings of salvation, and continual offerings.

**18.** He also presented firstfruits and drink offerings as the Jews did, gave them to the Levite whom he had appointed, and burned incense.

**19.** He made sacred furnishings—vessels, stands, tables, staffs, basins, lamps, and curtains—so that they might be used among the holy things of Israel if the LORD was pleased with him.

**20.** Because the children of Israel had lived according to his law and his ordinance, the LORD had not rejected them or delivered them into the hands of their enemies. In the same way Meqabis made all his ways good.

**21.** At all times he prayed to the LORD, the God of Israel, that he would be his guide and would not separate him from the congregation of Israel, whom he had chosen and who did his will.

**22.** Again he prayed that the LORD would give him seed in Zion and houses in Jerusalem, save him from the punishment spoken through the prophet, and receive all his repentance.

**23.** He wept with tears before the LORD, praying that his seed would not be destroyed from the earth and that the LORD would guard him in his going out and his coming in.

**24.** The people of Moab who were under the hand of Meqabis marveled, so that they began to believe that the path in which their ruler walked was upright. They examined justice and compared the ways of their own land and people, and they saw that the way of Meqabis was more upright and far better.

**25.** They came to him and heard the justice, righteousness, and goodness of Meqabis.

**26.** He possessed much wealth: servants and maidservants, donkeys and camels, and horses clothed in armor of iron.

**27.** From the time he worshiped the LORD, whenever he went into battle there was none who defeated him; rather, he was the one who prevailed.

**28.** His enemies came against him trusting in the power of their gods and cursed him in the names of their gods, but none could overcome him, because he had placed his trust in the LORD his God.

**29.** He continued in this way, overcoming his enemies and bringing nations under his hand.

**30.** He took vengeance on behalf of the oppressed and established justice for the orphan.

**31.** He received widows and orphans in their affliction, fed the hungry from his provisions, and clothed the naked from his garments.

**32.** He rejoiced in his works and in the labor of his hands. He gave freely from what he possessed without withholding, and he gave tithes to the house of the LORD. Doing these things, Meqabis came to rest in goodness.
