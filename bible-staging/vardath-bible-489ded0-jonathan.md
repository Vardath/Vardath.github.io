### Jonathan son of Mattathias

After Judah's life ended, the enemies renewed their wars against the Jews.

Jonathan went with a small company toward the Jordan and remained there.

Bacchides followed him.

When Jonathan saw him approaching, he crossed to the other side of the river and fortified himself there.

Bacchides came against him with his army and besieged him.

When the siege became severe, Jonathan and those with him went out by night against the army of Bacchides.

The army of Bacchides was thrown into confusion, and God put fear into their hearts, so they fled.

Bacchides also fled.

Jonathan pursued him, overtook him, and was about to kill him.

Bacchides asked Jonathan to spare him.

He swore that he would never again make war against Jonathan, that he would release all the Jews held captive by his men, and that he would treat them well.

Jonathan released him.

Bacchides kept his word.

He released the captives and treated them kindly.

After a short time Jonathan died, and his brother Simon took the leadership after him.
