## 1 Meqabyan 7

**1.** Whether you are a king or whether you are a judge, what are you? Is it not the LORD who created you?

**2.** He brought you forth from where you did not exist so that you might do his will, walk in his commandment, and fear his judgment. Just as you punish your servants and have authority over them, so there is One who can punish you and who has authority over you.

**3.** Just as you strike without mercy those who commit offenses, so the LORD is able to strike you and cast you into judgment, from which there is no escape forever.

**4.** Just as you imprison the one who will not humble himself before you and will not bring you tribute, why do you not bring tribute to the LORD?

**5.** Just as you desire that others should fear you, why do you not fear the LORD your God? He created you and made you ruler over his works so that you might shepherd his people and his flock in righteousness.

**6.** Do not show partiality to the great or to the small, but judge in righteousness and justice, as the LORD appointed you. Whom should you fear besides him? Keep his ways and his commandment.

**7.** Do not turn aside either to the right or to the left, just as Moses commanded the children of Israel and said to them, “I have set before you fire and water; stretch out your hand toward whichever you choose.”

**8.** Hear the voice of the One who established you, so that you may obey his voice and carry out his commandment. Do not say, “The counsel is beyond the river; who will bring it to me so that I may see it, hear its word, and perform the commandment of the LORD?”

**9.** Nor say again, “Who will ascend for me into heaven and bring down to me the word of the LORD so that I may hear it and do it?” For he said, “Behold, the word is near you, in your mouth, and behold, it is in your hand.”

**10.** If you do not listen to the Scripture, you have not listened to the LORD your God; and if you do not keep his word, you do not love him or perform his commandment.

**11.** You will enter eternal judgment if you do not love his commandment and do not perform the will of the One who exalted you and honored you above all your people so that you might shepherd them in righteousness.

**12.** He placed you above and made you head over all his people, while you remember the name of your God who created you and gave you authority to judge the people in righteousness and justice.

**13.** When people sin against you, correct them and teach them while remembering the way of the LORD, ordering your own mind rightly and judging with justice.

**14.** When people contend before you, do not show partiality. Do not accept influence from one man so that you pardon him while you oppress an innocent man, as though the whole land belonged to you.

**15.** If you do his will, he will prolong your days.

**16.** Remember that you will rise from the dead and stand before his face, and you will be questioned concerning everything you have done, whether evil or good.

**17.** If you have done good, you will dwell among the good kings in the houses of light; but if you have done evil, you will dwell in the habitation of wicked kings, and your former honor will not cause the LORD to spare you.

**18.** When you see the greatness of your majesty, the multitude of those who serve you, the wealth of your fields, your horses and the army under your hand, and those who blow the horn and play the lyre before you—

**19.** when you see all these things, you lift up your mind and stiffen your neck, and you do not remember the One who gave you all this honor. But when he says to you, “Leave it,” you also will leave it.

**20.** Your honor will be given to another because you disobeyed the One who appointed you.

**21.** There he will search you, examine you, and judge you, because death comes upon you suddenly; and at the resurrection there will be justice, and the deeds of every person will be questioned.

**22.** There will be no one to honor the kings of the earth, and the crown of pride will fall from the rulers of the earth, for rich and poor will stand together in judgment and justice before the righteous Judge.

**23.** At the judgment the soul will tremble, the books will be opened, and the deeds of mankind will be revealed; nothing will remain covered.

**24.** At the resurrection of the dead, the earth will give back what was placed in her womb, just as a woman cannot close her womb when the time of birth has come and cannot prevent what is within her from coming forth.

**25.** Can the clouds hold back the rain so that it does not go where the LORD commands it to fall? In the same way it is impossible for the dead not to rise when the time of resurrection has come, for the word of the LORD brought all things out of nothing, and the word of the LORD also brought all down into the depths.

**26.** And again the word of the LORD will bring all forth from the depths, just as Moses said, “Man does not live by bread alone, but by every word that comes from the mouth of the LORD.”

**27.** Behold, therefore, it is known that by the word of the LORD the dead will live.

**28.** Again it is said in Deuteronomy concerning those who become kings and rulers and who act wickedly: “Vengeance belongs to me; I will repay them. In due time their foot will slip, for the day of their end is near.”

**29.** Again the LORD says to those who do not understand his judgment: “Know, know that I am the LORD. I kill, and I make alive.

**30.** “I wound, and I heal. I bring down into Sheol, and again I raise up toward heaven, and there is no one who can deliver from my hand.”

**31.** Thus the LORD spoke concerning kings and rulers who do not keep his commandment: “Keep my law and my ordinances so that you may enter my eternal kingdom, for the kingdom that is upon the earth passes away and departs from morning until evening.”

**32.** For the cup of the LORD is for both honor and humiliation: this one he humbles, and that one he honors.

**33.** The one who does not do his will he removes, and the one who does his will he establishes.
