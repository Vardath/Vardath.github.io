### The Cleansing and Dedication of the Temple

After the victories God had given them, Judah son of Mattathias went up to Jerusalem with the company of the faithful.

They tore down the unlawful altars that had been built there and removed the defilement that had been brought into the holy place.

They cleansed the house of God and built a new altar for sacrifice.

They arranged the sacrifice and the wood upon the altar, but the sacred fire was no longer to be found.

Then they cried to God in prayer.

Fire came forth from a stone upon the altar, and they kindled the wood with it.

The service of the sanctuary was restored.

On the twenty-fifth day of Kislev they dedicated the altar.

They restored the bread to its place, kindled the lamps, and praised the LORD.

For eight days they celebrated the dedication with thanksgiving and songs of praise.

Thus the sanctuary that had been defiled under Antiochus was cleansed and dedicated again, and the people rejoiced that the worship of God had been restored.
