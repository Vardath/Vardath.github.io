## 2 Meqabyan 11

**1.** Remember Moses, the man of God, in his prayer and his humility. Though he saw how great the people were, he did not become angry and did not seek their destruction. Even for those who reviled him, whom the LORD had determined to destroy, he interceded before the LORD in gentleness, saying, “Have mercy, O LORD, upon your people, and do not reject them, for your people have sinned before you.

**2.** “And upon me, your sinful servant, have compassion, for I have sinned before you. Forgive them also, for you are merciful and compassionate.”

**3.** In this way he interceded for those who reviled him.

**4.** Because of this he was called meek.

**5.** The LORD loved him exceedingly above his Levitical brethren and made him an image of the LORD toward them, for through him he appointed the priests.

**6.** But the children of Korah who transgressed were swallowed beneath the earth and went down alive into Sheol, soul and body, together with their tents, their animals, and all that belonged to them. Every word Moses spoke came to pass for him as though spoken by the LORD, because he loved the LORD his God and did not turn aside from his commandment.

**7.** In the same way, if you do not transgress the commandment of the LORD, the LORD will do your will for you, honor your word, and preserve your kingdom.

**8.** The children of Korah and Asaph transgressed the commandment of Moses and murmured against him because he had said to them, “Make your souls upright before the LORD.”

**9.** They said to him, “Are we not also your children of Levi, who perform the work of priesthood in the Tent of Meeting?”

**10.** They took their censers so that they might burn incense. They went and offered incense, but the LORD did not receive their prayer. By the fire of their own censers they were burned and melted like wax consumed by flame. Nothing remained of them—not their bones or their bodies—except the bronze censers, which were brought into the house of the LORD by his command. For he said, “Their censers have been made holy by the burning of their flesh.” *The digital source places the continuation of this sentence on a stray line numbered 1; it is restored here to verse 10.*

**11.** Therefore the LORD said to Moses and Aaron, “Gather their censers into the tabernacle, and let them be made into work for my house according to all that I have ordained for it, from the outside to the inside.”

**12.** They beautified the work of the holy tabernacle with its bowls, vessels, cherubim, basins, and great receptacles,

**13.** its utensils, serving vessels, fittings, the enclosure around the tabernacle, its outer courts, and the altar on which they offered within the Tent of Meeting,

**14.** with freewill offerings, offerings of salvation, offerings of vows, offerings for sin, and the sacrifices prescribed by the law.

**15.** Everything the LORD commanded Moses concerning the Tent of Meeting, he commanded them to perform there.

**16.** They did not turn aside from serving their God, so that his name might be praised over them in the tabernacle of the law of the LORD their God, who had promised to give them the inheritance of their fathers, the land flowing with milk and honey which he had sworn to Abraham.

**17.** He established the covenant for Isaac and confirmed the oath for Jacob,

**18.** and for Moses and Aaron in the Tent of Witness.

**19.** And for Samuel and Elijah in Jerusalem, in the tabernacle and in the house of the sanctuary where Solomon built, so that it might be the house of the LORD, the resting place of the name of the Most High, and the dwelling of the Holy One of Israel.

**20.** For there is the place of petition and the forgiveness of sin, where he reveals himself to his priests who walk in gentleness.

**21.** There he hears the prayer of those who do his will.

**22.** There the law of the LORD, the Holy One of Israel, is established.

**23.** There the continual sacrifice and incense of sweet fragrance are offered to the LORD, the Holy One of Israel.

**24.** He speaks above the mercy seat in the Tent of Meeting and reveals his glory to his chosen ones, the children of Jacob, and to his holy ones who walk in the commandment and judgment of the LORD.

**25.** But those who rebel will become like the children of Korah whom the earth swallowed. In the same way sinners will be cast into the judgment of burning fire from which there is no escape forever.

## 2 Meqabyan 12

**1.** Woe to you, mighty men of Israel, who did not keep what was commanded you in the tabernacle and did not do the will of the LORD, but only the desire of your own selves: pride and arrogance, fornication and greed, drunkenness and drinking, and swearing falsely.

**2.** “Because of this my wrath will burn against you like straw before fire, like fire that burns mountains, and like a whirlwind that lifts dry chaff from the earth and scatters it into the air until no trace of its former place can be found.

**3.** “In the same way I will destroy all who practice evil before my face,” says the LORD, the Holy One of Israel. “Remember the LORD who rules over all, for nothing is impossible for him.

**4.** “He loves those who love him, forgives the sins and transgressions of those who walk in his commandment, and does not desire that you remain senseless and hardened in heart forever.

**5.** “Make your souls upright toward the LORD and cling to him, so that you may save your souls and he may deliver you from the hand of your enemy on the day of your affliction.

**6.** “When you call to me I will say to you, ‘Here I am; I am with you.’ I will deliver you from the hand of your enemy and will not forsake you on the day of your affliction, because you trusted in me, performed my commandment, did not transgress my law, and loved what I love,” says the LORD who rules over all.

**7.** He loves those who love him, and those who keep his commandment he receives, for he is merciful and compassionate.

**8.** He turns back the fullness of his wrath and does not burn them with all his punishment, because he is merciful. He knows that they are flesh and blood, and after their souls go out from their bodies they return to their earth.

**9.** They do not know where they are until the LORD wills to bring them forth from where they are not, for he created them from where they did not exist. He is the one who took their souls and returned their bodies to the earth, their inheritance.

**10.** Again he will bring them from nonbeing into being according to his will.

**11.** But Ṣiruṣaydan, the lawless one against the LORD, increased his pride before the LORD. When the LORD left him until the appointed time, he exalted himself greatly.

**12.** He said, “My days have become like the days of heaven. I am the one who causes the sun to rise, and I will not die forever.”

**13.** Before he finished speaking this word, the angel of death whose name was Ṭəlmyāqos came down and struck his heart. He died at once and was cut off from the beauty of his possessions, the greatness of his pride, and his evil deeds, because he had not given thanks to his Creator.

**14.** The army of the king of the Chaldeans was outside his city, and hostile forces were seeking to fight him. When he died, they came in, destroyed his country, plundered all his animals, and left nothing remaining.

**15.** They plundered all his possessions, took away the spoil, burned his city with fire, and returned to their own land.
