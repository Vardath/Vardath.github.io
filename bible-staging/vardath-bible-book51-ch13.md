## 1 Meqabyan 13

**1.** Woe now to Tyre and Sidon and to all the districts of the land of Judah that lift up their heads, for when I seize them in my wrath they will be humbled and punished.

**2.** The LORD says, “From among them will come forth an arrogant and proud son of perdition, an enemy of righteousness, one who stiffens his neck, boasts, and does not know his Creator. I have made him an example of my wrath so that I may reveal my power upon him,” says the LORD who rules all.

**3.** Capernaum, Samaria and Galilee, Syria and Damascus, Cyprus and Achaia, and all the nations of the district of the Jordan are stiff-necked. They walk according to their own ways, and darkness and the shadow of death cover them. Seblanios has covered their hearts, and they do not return to the fear of the LORD, being subject to that proud one.

**4.** Woe at that time to those who obey the unclean and offer sacrifices in their names, for they rebel against the LORD who created them and become like animals without understanding. That wicked son of perdition sets up his image in every place and delights in the work of his hands, in the desire of his soul, and in every violence, strife, rebellion, deceit, fornication, and falsehood.

**5.** In his days he will be known by doing these things, for it has been appointed before the LORD that he should do them.

**6.** The sun will be darkened, the moon will become blood, and the stars will fall from heaven. Every work will be shaken by the sign that comes in the latter days, when the earth and all that is in it are judged according to the works of those who dwell upon it.

**7.** The last enemy, death, will be abolished, for it exalted itself over the creation of the LORD and did as it wished for one hour.

**8.** After this it will have no authority, for “I will take vengeance,” says the LORD who rules all.

**9.** He too will descend into the punishment and judgment prepared for him from the day when he is seized in my wrath, for he draws all who are with him into destruction, punishment, and corruption. I am the one who brings out and who brings into the judgment of Gehenna.

**10.** Let the mighty not boast in their might, for the LORD gives strength and firmness to the weak, and again gives weakness to the strong and mighty.

**11.** He will repay vengeance for widows and orphans, for he is ruler and avenger for those who are oppressed by the hands of their oppressors.

**12.** Woe to you who boast and stiffen your neck, thinking that I will not seize you or avenge myself upon you in my wrath. As it was said, “Why has the morning star, which rises at dawn, fallen from heaven?”

**13.** For in his pride and boasting he said, “I will set my throne above the heavens and the stars, and I will become like the Most High.”

**14.** You dared this in your pride and did not remember the LORD who created you and formed you with his hands. Why did you exalt yourself, only to descend to the lowest depths because of the hardness of your heart?

**15.** By your pride you were humbled beneath all the angels who were like you, for they praise their Creator with humility of spirit and do not depart from his commandment. They know that he made and created them, and they guard their minds so that they do not transgress or turn aside from his commandment.

**16.** But you did evil in the pride of your heart and became despised among your companions. You became the keeper of every rebellion, sin, deception, and strife in which sinners like you walk. The wicked who are of your kind rebel as you did and walk according to your will and the commandment by which you teach them error.

**17.** Woe to you and to those whom you deceived by your counsel, for together you will descend into corruption.

**18.** And woe to you, children of the LORD, whom that deceiver led astray. Together you will descend into everlasting punishment, for you were led astray by what he and his demons taught you.

**19.** Formerly, while Moses the servant of the LORD was still among you, you provoked him at Horeb, at the waters of strife, at Mount Sinai, and at Amalek.

**20.** Again, when you sent scouts into the land of inheritance and they told you, “The road is far, their walls are strong and reach to heaven, their fortresses are great, and mighty warriors dwell there,” you were troubled by their words and rebelled against the word of the LORD, wishing to return to the land of Egypt and to bitter bondage.

**21.** You did not remember the LORD who delivered you from your affliction, who did great things in Egypt and led you by the hand of his angel. So that the sun would not burn you, a cloud shaded you by day; and so that your feet would not stumble in darkness, a pillar of fire gave you light by night.

**22.** When Pharaoh and his army terrified you, you cried aloud to Moses, and Moses cried aloud to the LORD. By his angel he guarded you so that you would not meet the army of Pharaoh.

**23.** But he turned them back into suffering, for the LORD said, “He alone guided them, and no foreign god was with them.” Their enemies he covered in the sea in a single moment, and not one fugitive remained among them.

**24.** Israel he caused to pass on foot through the midst of the sea, and no evil from the Egyptians reached them. He brought them to Mount Sinai, and there he fed them with manna for forty years.

**25.** Yet they neglected the worship of the LORD after he had done so many good things for them, for the children of Israel continually provoked the LORD. In the Book of the Generations of the Fathers it is said, “The heart of the children of men is dust, and all their works run toward strife and evil. None among them loves uprightness, but they gather possessions by violence, swear falsely, wrong their neighbor, steal, and contend.”

**26.** Toward this evil they run throughout all the days of their lives. Thus the children of Israel greatly provoked the LORD, breaking his commandments from the beginning to the end.
