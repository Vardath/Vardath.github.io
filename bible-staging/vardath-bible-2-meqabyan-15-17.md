## 2 Meqabyan 15

**1.** Those who excelled in good deeds will rejoice at that time. But those who were negligent, saying, “The dead will not rise,” will grieve when they see that the dead have risen together with the evil deeds from which there is no escape.

**2.** Their own deeds will judge them. No one will need to accuse them, for they themselves will know how they are condemned.

**3.** They will stand in the place of the wicked on the day of weeping and judgment, on the day of decision and on the day of the LORD.

**4.** It is a day of darkness, a day of gloom, a day of cloud, a day of thick darkness, and a day of lightning.

**5.** It is a day of fear and trembling, a day of thunder and storm, a day of terrors.

**6.** It is the day when the evil one is repaid according to the evil he has done, the pure according to the purity he has practiced, and the defiled according to the defilement he has committed.

**7.** On that day a master will not be honored above a servant, nor a mistress above a maidservant.

**8.** A king will not be honored above a poor man, nor a governor above a child, nor a father above his son, nor a mother above her daughter.

**9.** A rich man will not be honored above a poor man, nor the proud above the humble, nor the elder above the younger, for it is the day of justice, the day of recompense and judgment, the day on which everyone will be repaid according to what he has done.

**10.** It is the day on which those who did good will be rewarded and those who did evil will receive the recompense of their evil.

**11.** It is the day when those who are rewarded for good will rejoice and those who are repaid for evil will grieve. Those who falsified the Scriptures, saying, “The dead will not rise,” will see it.

**12.** Then all the sinners of the earth will weep because of the sins they committed and because sorrow without consolation has overtaken them, for they did not do good upon the earth.

**13.** But the joy of the good who did what is good will never come to an end, forever and ever, because while they were upon the earth they were diligent in doing good.

**14.** They did not transgress the commandment of their Creator, for they knew that they would rise from the dead.

**15.** Because they did not depart from his commandment, they will inherit—

**16.** and in heaven he will cause them to inherit the land of life which he swore to their fathers, on the day of resurrection when earthly riches are burned away.

**17.** The workers of evil who did not believe in the resurrection of the dead or in the law of the LORD, and who did not remember the day of resurrection, will cry out.

**18.** Then they will see the evil that overtakes them, which has no end and no healing and no consolation. Within their hearts there will be sorrow without consolation and no rest.

**19.** There the worm does not sleep and the fire is not quenched.

**20.** Outside their bodies are fire and smoke, the wind of the whirlwind, storm, hail, and thunder raining down upon them.

**21.** Burning fire is for those who did not believe in the resurrection of the dead.

## 2 Meqabyan 16

**1.** Consider yourself and what is in your own body: the nails of your hands and feet and the hair of your head. From time to time you cut them, yet they grow again and come forth. By this understand; and if you have understanding and knowledge, believe.

**2.** You may ask, “From where do these nails of my hands and feet and the hair of my head come?” Is it not the LORD who appointed the places from which they grow, so that you may know that the resurrection of the dead will be of your own flesh and not in some other flesh, and so that you may know for yourself that you will rise from the dead?

**3.** You will be repaid according to the deeds you have done, your offenses and your sins, at the resurrection of the dead, because you misled the people by saying, “There is no resurrection of the dead.”

**4.** Even now you may see the likeness of it before the day of your recompense reaches you: what you sow does not grow up as some other thing, whether it is grain or wheat.

**5.** Likewise, a tree that you plant does not change its growth. If it is a vine or a fig tree, its leaves and its fruit do not change into another kind.

**6.** If you plant a vine, it does not change into a fig tree. If you plant a fig tree, it does not change into a vine. If you sow wheat, it does not change into barley.

**7.** If you sow barley, it does not change into wheat. Everything according to its seed, its kind, its fruit, its tree, its leaf, and its form receives blessing through the dew of mercy from the LORD and brings forth fruit.

**8.** So also the earth will bring forth what the LORD has sown. It will cause the soul and body of those who did good to spring up without changing them into those who did evil, and those who did evil will not be changed into those who did good. What the LORD has sown will spring up in soul and body.

**9.** When the time for the blast of the horn arrives, the dead will rise through the dew of mercy from the LORD. Those who did good will rise into the resurrection of life, and their reward will be the Paradise of joy, the dwelling of the pure which he has prepared for the good, where there is no disease or sickness and where they will never die again.

**10.** But those who did evil will rise into the resurrection of judgment with the Devil who deceived them,

**11.** and with his evil demons, who do not desire that any of the children of men should be saved, into everlasting judgment.

**12.** They will go down below, into the extremity of darkness, where there is weeping and gnashing of teeth, where there is no mercy or compassion and no escape forever and ever, because they did no good during their life upon the earth while they were in their bodies.

**13.** Therefore they will be judged at the resurrection in soul and body.

**14.** As for those who do not believe in the resurrection of soul and body together, there the LORD will show the greatness of his sign.

**15.** Each one will be repaid according to his deeds and according to the labor of his hands. *The digital Geʽez source splits this sentence across a stray line numbered 1 after verse 15; the two fragments are restored here as one verse.*

## 2 Meqabyan 17

**1.** A grain of wheat, unless it dies, does not sprout and does not bear fruit. But if the grain of wheat dies, it makes a root in the earth, sends forth leaves, becomes a stalk, and bears fruit, as you yourselves know.

**2.** From one grain of wheat there come to be many grains.

**3.** In the same way this seed grows by wind, water, earth, and the sun in the place of fire, for without the sun wheat cannot bear fruit.

**4.** The wind likewise takes the place of breath, for without wind wheat cannot bear fruit; and there is also the water that waters and moistens the earth.

**5.** When the dust and earth have been moistened, the seed makes a root, rises upward, and bears fruit according to the measure with which the LORD has blessed it.

**6.** The grain of wheat is like Adam, upon whom a speaking spirit from the LORD was breathed. The vine likewise is watered with water, makes roots, and draws water through its fine roots.

**7.** It raises its growth upward into a crown of leaves, for the dew of mercy from the LORD washes it among its long branches. It ripens in the heat of the sun and makes fruit from the LORD.

**8.** It produces a good fragrance that gladdens the heart. When its fruit is eaten it satisfies, as water and bread satisfy hunger and thirst; and when it is pressed it produces the blood of the grape.

**9.** When people drink it, it gladdens the human heart, as it is said in the Psalm, “Wine gladdens the heart of man.” A man who drinks freely becomes drunk when he fills his mouth, swallows, fills his belly, and the wine enters his blood and his heart.

**10.** It can make him senseless, for drunkenness from wine can make a person exceedingly foolish. It makes cliffs and ravines seem like level ground to him, and he no longer notices the ditch or the thorns beneath his hands and feet.

**11.** Thus the LORD has acted through the vine and its fruit so that his name may be praised by those who do his will and by those who believe in the resurrection of the dead.

**12.** He will make those who believe in the resurrection of the dead rejoice in the dwelling of life.
