## 1 Meqabyan 22

**1.** Remember the names of those who do what is good, and do not forget their deeds.

**2.** Strive so that your name may become like theirs, so that in heaven you may rejoice with them in the dwelling of light prepared for good kings and rulers who did the will of the LORD.

**3.** Again, learn and inquire about the names of wicked kings and rulers—how they are condemned and spoken against among people after their death.

**4.** In heaven also they are judged with the wicked and lawless who did not do the will of the LORD; though they saw and heard, they did not make their way good.

**5.** Therefore do not walk in the way of the wicked who angered the LORD by their evil deeds, but be good, gentle, and upright.

**6.** Judge with righteousness. Vindicate the widow and the orphan, and deliver them from the hand of sinners who oppress them.

**7.** Stand for the orphan as a father, so that you may deliver him from the hand of the one who oppresses him. Tremble when the tears of the orphan are poured out before you, lest you yourself tremble before the sea of fire in which sinners who did not repent are judged.

**8.** Set your feet straight in the way of peace; seek peace and pursue it, for the eyes of the LORD are toward his righteous ones and his ears toward their petitions.

**9.** But the face of the LORD is against those who do evil, to erase their memory from the earth and leave no one to commemorate them in the mountains or in the plains.

**10.** “I will not turn away my face until I destroy those who did not keep my word, for I am the LORD who is jealous, who takes vengeance on those who hate me and on those who do not keep my word.

**11.** “But I will honor those who honor me and keep my word.”
