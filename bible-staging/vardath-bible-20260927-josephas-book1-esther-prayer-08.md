### Esther’s Fast and Prayer

All the people in the city of Ahasuerus and in Shushan the palace learned what the enemy intended against them, and they cried out to God because of the danger that had come upon them.

Esther the queen also became deeply afraid when she learned of the plot against her people. She removed the garments and ornaments of royalty, loosened her hair, covered herself in mourning, and humbled herself with fasting and prayer. She fell upon her face before God and prayed:

“O LORD God of Israel, King of kings, Creator of all things and ruler over them, help your servant, for I have no helper besides you.

“You know that while I was still young I lost my father and mother, and I came into this foreign land as an orphan. This king took me, not because I sought it, and I have lived among a people who are not my people and among those whose religion is not my religion.

“Yet I have not abandoned your worship. Neither the world nor the royal estate in which I live has turned me away from obedience to you.

“You know what this enemy and his followers intend against us. He seeks our destruction, and he has turned the heart of the king against me and against my people.

“I come before you with supplication, prayer, and humility, asking for mercy and favor.

“If you have determined that we are to perish, let me die before I see evil come upon my people. But if it is your will that we remain in this world, then hasten to your servants and rescue the sheep of your pasture from the fierce lions and from the beasts that wait to seize and destroy them.

“You are the one who has always aided your people in their affliction and delivered them from their enemies.

“Therefore, merciful LORD, deal with us again according to your former compassion. Take my hand, for I am an orphan who clings to you and places my trust in you.

“Deliver me from this enemy and grant me victory over him. Preserve me from harm when I enter before the king and when I depart from him, so that I may go in and come out safely beneath your mercy.

“Look upon your servant with compassion and turn the king’s heart toward kindness, for the hearts of kings are in your hand, and you are able to direct them as you will.”

### Esther Goes Before the King

On the third day Esther put on her royal garments and adorned herself with the ornaments of the queen.

She took two of her maidservants with her. She leaned upon one according to royal custom, while the other followed behind and lifted the train of her robe from the ground.

Esther made her face appear joyful, but sorrow remained hidden within her heart.

She entered the innermost court where the king sat and stood before him in great fear.

The king became angry because she had broken the royal custom by entering without being summoned.

When Esther saw anger upon the king’s face, her fear increased and her grief became heavy. She nearly collapsed as she stood supported by her maidservants, while inwardly she continued to call upon God.

God looked upon her humility and distress and showed her mercy. He gave her favor before the king and increased the grace and beauty that appeared in her.
