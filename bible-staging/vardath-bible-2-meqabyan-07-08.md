## 2 Meqabyan 7

**1.** “Woe to you and to those like you who did not know the LORD who created you and did not know his commandment and his word. You will still seek to repent in a place where repentance is no longer possible, when you are tormented in the suffering of Sheol.

**2.** “There will be no escape from it forever for you, your gods, and those to whom you sacrifice and bow down as though they were the LORD your Creator. They have neither soul nor breath; they do not avenge the one who wrongs them or reward the one who does good to them.

**3.** “They are the work of human hands. Satan speaks through them to deceive the hearts of fools like you, so that he may bring you, the ministers of your gods, and the demons who command you down into the Gehenna of fire.

**4.** “For you do not understand that there is nothing by which you can be saved while you wander and go astray.

**5.** “The animals that the LORD created for your food are better than you, as are the wild beasts and the dogs; for they have no second judgment after their one death.

**6.** “But you will die and afterward be judged in the dreadful Gehenna from which there is no escape forever.”

**7.** When they had said these things, they passed away and disappeared from him.

**8.** Ṣiruṣaydan remained in great fear and trembling, and the terror did not leave him until morning.

**9.** Afterward he went to the place where he had left the bodies of the righteous, but he did not find them. He had wished to honor them and was unable to do so, for the LORD had hidden them so that their bodies would not be sought.

## 2 Meqabyan 8

**1.** He continued in pride and in the arrogance of his heart.

**2.** He went about in the ways of the surrounding nations, for his kingdom was spoken of as the iron kingdom which Daniel the prophet saw.

**3.** He walked in every madness, evil, and disturbance.

**4.** As was said before, he tore, crushed, and devoured.

**5.** What remained he trampled beneath his feet, for he was abominable like his father the devil and stiff-necked.

**6.** He did not know the LORD as his Creator, but said, “My days will be like the days of heaven.”

**7.** He imagined in his heart that even the sun set because of him.

**8.** He rose up in Gad and dwelt in the land of Zebulun. He raised war against Macedonia, took provisions from Samaria, and received tribute from Syria.

**9.** He extended his reach as far as the tents of Kedar and as far as Sidon. He gathered tribute in Achaia, lifted up his neck as far as the sea of Tekeze, and sent his message as far as the sea of Hindeke.

**10.** Thus he lifted up his neck as far as heaven.

**11.** He never lowered his head, but remained in every evil and arrogance.

**12.** His whole path was in slipperiness and darkness, arrogance and lawlessness, violence and the shedding of blood.

**13.** He did everything the LORD hates, just as Sablanios, his teacher of evil, had taught him: wickedness, strife, fornication, and oppression of the orphan, and he showed no mercy to the poor.

**14.** He brought the kings of the nations under his hand and killed them.

**15.** He condemned the messengers of his enemies, subdued many nations, and made them serve him as he wished.

**16.** He left none who escaped him, from the sea of Tarshish as far as the sea of Jericho.

**17.** He bowed to idols and ate carrion, blood, torn flesh, and what had been sacrificed to gods. There was no justice in him; all his ways were injustice, wrath, anger, and turmoil. He became a terror to the nations under his hand and enslaved them as he wished.

**18.** There was no fear of the LORD before his eyes, for everything his heart desired he did before himself, and he walked in pride before the LORD his Creator.

**19.** He did not regard the LORD as his God. But the LORD will repay him according to the evil he did to his fellow when the day comes in which he is seized by wrath.

**20.** The LORD will take vengeance on him in its time, just as he erased the sinners who came before him. For he says, “I am the LORD who takes vengeance upon the proud who do not walk in my commandment, and I erase their memory from the earth.”

**21.** The wicked will be repaid according to the evil they have done.

**22.** But those who do good will be followed by good from the LORD.

**23.** Thus Joshua brought the kings of Canaan to an end at one time—five kings in a single cave—and through his prayer caused the sun to stand still at Gibeon so that he might destroy their armies.
