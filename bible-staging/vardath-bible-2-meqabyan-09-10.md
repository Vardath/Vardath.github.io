## 2 Meqabyan 9

**1.** And you, O man—you are not the LORD. Why are you proud? Today you are a man, but tomorrow you will be earth and dust, and plants will grow in your grave.

**2.** Sheol also will follow you and those who do deeds like yours. Your teacher is Sablanios, who brought upon his own head the sin of the whole world because in his pride he deceived our father Adam.

**3.** He refused to bow before the work of his God because he exalted himself and stiffened his neck.

**4.** You also, like your teacher, refused to bow before the LORD your God.

**5.** You walk as your predecessors walked, those who did not know the LORD their God.

**6.** He took vengeance upon them, and they descended into Sheol because of the evil deeds they had done upon the earth.

**7.** You also, like them, will descend into Gehenna.

**8.** Do you think you can escape from the hand of the LORD? His wrath has been given over against you because you despised the worship of the LORD who gave you authority over kingdoms. *The digital source splits the final word of this sentence into a stray line numbered 5; it is restored here to verse 8.*

**9.** He tested you to see whether you would do his will. If you make your way good upon the earth, the LORD will make your ways good. Everything to which you put your hand he will prosper and bless, and he will subdue all your enemies and adversaries before you.

**10.** You will rejoice in your going out and your coming in, in the fruit of your body, your treasuries, and your flocks. Whatever you point to with your finger and whatever you devise in your heart will obey you, for abundance from the LORD will overflow to you, so that you may build, plant, and rule.

**11.** But if you refuse to hear the word and law of the LORD and do not walk in his judgment, you will have nowhere to escape from the hand of the LORD, like all the lawless people before you who did not worship the LORD in purity or trust in his righteous judgment, for all his judgment is righteous.

**12.** Nothing is hidden from his eyes; everything is open and manifest before him.

**13.** He is the holder of the authority of kings and the one who removes the thrones of the mighty.

**14.** He is the one who raises the humble and lifts up those who have fallen.

**15.** He is the one who frees prisoners and raises the dead. Those whose flesh has decayed and become like dust he will awaken when he wills, for the dew of mercy is from him.

**16.** But those who have done evil he will raise for judgment, because they provoked him, and he will take vengeance upon them.

**17.** Even upon the earth he will cause their seed to perish, because they are lawless against the law and ordinance of the LORD.

**18.** Sinners will not remain in the counsel of the righteous, for the way of the righteous is distinct from the way of sinners.

**19.** As heaven is high above the earth, so far is the way of the righteous from the way of sinners.

**20.** The way of sinners is fornication, strife, violence, sexual immorality, uncleanness, greed, theft of another’s property, and drunkenness accompanied by violence.

**21.** It is running toward the shedding of blood, wasting what ought not to be wasted, bringing tears to orphans, eating carrion and blood, eating forbidden flesh and camel, and having intercourse with a woman after childbirth or with a menstruating woman before purification.

**22.** All these deeds of sinners are the broad path and the wide net of Satan that carries them into perdition and everlasting judgment.

**23.** But the way of the righteous is narrow and confined, and it leads into life: humility and gentleness, love and peace, fasting and prayer, purity of the body, abstaining from forbidden foods, carrion and torn flesh, from fornication, and from another man’s wife.

**24.** They guard themselves from every defiled deed and from unclean foods that are not commanded in the law, and from every impure work that does not please the LORD; for sinners do all these things.

**25.** The righteous withdraw from every evil way that does not please the LORD.

**26.** He loves them, receives them, and delivers them from all their affliction.

**27.** But Satan rules over sinners because they keep his law and ordinance and everything that he loves.

## 2 Meqabyan 10

**1.** And you also, kings and rulers, do not walk in his way, but fear the LORD who created you and has preserved you until this day.

**2.** Walk in the commandment and judgment of the LORD who rules over all.

**3.** For when the children of Israel came toward Amalek to inherit the land of the Canaanites, Hittites, and Perizzites, Balak son of Zippor arose.

**4.** He said to Balaam, “Come and curse them for me, for the one whom you curse is cursed and the one whom you bless is blessed. I will give you much gold and silver and honor you greatly if you curse them for me.”

**5.** Balaam came hoping for the reward of wrongdoing, and Balak son of Zippor showed him where the children of Israel had encamped.

**6.** He built altars, offered sacrifices, and slaughtered sheep and cattle from the best of them, intending to curse the children of Israel.

**7.** But the LORD did not permit him to curse them; rather, by his word he turned the curse into a blessing.

**8.** Balaam said, “May those who bless you be blessed, and those who curse you be cursed, for you are the people whom the LORD has chosen and the dwelling of the Beloved who will come from on high.”

**9.** Then Balak son of Zippor grieved, became angry, and was enraged because Balaam had blessed them before him when he had commanded him to curse them.

**10.** Balaam said to him, “I cannot curse those whom the LORD has blessed, for the people whom the LORD chose have come into this land.”

**11.** Balak son of Zippor said to Balaam, “I wanted you to curse them for me, but you did not curse them; instead you blessed them before me. Even if my house were filled with gold and silver, I would have given it to you when you said, ‘Give it to me and I will curse them for you.’ But you have blessed them, and you have not done good to me; therefore I also will not do good to you.”

**12.** Balaam said, “I cannot alter or transgress the word of the LORD. Whatever word the LORD puts into my mouth, that alone I will speak.

**13.** “I will not value possessions above my soul, for if I loved possessions, the LORD would be angry with me for cursing a blessed people.

**14.** “For the LORD said to Jacob their father, ‘Those who bless you shall be blessed, and those who curse you shall be cursed; everyone who blesses you is blessed.’

**15.** “The one who curses you unjustly is cursed. Make your deeds and your way good so that the LORD may be pleased with you.

**16.** “Do not be like the peoples of former times who provoked the LORD by their evil. He rejected them: some he wiped away with the waters of the flood,

**17.** “and some he destroyed by the hands of their enemies. He brought against them a hostile and evil people who afflicted them and carried away their kings together with their prophets and Levites.

**18.** “They took them to a strange land that they did not know, carried them captive, plundered them, chastened them, and devastated their country.

**19.** “They made Jerusalem like a field, for they destroyed the foundation and the wall of the holy city.

**20.** “The priests were taken captive, the law was despised, and hands fell by the spear.

**21.** “Widows were taken captive and could not mourn over their dead husbands, for captivity itself was upon them.

**22.** “Children wept, elders were dishonored, and they showed no mercy to the face of the aged or the gray-haired.

**23.** “They spared neither those with child nor virgins. They destroyed everything they found in their cities and removed them to a strange land they did not know. For when the LORD wills to show wrath toward his people, he first allows his sanctuary to be taken away.

**24.** “Therefore, when the LORD rejected the children of Israel, he caused Jerusalem to be plowed like a field because they continually provoked their Creator.

**25.** “Yet he did not utterly destroy them in his mercy, for he showed mercy to them because of their fathers and not because of themselves, because he loved their fathers Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, who reigned in righteousness and walked uprightly before the one who created them.

**26.** “He appointed them as a boast of glory in heaven and upon earth and established for them a kingdom.” *The digital Geʽez source places the final words of this sentence on a stray line numbered 2; they are restored here to verse 26.*

**27.** “So also you kings and rulers who live in this passing world: remember those who came before you, who walked in purity, inherited the kingdom of heaven, and whose names became good from generation to generation.

**28.** “Make your kingdom upright and make your name good, like the good kings before you who pleased the LORD by the goodness of their lives.”
