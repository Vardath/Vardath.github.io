## 3 Meqabyan 1

**1.** Let the islands of Egypt rejoice in the latter days because of the good and gentle one who will come upon them and avenge those whom Sabellius oppressed and led astray, he who opposed the way of his Creator.

**2.** He will take vengeance upon him and bring his glory down into the dust and into humiliation, because he exalted himself in his thought.

**3.** And he said, “Who will rise above me? I will enter into the heart of the seas and ascend into the heights. I will look upon the waters and count them as a bird’s nest among the children of Adam.

**4.** “No one will snatch them from my hand, for I will not be overcome by those who dwell upon the earth, even if they do the will of the LORD. I will devise plans against them so that I may turn them away from the straight way of the LORD.

**5.** “I will bring them into my evil way so that they may walk with me into destruction.

**6.** “For this reason those who love him and keep his commandment will hate me. But those who stray from their Lord will come to me, love me, keep my covenant, and perform my command as I command them, for I harden their hearts and twist their thoughts so that they will not return to the LORD their Creator.

**7.** “When I show them the possessions of the world, I turn their hearts away from the straight way. When I show them beautiful women and virgins, by these also I turn them away from the straight way.

**8.** “When I show them precious stones from India, glittering gems, gold, and silver, by these also I turn them away from the straight way so that they may return to my deeds.

**9.** “When I show them fine garments, linen, silk, brocade, and costly cloth, by these I turn them from the straight way and bring them into my thoughts. When I show them herds and animals as numerous as sand, by these also I turn them toward my deeds.

**10.** “When I show them the desires that arise from pride, from women, from wealth, and from wrath, through all these things I turn them into my way.

**11.** “When I show them omens, I enter into the hearts of their companions and whisper into their hearts matters concerning those omens. I show a sign through their words, and I drive them into madness.

**12.** “Those whom I have made my dwelling I show omens, whether through the course of the stars, the form of the clouds, the burning of fire, or the movements of birds and wild animals. Through all these things I place signs within their hearts, because they are my dwelling.

**13.** “They speak and give signs to their companions, and I go before them and make the sign come to pass according to what their diviners have told them.

**14.** “I make their words into signs so that those who consult them may go astray. They give them payment and tell their companions, saying, ‘There are no wise diviners like this man or that woman, for what they say comes to pass. They know prophecy and distinguish good and evil, and everything happens according to what they have spoken.’

**15.** “When they say these things, I rejoice, because those who perish become many and those who go astray through me increase, so that the children of Adam may perish with me. For because of their father Adam the LORD cast me down from my throne when I said, ‘I will not bow down to this lowly one.’

**16.** “I will drag into destruction all his children who walk according to my command. Through me they incur vengeance from the one who created me, so that those whom I have led astray may be cast with me into the fire.

**17.** “I pleaded with my Lord when his wrath burned against me and he commanded that I be bound and cast into Gehenna and into the depths of Sheol. When my Creator commanded this, I pleaded before him, saying, ‘My Lord, because you were angry with me you disciplined me, and in your wrath you punished me. Allow me to speak one more word before you.’

**18.** “My Lord answered me and said, ‘Speak, and I will hear you.’ Then I began and made supplication to him, saying, ‘Since I have been driven from my throne, let those whom I lead astray be with me in the torment that I suffer.

**19.** “‘But those who refuse me, who do not go astray through me and do not keep my command, but instead perform your command, fulfill your will, and keep your word—those who reject me and do not follow the error into which I would lead them—let them belong to your kingdom and receive the crown that you once gave to me when you loved me.

**20.** “‘As for all the powers that were exiled with me and were called satans, give them their crowns and seat them at your right in the throne that was mine. You are more righteous than I and than my angels.

**21.** “‘Let them praise you according to your will and become as I was and as my angels were. Let them praise you according to your will, for my authority has been taken away and theirs has been raised up, because you hated me and loved those who were created from earth and dust.’

**22.** “My Lord answered me and said, ‘As you have asked: those who do not desire my service shall be yours according to your word, for although they see and hear, you lead them astray.

**23.** “‘I will not grieve over their destruction when they come to you after abandoning my commandment and the words of my Scriptures. If you lead them astray, they will be condemned with you in the judgment.

**24.** “‘There will be no escape for you forever and ever. Those who go astray through you will enter the judgment, and you will suffer in the fire forever and ever.’”
