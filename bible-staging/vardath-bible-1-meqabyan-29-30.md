## 1 Meqabyan 29

**1.** When Egypt oppressed the children of Israel with clay work, forced labor, every kind of service they compelled them to perform, and beatings without mercy,

**2.** they set taskmasters over them and made them serve. Then they cried out to the LORD that he might deliver them from all the forced labor of Egypt.

**3.** At that time he sent them their helpers, Moses and Aaron, and delivered them from the work of bondage. The LORD sent them to bring his people out from the house of servitude of Pharaoh king of Egypt, for in his great pride Pharaoh refused to release the people so that they might cease serving him and offer sacrifice to the LORD in the wilderness.

**4.** For the LORD repays the proud. Because of Pharaoh’s pride, the LORD drowned him together with his army in the Eritrean Sea.

**5.** So also those kings and rulers who reject the word of the LORD and do not act rightly in everything for which he appointed and enthroned them to do his will: he will reward those who serve in goodness and honor his great name.

**6.** “If you establish my kingdom in righteousness, I also will establish your kingdoms,” says the LORD who rules over all.

**7.** “Do good before me and I will do good to you. Keep my commandment and I will preserve your souls. Walk in my commandment and I will walk with you according to the desire of your hearts.

**8.** “Love me and I will love your lives. Entrust yourselves to me and I will strengthen you.

**9.** “Put your trust in me and I will save you,” says the LORD who rules over all.

**10.** “Do not wander from side to side. Draw near to me and I will draw near to you, for I love upright paths,” says the LORD who rules over all. “Cleanse your hands, sinners and violent people, and purify the evil of your hearts.

**11.** “Then I will turn my wrath away from you and return to you with compassion and mercy.

**12.** “I will remove from you lawless enemies who work violence, just as I delivered David my servant from the many dangers that overtook him: from the hand of Goliath the warrior, from the hand of Saul who sought to kill him, and from the hand of Absalom his son who wanted to take his kingdom.

**13.** “In the same way I will deliver those who do my will, keep my commandment, and endure in my law. I will give them glory and set them above all, so that they may rejoice both in this world and in the world to come.

**14.** “They shall share the company of the blessed rulers who pleased the LORD by the goodness of their lives, as Samuel did while he was still a child, whom the LORD chose from his youth because of the goodness of his life.

**15.** The LORD spoke to Samuel so that he might tell Eli, the aged priest. Samuel the prophet was good in his conduct while he served in the house of the LORD God.

**16.** When he grew up in the house of the LORD while serving there, the LORD appointed him to establish the people and to anoint kings at the LORD’s command. He placed the anointing of kingship in his hand when he did the will of the LORD his God, for the LORD was pleased with him and chose him to appoint a ruler over the people whom the LORD had chosen from the children of Israel.

**17.** While Saul was king, the LORD said to Samuel the prophet, “Go and anoint David son of Jesse, from the house of Judah, for I have chosen him.”

## 1 Meqabyan 30

**1.** “I rejected the house of Saul because he provoked me by transgressing my word.

**2.** “I rejected him, and kingship shall not again be established from his house, because he did not keep my word.

**3.** “In the same way, from those who do not keep my word and do not walk in my law and my ordinances, I will remove my gift and my kingdom from their seed forever.

**4.** “I will turn from magnifying them any longer, except to bring them down, for when I magnified them they did not magnify me, and when I honored them they did not honor me.

**5.** “When I did good to them, they did not do good before me, and when I showed them mercy, they did not respond with mercy.

**6.** “For I made them the head over all, but they did not make me the head over them. When I honored them above all, they did not honor me and did not keep my commandment.

**7.** “Therefore I took away the gift that I had given them, and it shall not return to them, as I swore in my wrath. But the one who honors me I will honor, and the one who loves me I will love.” Thus says the LORD who rules over all.

**8.** “Those who do not honor me and do not keep my commandment I will strip of the gift that I gave them.”

**9.** The LORD who rules over all says, “The one who loves me I will love, and the one who exalts me I will exalt.

**10.** “For I am the LORD who judges all, and there is none who can escape from my hand in heaven above or on the earth beneath. I am the LORD who kills and gives life, who chastens and shows mercy.

**11.** “Greatness and honor are mine. The one whom I love I honor, and the one whom I reject I bring low.

**12.** “I am the one who shows compassion to those who love me and remember my name at all times. For the poor and for the rich, I am the provider of food.

**13.** “And not for mankind alone, but also for the animals, the birds, the wild beasts, the fish that are in the sea, and the serpents.

**14.** “I provide also for the great sea creatures, the creeping creatures, the small living things, Behemoth, and the horned creatures.

**15.** “Everything that moves in the waters and everything that flies above the earth is mine.

**16.** “I am the one who feeds them in what is fitting and according to what is needed, all those who seek me.”
