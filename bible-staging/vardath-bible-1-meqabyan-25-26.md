## 1 Meqabyan 25

**1.** “Woe to their souls when I seize them in my wrath,” says the LORD who rules over all, “for their way is crooked, and all who walk in the path of Satan are deceived.

**2.** “They do not know the way of the LORD. They turn their backs upon it and reject my law.

**3.** “I will repay them according to the evil of their deeds. In the latter day, on the day of judgment, I will take vengeance on them according to the evil deeds written before me. They shall not escape from my hand, for I am the LORD who kills and gives life, who chastens and shows mercy.

**4.** “Nothing can escape from my hand, neither beneath the earth nor above the heavens, neither in the sea nor in the depths of the waters; for I am the LORD from one end of the world to the other, and every soul is in my hand.

**5.** “I command the serpent beneath the earth, the creatures in the sea, the birds in the air, and the wild creatures in the wilderness. From end to end, to the uttermost bounds of the world, all is mine.

**6.** “There is nothing that can escape from my hand in heaven above or on the earth beneath. I am the one who works wonders and mighty deeds before my eyes, and there is none who can say to me, ‘Where are you going?’ or ‘What are you doing?’

**7.** “I command the angels and the princes above. Every name that is named is mine, and all the birds of heaven, the wild beasts of the wilderness, and the animals are mine.

**8.** “The south wind rises, and the heat of the east wind; the Eritrean Sea roars at the greatness of the majesty of the one who will come to it in the latter days.

**9.** “For he judges the living and the dead, together with Noba, Saba, Ethiopia, Hindeke, and all their lands and boundaries.

**10.** “He sees all with gentleness by the staff that is in his hand, and he tends with the shepherd’s staff of his flock, for his staff is exalted above every staff.

**11.** “His horn is mightier than every horn, his kingdom is exalted above every kingdom, and his authority holds the whole world. He is able to do all things, and nothing is impossible for him.

**12.** “He commands the clouds above, causes grass to spring up for the animals below, and gives fruit upon the crops.

**13.** “He feeds all according to his will, each according to its kind, its fruit, and its appointed food. He feeds the small creatures beneath the earth and the wild beasts and animals upon the earth. He grants the prayer of the one who prays, and he does not disregard the petitions of widows and orphans.

**14.** “Above all, he receives the prayers of the pure who pray to him at all times. The boasting of the wicked is like a whirlwind, and the gathering of the lawless like the vapor of a cloud.

**15.** “Their rejoicing is like a bird that flies away, and the beauty of gold and silver upon the earth passes away. Their gold will not profit them, but will become a testimony against the wicked, and the wealth of their garments will be eaten by moths.

**16.** “Their storehouses of grain and provisions will be consumed by worms and weevils. Everything passes like yesterday, a day that has gone by. As a word that has gone out from the mouth does not return, so is the wealth of sinners; and as a passing shadow, so is the beauty of their possessions, and as a borrowed garment, so is the wealth of sinners before the LORD.

**17.** “But if the righteous possess wealth, the LORD will not reject them when they show mercy to the poor, uphold the cause of orphans and the needy, clothe the naked from their own garments, and do not withhold from the produce of their household which the LORD gave them, but give to the stranger and the poor.

## 1 Meqabyan 26

**1.** The poor also ponder upon their beds, and if the wealthy do not receive them, they become like a dry tree that has no moisture. Where there is no moisture, no root springs up; and where there is no root, no leaf springs up.

**2.** If no leaf springs up, it bears no fruit, for the leaf serves for the adornment of the fruit. One who has no fruit has no good work, for the fruit of a person is righteousness.

**3.** If a person practices righteousness, he bears uprightness as fruit, and the LORD rejoices in the person who works righteousness and uprightness.

**4.** He grants the prayer of the one who prays and the labor of his lips, and he does not wrong the righteous because of the righteousness he has done.

**5.** Nor does he justify the sinner without repentance for the evil deeds he has committed; for the LORD is righteous and loves righteousness. He shows no partiality to the rich or to the poor in judgment, for he is ruler of heaven and earth, and every soul is in his hand.
