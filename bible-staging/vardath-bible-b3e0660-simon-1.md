### Simon son of Mattathias

When Simon son of Mattathias took the leadership, he compelled those who opposed him to submit to the Jews by force.

He was a man of good conduct and governed his people well.

He directed them with excellent judgment, so his authority became secure and the condition of the nation was set right through him.
