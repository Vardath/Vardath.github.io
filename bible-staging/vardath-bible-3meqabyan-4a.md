## 3 Meqabyan 4

**1.** “The Lord of Adam remains to redeem him; he will put you to shame and will deliver the sheep from the hand of the wolf.

**2.** “But you will rise with those whom you have made subject to you, and they will be carried with you into destruction.

**3.** “Those who keep the law of the LORD their God will rejoice with their Creator. The LORD has hidden them from your evil work so that he may make them his own portion and so that they may praise him with his holy angels, who did not transgress the law of their God as you did.

**4.** “But because of your pride the throne of the Most High LORD, which he had given you above all those like you so that you might praise him with his angels, was taken from you.

**5.** “You refused, and you and those with you came to be called demons. But those who love the LORD will become his people like the holy angels; without weariness the cherubim and seraphim spread their wings as they praise him.

**6.** “Those who love the LORD will become his people like the holy angels; without weariness the cherubim and seraphim spread their wings as they praise him.

**7.** “But through your falsehood and your pride you destroyed the praise appointed to you, which you had been commanded to offer at all times together with your order and all your company who were created with you in fellowship.

**8.** “When you neglected the praise of the one who created you, imagining that he could not make another like you and that the praise of the LORD who created you would cease, he made a tenth order when you were separated from the assembly of your brothers, so that the praise of the one who created you would not cease.

**9.** “Because of the pride of your heart you grew weary of praising the one who created you. Therefore he became angry with you, brought you low, and bound you in the terror of Gehenna together with your demons.

**10.** “Then with his holy hands he took clay from the earth, mixed it with water, fire, and wind, and formed Adam according to his own image and likeness.

**11.** “He placed him among all the works of his hands so that the praise missing because of you might be filled. Thus the praise of the earthly one was joined to the praise of the heavenly ones, and their praise became one.

**12.** “But through your pride and the stiffness of your neck you were driven away, and you destroyed yourself from the praise of the LORD who created you.

**13.** “Know that his praise was not diminished, for according to the counsel of his will he made another to praise him, so that the praise of the LORD would lack nothing.

**14.** “For he knows everything before it comes to be. Even before he created you he knew that you would transgress his commandment; and when you transgressed it, he made Adam his servant according to his image and likeness, for before the world there was hidden counsel with him.

**15.** “As Solomon said: ‘Before the mountains he begot me; before the world was fashioned he created me; before the foundations of the earth were established.

**16.** “‘Before the stations of the mountains and hills were ordered, before the paths of the world were made firm, before the light of the sun and moon shone, and before there were courses of stars and seasons.

**17.** “‘Before night and day were distinguished, before the bounds of the sea were established, and before all that has come to be existed.’

**18.** “Before what is now visible had appeared, and before everything now bearing a name had been named, neither you nor those like you existed except within the thought of the LORD; Adam his servant also was within his counsel.
