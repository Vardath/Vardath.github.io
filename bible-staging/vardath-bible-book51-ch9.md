## 1 Meqabyan 9

**1.** The plains of Gilboa and the mountains of Lebanon will not bring forth grass for livestock and wild animals unless the rain of mercy descends upon them from the LORD.

**2.** The mountains of Gilead and the plains of Elam will give no green growth for goats and sheep, nor for the wild animals of the wilderness, the oribi, the ibex, and the strong beasts.

**3.** So also with apostates and heretics who have made rebellion and error their possession: unless the dew of mercy from the LORD descends upon them, the dead will not rise. This concerns sorcerers, diviners, Qopros and Dimaso, and those who stir up strife.

**4.** It concerns those who deal in roots and spells, those who practice sorcery, and those who cause people to fight and kill one another.

**5.** It concerns those who commit fornication outside the law, the people of Athens and the Medes who trust in their idols, and those who sing and make music for them with drum, lyre, and harp.

**6.** They go astray with their idols and desire the work of their own hands. They cannot save themselves, and they will be condemned on the day of the Judge, at the resurrection.

**7.** O foolish and empty-minded one, does it seem to you that the dead will not rise?

**8.** Do not think this way, for you will not remain asleep there when the trumpet is blown at the resurrection of the dead by the mouth of Michael, chief of the angels.

**9.** Mountains and hills will be made low; rivers and depths will be leveled, and they will become a straight highway.

**10.** There will be a resurrection for all flesh.
