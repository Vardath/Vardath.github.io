## 1 Meqabyan 2

**1.** There was a man of the tribe of Benjamin whose name was Meqabios.

**2.** He had three sons, handsome in appearance and exceedingly strong, and they were loved by the people throughout the region of Tsiruts-Saydan, the land of the Chaldeans, Midian, and the land of Medon. *The Beta maṣāḥǝft XML is lacunose after “and he had”; the substance of this verse is restored from parallel witnesses of the same chapter.*

**3.** The king’s men found them and said, “Will you not bow down and offer sacrifice to the gods of King Tsiruts-Saydan, as he commanded?”

**4.** “If you refuse, we will seize you and take you to the king, and all your possessions will be destroyed according to the king’s command.”

**5.** Those noble young men answered them, “We have the God of our fathers, before whom we bow: the Creator of the heavens and the earth, the sea and everything within it, the Maker of the sun and the moon, the stars and the clouds. He is the true God whom we worship and in whom we believe.”

**6.** The king’s officers were four commanders, each accompanied by [fifty] armed men carrying the weapons of their warfare. *The troop numeral is damaged in the digital transcription and is restored from parallel witnesses.*

**7.** When they attempted to seize these holy men, the brothers escaped from their hands and no one could lay hold of them. They went away carrying their weapons, for the young men were exceedingly strong and powerful.

**8.** One of them could kill a bear by seizing it by the throat and strangling it at once as easily as a chicken.

**9.** Another could kill a lion with a single cast of a stone or with one blow of a staff.

**10.** Another, in the midst of battle, could strike down a hundred men at one time with a single sword. Thus their name and the fear of them became known throughout the land of Moab and among the Chaldeans.

**11.** They were mighty in strength, and they possessed beauty, favor, and eloquence.

**12.** Their outward appearance was wonderful, but greater still was the beauty and courage of their hearts, because they worshiped the LORD and did not fear death.

**13.** When the army sought to terrify them, no one was able to capture them. Being strong, they escaped into the heights of the mountain.

**14.** The soldiers returned to the city, shut its gates, and threatened the inhabitants, saying, “Unless you bring us these warriors, the Meqabyans, we will burn your city with fire. We will send word to the king, and your land will be destroyed.”

**15.** Then the people of the city came out together—the poor and the wealthy, men and women, widows and orphans. They lifted their eyes toward the mountain and cried out to the brothers, saying, “Do not let us and our city be destroyed!”

**16.** Then they all wept together and were filled with fear before the LORD.

**17.** They turned their faces toward the east, stretched out their hands together, and prayed to the LORD, saying, “O Lord, shall we yield ourselves to these transgressors of your law and your commandment?

**18.** “We do not desire to obey the word of this lawless man who has no faith in your law, but trusts instead in gold and silver, in wood and stone fashioned by human hands.

**19.** “He imagines that these things created him, though you are the Creator, the one who kills and gives life. He, however, is a shedder of human blood and a devourer of human flesh.

**20.** “We do not desire to look upon the face of this lawless man or to listen to his word.

**21.** “Yet if you command us to go to him, we will go, and for your sake we will surrender our lives to death. But we will not obey the word of that lawless man when he says, ‘Offer sacrifice to my gods.’

**22.** “O Lord, with all our hearts we have put our trust in you, the God of our fathers who did your will and walked in your law—the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, who searches the heart and the inward parts.

**23.** “You examine the righteous and the sinner; you search what is within the human heart. Nothing hidden is concealed from you, for every secret lies open before you.

**24.** “We have no other God besides you.

**25.** “Be our strength, our firmness, and our refuge in this work by which we serve you, as we give our lives to death for the sake of your holy name.

**26.** “As you heard the prayer of Jacob when Israel entered the land of Egypt, hear us now also, O holy God, for we call upon you.”

**27.** Then a sign appeared before them: two men stood in their presence, and fiery swords descended like terrifying lightning and struck them so that they died; but immediately they rose again alive before the brothers. *The Beta maṣāḥǝft transcription breaks off after the opening words of this verse; the event is restored from parallel witnesses.*

**28.** Their appearance became more wonderful still; they shone more brightly than the sun, and their beauty was greater than before.
