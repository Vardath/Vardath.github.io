### The Cleansing and Dedication of the Sanctuary

After Judah had prevailed against the armies sent against him, he returned to Jerusalem.

He ordered the sanctuary to be cleansed from everything Antiochus had established there contrary to the law.

The idols and every defilement were removed.

The holy place was purified from the pollution that had been brought into it, for swine had been offered there and the sanctuary had been profaned.

When the cleansing was completed, Judah and those with him prepared a new altar.

They placed wood upon it and prayed to God, asking him to show them a sign concerning the restored service.

Then a sign of fire appeared among the stones of the altar by the power of God.

The fire kindled the wood and consumed the offerings.

The people rejoiced and gave thanks to God for the deliverance he had given them.

They celebrated the dedication for eight days with prayer, praise, and thanksgiving.

The first day was the twenty-fifth day of the month Kislev.

They established these eight days as a remembrance to be observed from year to year.

Thus the sanctuary at Jerusalem was purified and its worship restored after the defilement brought upon it in the days of Antiochus.
