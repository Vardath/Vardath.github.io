### Ptolemy and the Translation of the Sacred Books

Among the Greek rulers after Alexander was Ptolemy, king of Egypt.

Ptolemy loved wisdom and books and desired to gather writings from many peoples and languages into his royal collection.

When he learned that the Jews possessed sacred books containing their law and the words of their prophets, he wished to understand them in the language of the Greeks.

He therefore sent to Jerusalem and asked the high priest to provide men learned in the Hebrew language and also skilled in Greek, so that the sacred writings might be translated faithfully.

The request was accepted, and learned men of Israel were sent to Egypt with the books.

Ptolemy received them with honor and provided what was needed for their work.

They translated the books of the law and the prophets from Hebrew into Greek so that the king and those among his people who knew Greek might understand them.

When the work was completed, the translations were examined and found to agree in their meaning.

Ptolemy rejoiced greatly over the work and honored the translators with gifts.

He also showed favor toward the Jews who lived under his authority and sent gifts for the service of the house of God at Jerusalem.

Thus the sacred writings of Israel were made known in the Greek language during the rule of Ptolemy.
