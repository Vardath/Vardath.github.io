## 1 Meqabyan 23

**1.** Do not walk in the way of Cain, who killed his brother, though his brother followed him in gentleness and Cain supposed that his brother loved him.

**2.** He killed his brother because of jealousy over a woman. In the same way are those who work violence, jealousy, and betrayal against their companion.

**3.** They walk in the way of Cain and not in the gentle way of Abel; for Abel was gentle like a sheep, and his blood was like the clean blood of a sheep offered to the LORD with a pure heart.

**4.** Those who walk in the gentleness of Abel love the LORD. The LORD loves everyone who walks in gentleness, just as Abel was righteous and gentle.

**5.** But the LORD will requite the wicked. Their judgment is fixed upon their necks and written upon their shoulders, and on the day of judgment it will be read before angels, before mankind, and before all creation.

**6.** Then the lawless and the rebellious, who did not do the will of the LORD, will be put to shame.

**7.** And a word of terror will be spoken concerning them: “Cast them into the judgment of the burning fire, where there is no escape forever.”

## 1 Meqabyan 24

**1.** When Gideon trusted in the LORD, with a small force he defeated many thousands of the uncircumcised, an army beyond number like locusts. *The numeral immediately before “thousands” is damaged in the digital Geʽez source and is therefore not silently reconstructed here.*

**2.** “Do not trust in other gods, for there is no other God besides me, O kings and rulers.

**3.** “For I am the LORD your God, who brought you forth from your mothers’ wombs, raised you, fed you, and clothed you. Why then do you make excuses and worship another god besides me?

**4.** “I have done all these things for you. What have you given me, and what do I desire from you, except the salvation of your souls—that you walk in my law, my ordinances, and my commandments, and that I give salvation to your souls?”

**5.** Thus says the LORD who rules over all: “Save yourselves from idols, sorcery, and divination.

**6.** “For the punishment of the LORD comes upon those who practice these things, upon those who listen to them, upon those who do their will, upon those who join themselves to them, and upon those who walk according to their commands.

**7.** “A people who do not know you and will not pity you will rise against you if you do not do the will of the LORD who made you. As the prophets declared—from Samuel the prophet, and again Ezekiel and Enoch, and the prophecy of Asaph—strangers will consume the fruit of your labor.

**8.** “Fierce men will come, changing their garments. There is no law among them except eating and drinking, adorning themselves with gold and silver, and doing every work of the flesh that does not please the LORD.

**9.** “They are ready for eating and drinking, and from the time they rise from sleep, from morning until evening, they run after evil deeds. There is no path of peace for their feet, but only misery and destruction in their way.

**10.** “They do not know the way of peace, and there is no fear of the LORD before their eyes. They are a crooked and violent people, without faith or integrity—greedy, gluttonous, drinkers and drunkards, lawless in their conduct, pursuing fornication, bloodshed, theft, treachery, and the violent seizure of another’s property.

**11.** “They condemn without law and without justice, and there is no fear upon their faces, for they do not fear the LORD who made them.

**12.** “They are not ashamed before the face of anyone who sees them, nor before the gray-haired or the aged. When they merely hear of the wealth of this world, they make it their own before they have seen it with their eyes; and once they see it, they imagine that they have already swallowed it with their mouths, because there is no fear of the LORD before their eyes.

**13.** “Their rulers consume what is entrusted to them. What they say in the morning they do not repeat in the evening, for they are faithless and there is no truth in their mouths.

**14.** “Their kings hasten toward evil. They disregard the cries of the poor and needy, and they do not deliver the stranger from the hand of the one who oppresses him.

**15.** “Therefore let kings not be harsh toward those under them, but let them take thought for the stranger and for the oppressed.

**16.** “But these are violent men who drink blood. They show no mercy even to the calf with its mother or the bird with its young, for their whole way is savage; whatever they see and hear they seize for themselves.

**17.** “They have no mercy on the poor and the weak, but love to gather everything for themselves. By violence they seize another’s property, and whatever they find they heap up in their houses so that they may delight in it.

**18.** “Woe to their souls when the LORD seizes them in his wrath because they did not make their way good while they lived. They will disappear quickly like smoke rising from a pit, whose trace is not found and which does not return to its house.

**19.** “So they will perish all at once when the LORD repays them. Yet the LORD does not hurry to destroy them; he is patient, in case they return in repentance. But at their appointed time they will come to an end.

**20.** “If they do not return in repentance, the LORD will destroy them as he destroyed those who came before them, who likewise did not keep the commandment of the LORD as they should.

**21.** “They are eaters of human flesh and drinkers of blood, and the fear of the LORD is never before them. They gird themselves with violence and run toward wrongdoing; from the time they rise from their beds they do not rest from committing injustice.

**22.** “Their work is eating and drinking and running after evil and destruction, so that they ruin the lives of many upon the earth.”
