### Alexander son of Hyrcanus

After the death of Aristobulus, the soldiers removed the chains from his brother Alexander and brought him out of prison.

Alexander took the kingship, and his authority was established.

He learned that the people of Acre and Gaza had rebelled after the death of Aristobulus.

Alexander therefore marched against Acre and laid siege to it.

The people of Acre sent to Lathyrus, son of Cleopatra, asking him to come quickly with an army to help them against Alexander.

Lathyrus had rebelled against his mother Cleopatra and had fled from Egypt to Cyprus.

He sailed to Acre with thirty thousand men in order to aid its inhabitants.

When Alexander heard that Lathyrus was approaching, he withdrew from Acre.

But after Alexander had departed, the people of Acre became afraid of Lathyrus and refused to admit him into the city.

Lathyrus was angered because they had called him to their aid and then shut their gates against him.

The people of Acre then turned to the ruler of Sidon and asked him to help them against Alexander.

He agreed.

When Alexander learned of this, he secretly sent a large sum of money to Lathyrus and asked him to aid him against the ruler of Sidon.

Lathyrus agreed to the request.

Alexander then marched against Sidon, took the city, and plundered it.

He returned to Jerusalem victorious.

### Alexander, Lathyrus, and Cleopatra

After this Alexander secretly sent word to Cleopatra, queen of Egypt:

“Your son Lathyrus, who rebelled against you and whom you have been seeking, has left Cyprus and is now in my land.

“If you desire to take him, come quickly with your army.

“I also will march against him with my army, and the two forces will surround him so that he cannot escape.”

When Lathyrus learned what Alexander had arranged with Cleopatra, he moved into the hill country of Galilee.

He killed many of its inhabitants and caused great destruction there.

Then he advanced against Alexander and came down toward the Jordan.

Alexander heard of his movement and marched from Jerusalem to meet him with six thousand chosen fighting men.

The armies met near the Jordan, and a great battle followed.

Lathyrus prevailed over Alexander.

Many thousands from Alexander's army were killed, and the survivors were scattered.

Alexander fled among the hills and wooded places and afterward returned to Jerusalem in defeat.

The account says that his downfall in that battle came because he trusted too greatly in himself, in his army, and in the number of his soldiers, while placing too little reliance upon God.

Cleopatra had meanwhile come out from Egypt in pursuit of Lathyrus, according to the agreement she had made with Alexander.

When Lathyrus heard that she had arrived, he boarded a ship and escaped again to Cyprus.

Word of his departure reached Cleopatra, and she returned to Egypt.

### Alexander Takes Gaza

In the following year Alexander marched against Gaza.

He captured the city and killed many of its inhabitants because they had previously helped Lathyrus in his war against him.

He burned the sanctuary of the idol which they worshiped and killed the priests who served there.

Afterward Alexander returned to Jerusalem.
