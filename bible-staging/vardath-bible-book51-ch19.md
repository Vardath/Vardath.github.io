## 1 Meqabyan 19

**1.** When the children of Cain multiplied, they made harps, drums, lyres, pipes, and every kind of music and dancing.

**2.** Beautiful and comely daughters were born to Cain from the wife of righteous Abel, whom he killed because of her beauty. After he killed his brother, he took her for himself and made her his possession.

**3.** Separating himself from his father, he took them to the plain of Qefaz toward the west, and the children of that beautiful woman were beautiful like their mother.

**4.** Because of this the angels descended among the children of Cain. When they saw them, they did not remain even one hour before taking for themselves wives whom they chose.

**5.** Because of this the LORD became angry with us and with them, for together we were drawn into error with them because of their transgression.

**6.** The devil also deceived our father Adam and our mother Eve into his error, speaking deceitfully to them and saying, “You will become gods like the LORD your Creator.”

**7.** In their foolishness it seemed true to them, and they transgressed the commandment of their Creator, who brought them from nonexistence into existence so that they might worship and praise his holy name.

**8.** But their Creator humbled Adam and Eve, who sought divine status for themselves, and he also humbled the proud one.

**9.** As David said, “In the pride of the sinner the poor is burned.” Through the pride of the devil our father Adam was condemned by the righteous judgment of the LORD his God.

**10.** In the same way, the angels who went astray after the daughters of Cain drew us into their error. Because of this, the days the LORD has given us are fewer than the days of our fathers.

**11.** Our fathers did what was good and no evil enemy could come near them, for they strengthened their hearts in the LORD and taught their sons and daughters not to depart from the commandment of the LORD that they had been taught.

**12.** But even if people themselves do good, it profits them nothing if they do not tell and teach their children.

**13.** As David said, “They did not hide them from their children for another generation, but declared the praises of the LORD, his power, and the wonders he had done.” They taught their children so that their children might understand, know, and do his covenant, declare the faithfulness of the LORD’s law, and keep his commandment like their fathers who pleased the LORD by their good conduct.

**14.** They did not abandon the commandment their fathers entrusted to them in their youth—the fear of the LORD and his judgment—just as they themselves had learned from their fathers.

**15.** Their children likewise learned from their fathers to order their lives well and offer praise to their Creator, because they kept his commandment and loved him.

**16.** He hears their prayers and does not turn away from their supplication, for he is merciful.

**17.** Though his wrath becomes great, he turns it back from them and does not consume them with the fullness of his punishment.
