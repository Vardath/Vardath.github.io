### The Last Kings of Rome

After Romulus died, Numa Popilius reigned after him for forty-one years. After Numa, Polius reigned thirty-two years. After Polius, Tarquinius reigned thirty-seven years. After him Servius reigned thirty-four years. After Servius, another Tarquinius became king.

This Tarquinius desired a woman of Rome who was already married, and he took her by force. The woman was overwhelmed by what had been done to her. She took a knife in her hand, opened her body with it, and died.

Her husband and her brother went out against Tarquinius. They knew that he was in the temple, and when he entered according to his custom they fell upon him and killed him.

Then the people of Rome swore a solemn oath, binding themselves and the generations after them, that no king should ever again rule over them.
