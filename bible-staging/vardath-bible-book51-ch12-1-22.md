## 1 Meqabyan 12

**1.** O daughter of Jerusalem, this is your shame that was written by the prophet: as were the deeds and sins of the people of Sodom and Gomorrah, so are your deeds and your sins, daughter of Jerusalem.

**2.** Your shame is like that of Sodom and Gomorrah. Their vineyard was planted in fornication and pride.

**3.** No rain of humility and mercy fell upon them, but only the rain of pride and fornication. Their vineyard brought forth nothing except injustice, violence, strife, the shedding of blood, and the forgetting of the name of the LORD their God.

**4.** They did not know their Creator, but only their idols and their evil works. They delighted in the work of their hands and practiced fornication with beasts and with men.

**5.** They did not know the LORD in their deeds, for their eyes were blinded so that they would not see, and their ears were stopped so that they would not hear or do the will of the LORD. They are the vineyard of Sodom and the vine of Gomorrah, which does not bear sweet clusters.

**6.** When their wine is pressed, it becomes a poison that kills, for from its beginning the vine was planted under a curse, and its growth came in a time of destruction.

**7.** At all times there is nothing good in their vine, for their vineyard was watered with the dew of Sheol, and its branches were strengthened by a scorching wind.

**8.** When it bears fruit and is pressed, it becomes corruption and punishment. It hardens the hearts of those who drink from it and makes them contemptible and far from the LORD.

**9.** They become a dwelling place of the devil, because they walk in his evil works; and the vine of Sodom takes its nourishment and climbs among the mountains and the trees.

**10.** She surrounds herself with the objects of worship honored by the peoples around her, and she sacrifices her sons and her daughters to demons that do not know good from evil.

**11.** She pours out innocent blood, and from the clusters of the vineyard of Sodom she continually pours out offerings in the winepresses.

**12.** She worships and honors Dagon of the Philistines and sacrifices to him from her fattened animals and her flocks, delighting in the drunkenness and folly that the demons taught her, so that she might sacrifice to them and perform their will.

**13.** Thus she does not know the LORD her Creator, who feeds her and cares for her at all times, from her youth through the days of her maturity and again to her old age and the day of her death.

**14.** Again I will take vengeance upon her at the resurrection, and her appointed time will endure forever, because she did not return to my law and did not walk in my commandment.

**15.** If her gods are truly gods, let them rise with her and run into Gehenna, and let them save her when I am angry with her in my wrath and remove all her lovers who committed fornication with her.

**16.** I made her despised in all things, just as she brought reproach and shame upon my house and upon my holy things.

**17.** She brought contempt upon my name that was called over her, though people said, “Here is the people of the LORD and the house of the LORD of Israel, the dwelling place of the name of the Most High, Jerusalem, holy among the holy places, the city of the great King.”

**18.** She boasts in me as though she were my servant and I were her Lord, yet she does not fear me or perform my will as her Lord; instead she accuses me as though I were a transgressor.

**19.** She serves foreign gods that do not feed her, cause her to grow, or clothe her. They became a stumbling block to deceive her and to lead her far from me.

**20.** She sacrifices to them and eats their sacrifices. She pours out drink offerings to them and drinks the wine of their offerings. She burns incense to them and takes pleasure in its fragrance. They command her, and she obeys them.

**21.** Again she sacrifices her sons and her daughters, and she exults in the work of her hands and in the labor of her lips, burning with love for them.

**22.** Woe to her on the day of judgment, and woe to the gods whom she loves and embraces. With them she will descend into the lowest Sheol, where the worm does not sleep and the fire is not extinguished.
