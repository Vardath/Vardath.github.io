### Pompey Enters the Temple

Pompey entered the house of God with his commanders.

When he saw the beauty and majesty of the sanctuary, awe came upon him.

He looked upon the money, precious objects, and jewels that were there and marveled at them, but he took nothing.

He summoned the priests who remained and ordered them to bury those who had been killed, cleanse and purify the sanctuary, and restore the sacrifices according to their customary service.

### Hyrcanus Restored and Aristobulus Taken

Pompey then went out and set Hyrcanus over the Jews.

He bound Aristobulus and put many of his supporters to death.

He also removed Jewish authority from the peoples who had been brought under their rule and restored to those peoples the cities, lands, villages, estates, and other possessions that the Jews had taken by the sword.

He imposed upon the Jews a tribute to be paid to Rome every year.

Pompey then departed from Jerusalem toward Rome, leaving Hyrcanus and Antipater in the land.

He took Aristobulus with him in chains together with two of his sons.

Another son of Aristobulus, named Alexander, escaped, and Pompey was unable to capture him.
