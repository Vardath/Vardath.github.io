## 3 Meqabyan 6

**1.** Remember the day of your death, and consider what will come upon you when your soul goes out from your body, when you leave your wealth to another and travel a road whose end you do not know.

**2.** Those who come to meet you will be harsh exactors, terrible in appearance and fearsome in their majesty. They will not listen to your words, nor will you understand theirs.

**3.** Even if you plead with them, they will not heed you, because you did not do the will of the LORD your God. Therefore fear that day greatly.

**4.** But those who have done the will of the LORD have no fear, for they are held in honor; but the souls of sinners are mocked by demons.

**5.** The souls of the righteous rejoice at the voices of the angels, for they are greatly comforted because they renounced this world. But the souls of sinners are met by angels of exaction.

**6.** The souls of the righteous and the good are met by angels of mercy, sent from the LORD to comfort the souls of the righteous. But for the souls of sinners, angels of exaction are sent from the devil to mock them.

**7.** Woe to you, sinners. Weep over yourselves before the day of your death reaches you, for after your death the days that have passed will not return.

**8.** Before your days pass away, repent while you still live, so that you may come before the LORD in joy, free from affliction and pain.

**9.** Do not let evil desire, gluttony, love of pleasure, and love of food rule over you, lest they draw you into empty desire and separate you from the LORD. For a soul filled beyond measure does not remember the name of the LORD; the Spirit of life does not dwell upon it, but the spirit of the devil.

**10.** As Moses said: “Jacob ate and was filled; he grew fat and thick and broad, and he forsook the LORD his Creator.”

**11.** He became far from the LORD who was his life. For the belly filled without measure is like a neighing horse and like the wild creatures of the desert: eating and drinking without restraint, and lust.

**12.** But the one who does not fill his belly without measure stands upon the firm foundation of the LORD, like the fixed horizon and like a strong wall with a rampart. For it is said, “The wicked flee when no one pursues.”

**13.** But the righteous is confident like a lion.

**14.** Those who do not love the LORD do not keep his word, and their heart is not upright.

**15.** The LORD gives them dread and sorrow while they are upon the earth, so that they find no rest: fear and trembling, the loss of their possessions, countless afflictions, and chains upon their hands from the hands of their masters.

**16.** Their own diseases terrify them, so that their dwelling is neither restful nor peaceful.
