### Ṣefo son of Eliphaz

The author says: When Joseph son of Jacob went up to Syria to bury his father, the sons of Esau met him and fought against him. Joseph overcame them and took Ṣefo son of Eliphaz captive, together with a hundred of their men, and brought them with him to Egypt. After Joseph died, Ṣefo fled from Egypt to Agnias, king of Africa. Agnias received him and treated him well.

At that time there was among the Kittim a man called Uṣo, who was held in great honor by them. He died without a son, but he had a daughter named Binah, renowned for her beauty and the fairness of her face. Agnias, king of Africa, sent to the Kittim asking for her in marriage. Turnus, king of Benevento, also sent asking for her.

The Kittim sent to Turnus, saying, “Agnias, king of Africa, has sent to us asking for the same thing that you ask. If we refuse him, we fear that he will invade our land. We have no strength to withstand him, and you are not able to deliver us from him.”

When Agnias learned what had happened, he marched with his army against Turnus. They met in the land of Spain and fought many battles. At first Turnus had the victory, and he killed a number of Agnias’s men. Afterward Agnias prevailed over Turnus, killed him, and overran his army.

Agnias ordered Turnus to be placed in a coffin of bronze and Palos, his sister’s son, in a coffin of gold. He buried them and built two great towers over their graves, facing one another beside the road. They remained standing between Albano and Rome. Then Agnias took Binah daughter of Uṣo and went with her to Carthage, the city of his kingdom.
