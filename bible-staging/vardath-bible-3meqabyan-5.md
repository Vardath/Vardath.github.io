## 3 Meqabyan 5

**1.** Know the one who created you, and do not forget the one who strengthened you and saved you, the Holy One of Israel. Though you are earth, he made you in his image and likeness and placed you in Paradise so that you might delight in it and work the ground.

**2.** But when you transgressed his commandment, he brought you out from there into this dry land, a land of thorns and thistles, which he cursed because of you.

**3.** For you are earth, and it is earth; you are dust, and it is dust; you are soil, and it is soil. From it you are fed, and to it you will return, and you will become dust until the day when he wills to raise you and question you concerning everything you have done—transgression and sin.

**4.** At that time consider what you will answer, and remember here what you have done, both evil and good, and the judgment of whether the evil or the good is greater.

**5.** If you have increased the good, it will be well with you, so that you may rejoice and be comforted at the resurrection of the dead.

**6.** But if you have increased evil, woe to you, for you will be repaid according to the works of your hands, according to the evil of your thought, according to how you despised your brother, and according to how you did not fear the LORD. You will be repaid according to your deeds.

**7.** According to how you deceived your companion and swore falsely by the name of the LORD, you will be repaid according to your deeds.

**8.** For you make falsehood into your word as though it were truth, misleading your companion, while you yourself know that what you speak is false.

**9.** Those who stand with you accept your word as true because you persuade them and multiply speech about what is not true. You will be repaid according to your deeds, and according to how you say, “I will give to you,” where you do not give to your companion, and according to how you deceive another.

**10.** When you say with a pure heart, “I will give,” demons wait for you like dogs and make you forget everything. Even if you have received something and intended to give, the possessions of the world entice you so that you store up what you do not need and what you cannot consume. For it is said, “They store up and do not know for whom they gather.”

**11.** Again it says, “The lying children of men act unjustly with the balance; together they are vanity upon vanity.”

**12.** O people, do not pervert justice with a false balance or with a false measure. Do not seize another person’s possessions and add them to your own by violence, trespassing against the property of your companion and turning everything to your own profit rather than to your companion’s.

**13.** If you do this, you will be repaid according to your deeds.

**14.** O people, do not put your trust in robbery. Instead, feed yourselves by what is upright, through the work of your own hands. Do not desire another person’s labor so that you may eat by theft and seizure without justice and righteousness.

**15.** What does not satisfy you when you eat, and what does not fulfill you when you store it up, you will leave to another when you die.

**16.** If your riches increase, they make your heart proud. A little held in righteousness is better than the great wealth of sinners, for the wealth of sinners is like smoke rising from the mouth of a furnace and carried away by the wind.
