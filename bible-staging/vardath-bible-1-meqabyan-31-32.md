## 1 Meqabyan 31

**1.** Kings are not appointed apart from my will, nor do the poor become poor apart from my command, nor are the mighty made strong apart from my will.

**2.** I am the one who gave favor to David and wisdom to Solomon, and I am the one who added years to Hezekiah.

**3.** I am the one who shortened the days of Goliath. I gave strength to Samson, and afterward I weakened his strength.

**4.** I am the one who delivered David my servant from the hand of Goliath the warrior,

**5.** and also from the hand of Saul the king, and again from the hand of another warrior who opposed him, and from the hands of all who resisted and contended with him, because he kept my commandment.

**6.** I loved him. And all kings and rulers who keep my commandment I also will love; I will give them strength and victory over their enemies, because they have pleased me.

**7.** Again I will give them the land of inheritance which I swore to their fathers, a pure and radiant land, so that they may inherit the land of their fathers.

## 1 Meqabyan 32

**1.** “And you also, kings and rulers, hear my word and keep my commandment,” says the LORD who rules over all. “The children of Israel provoked me and worshiped other gods, although I am the LORD their God, who guarded them, delivered them, caused them to grow from their fathers, and fed them with everything they desired.

**2.** “I raised them upon the strength of the land and fed them with the blessing of the earth: vineyards they had not planted, oil for anointing, and sweet water from wells they had not dug.

**3.** “I gave them flowing honey, sheep’s milk with the finest wheat, adorned garments, and everything they desired.

**4.** “I withheld from them nothing that they asked of me.”
