### Aristobulus and Hyrcanus Contend for the Kingdom

After the death of Alexandra, Aristobulus marched with his army and encamped near the Jordan.

His brother Hyrcanus went out against him with an army drawn from the Pharisees and from those who supported his claim.

The two armies met in battle.

Hyrcanus was defeated and fled back to Jerusalem.

Aristobulus pursued him.

He came against the city, surrounded it with his forces, and prepared to break down its defenses.

Then the priests and the elders of the Jews went out and placed themselves between the brothers.

They pleaded with Aristobulus to stop the fighting and to make peace with Hyrcanus.

Aristobulus agreed.

The brothers established a settlement between them.

Aristobulus was to be king and to hold the governing authority.

Hyrcanus was to remain high priest over the house of God.

They swore to one another and confirmed the agreement.

For a time the affairs of the people and the land became settled, and the internal fighting ceased.

But this peace lasted only until Antipater came between Hyrcanus and Aristobulus and stirred up hostility between the brothers.

From that interference came renewed wars, divisions, and great affliction upon the nation.
