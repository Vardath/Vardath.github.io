## 1 Meqabyan 15

**1.** At that time the people of Midian rose up against them and raised their army to fight Israel. The name of their king was Akrandis, and early in the morning he gathered a great army from Syria, Cilicia, and Damascus.

**2.** He encamped by the Jordan and sent messengers to Israel, saying, “Give me tribute in dinars. If you do not, I will come and conquer you; I will plunder your possessions, take all your livestock, and carry away your children.

**3.** “I will remove you to a land you do not know, and there I will make you cutters of wood and drawers of water.

**4.** “Do not boast, saying, ‘We are the people of the LORD, and no one can overcome us.’ Was it not the LORD who sent me to destroy you, plunder your possessions, and gather up everything that belongs to you?

**5.** “Were the gods of the foreign peoples able to save those whom I judged, whose possessions and livestock I plundered, whom I killed, and whose sons and children I took captive?

**6.** “So also will I do to you if you do not pay me the tribute I commanded.” Then he crossed the Jordan to plunder their possessions and livestock and to carry away their women.

**7.** The children of Israel then wept bitterly before the LORD. They cried out and wailed, seeking one who would help them.

**8.** Because of this the LORD gave strength to three brothers. Their names were Mebkyus, Meqabiyos, and Judah. They were noble in character and strong with all their might.

**9.** When they heard the children of Israel weeping and lamenting, their hearts were grieved for all Israel—for the widows and orphans, the priests and rulers, and every family of Israel, men, women, and children. All were weeping, casting dust upon their heads, and their honored ones were clothed in sackcloth.

**10.** These three good and noble brothers strengthened themselves to go and save them. They took counsel together and said, “Let us go and give our lives over to death for the sake of these people.”

**11.** They went out boldly, with their swords at their sides and their spears in their hands, prepared to meet the enemy.

**12.** They arrived while the enemy king was at his meal. Mebkyus struck him and severed his neck with a single blow while food was still in his mouth. Judah and Meqabiyos struck with the sword those who stood at the right and left of their king.

**13.** When their king fell, all of them scattered and fled. Terror entered the hearts of his companions, their knees gave way, and they were broken.

**14.** But the three good and noble brothers escaped, and no evil reached them. The wicked struck one another, for the LORD had brought punishment upon them.

**15.** They fell and died, abandoning everything that belonged to them as they fled back across the Jordan. When the children of Israel saw that they had scattered and fled with their camp, they took the spoil for themselves.

**16.** Thus the LORD saved the children of Israel by the hand of Mebkyus and his brothers.

**17.** For a few days afterward the children of Israel lived in a way pleasing to the LORD.

**18.** But after this they returned again to their evil and neglected the worship of the LORD. He therefore again brought against them foreign peoples who plundered the produce of their fields, ruined their vineyards, carried away their brides, and milked their cattle before their eyes. They were unable to save themselves, for they were a people who transgressed the law and continually provoked the LORD.
