## 1 Meqabyan 14

**1.** When the LORD punished the first peoples with the waters of the flood because of the sins of the children of Cain, he submerged the earth in the waters of the flood and washed it clean from all the defilement and error of the children of Cain.

**2.** He destroyed all the rebellious, for he said, “I regret what I have done concerning the children of men.” He destroyed them all and left only eight souls. Afterward he multiplied them, and they filled the earth and shared the inheritance of their father Adam.

**3.** Noah made a covenant with the LORD that he would not again destroy the earth with the waters of a flood. The children of Noah also covenanted that they would not eat what had died of itself or become corrupt, and that they would worship no foreign gods apart from the LORD their Creator. He would be their God, would not destroy them suddenly in vain, would not withhold the rains of spring and autumn, and would give food in its season to people and animals—fruit, grass, and plants—while they ordered their lives according to everything the LORD loves.

**4.** After he had given them this ordinance, the children of Israel angered the LORD by their wickedness. They did not walk as their fathers Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob walked, who did not turn aside from the commandment of the LORD their Creator.

**5.** The children of Israel were crooked in their deeds, from the least of them to the greatest, and they did not keep the commandment of the LORD.

**6.** Their Levites, their leaders, and their scribes—all of them transgressed the commandment of the LORD.

**7.** They did not stand firm in the law and ordinance of the LORD that Moses commanded them in the Book of the Law, saying, “Love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul.

**8.** “Love your neighbor as your own soul. Do not worship foreign gods. Do not go in to another man’s wife. Do not kill a person, and do not steal.

**9.** “Do not bear false witness. Do not covet the possessions of your neighbor or anything your brother has acquired, whether his donkey or his ox.”

**10.** Yet after he commanded them these things, the rebellious children of Israel turned back toward sin, deceit, violence, strife, another man’s wife, theft, falsehood, and the worship of idols.

**11.** At Horeb the children of Israel angered the LORD by making the image of a calf that feeds upon grass. They bowed before it and said, “Behold our gods who brought us out of the land of Egypt.”

**12.** They rejoiced in the work of their hands, ate and drank until they were full, and rose up to dance.

**13.** Because of this Moses became angry and came down from Mount Sinai, for the LORD had said to him, “Your people whom you brought out from Egypt, from the house of bondage, have sinned and gone astray. They have made the image of a calf and bowed before the idol.”

**14.** Moses came down with Joshua his attendant, being angry with his people. When Joshua heard the noise, he said, “Behold, I hear the sound of battle in the camp of Israel.”

**15.** Moses said to Joshua, “It is not the sound of battle, but the sound of revelry over wine.” He went down and shattered their image, grinding it until it became like dust, and cast it into the spring from which the children of Israel drank at the foot of the mountain.

**16.** After this he commanded the Levites to fight among their own people because of the evil they had done before the LORD.

**17.** They did as he commanded them, knowing that the anger of the LORD was more terrible than the killing of themselves, their kindred, and their fathers.

**18.** Because of this Moses said to them, “You have appeased the LORD whom you angered—the one who fed you, protected you, brought you out of the house of bondage, and gave you the inheritance that he swore to your fathers to give to them and to their descendants after them.”

**19.** Yet even there they did not cease from provoking the LORD, for they ran toward every evil and sin.

**20.** They were not like their fathers Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, who pleased the LORD by their good conduct from their youth through the days of their maturity and old age, so that he might give them what is on earth and what is in heaven, which he prepared for those who love him: here, the land of inheritance flowing with milk and honey; and there, the paradise of joy prepared for the righteous—what eye has not seen, ear has not heard, and the heart of man has not conceived—which the LORD prepared for those who love him, for Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, who pleased the LORD in their lives.

**21.** But their children were faithless and wicked, walking according to the desire of their hearts. They did not hear the word of the LORD who fed them, nurtured them, and guarded them from their youth.

**22.** They did not remember the LORD who brought them out of the land of Egypt and delivered them from the labor of bricks and from bitter bondage.

**23.** Instead they provoked him greatly, and he raised up against them the foreign nations that surrounded them; these rose against them and made them serve according to their will.
