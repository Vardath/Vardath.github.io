After the Flood, the people of the earth gathered in one place and remained there, and they had one language. God scattered them over the earth and made their tongues and languages differ, so that they became many nations.

When God had dispersed them over the earth, the sons of Kittim went to the land of Spain and settled there. The sons of Tubal went to the land of Yusina and settled there, and they built a city and called it Spain after the name of the builder who built it. The Kittim desired to intermarry with their kinsmen, the sons of Tubal, and asked them to give them their daughters, but the sons of Tubal rejected them and would not give them in marriage.

In one of the years, at harvest time, the sons of Tubal went out to reap their crops, and the city was left without men. A company of the Kittim gathered, went to the city, entered it, seized the girls they found there, and carried them away to their strongholds on an inaccessible mountain.

When the sons of Tubal learned of this, they hurried to the Kittim and fought them, but they could not prevail against them. They withdrew from them that year and returned against them in the following year. When the Kittim learned of their coming, they took the children who had been born to them from the daughters of Tubal during that time and raised them upon the city wall. Then they said to the sons of Tubal, “We do not wish to fight you. If you fight us, you will be fighting your own children and your own kindred who are among us.” So the sons of Tubal ceased from fighting them.
