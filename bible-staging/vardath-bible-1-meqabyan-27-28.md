## 1 Meqabyan 27

**1.** He created the whole world from nothing. He prepared and established the mountains and hills, and he made the earth firm upon the waters so that it would not be shaken, fencing it with sand. For the first word of the LORD was, “Let there be light.”

**2.** And there was light while darkness was over the world. The LORD formed his work, ordering and establishing the world in what was fitting and right, and he said that there should be evening and darkness.

**3.** Again the LORD said, “Let there be light,” and morning came and there was light. He raised the lower waters above the heavens.

**4.** He spread them out like a tent and established them by the wind; and the lower waters he set beneath, in the depths of the sea.

**5.** He shut the gates of the sea with sand and strengthened them by his Spirit so that they would not sink beneath the depths. He placed within the waters wild creatures and living things, and great creatures without number, both those that are seen and those that are unseen.

**6.** On the third day the LORD made plants upon the dry land, every herb and every tree according to its kind, bearing its fruit.

**7.** He made the tree of life, beautiful to behold and sweet for food, and the grasses and all the green growth of the earth having seed within them. He appointed food for every animal, every wild creature, and every bird, and gave nourishment to everything he had created.

**8.** There was evening and morning. On the fourth day the LORD said, “Let there be lights in the firmament of heaven.” The LORD made the sun, the moon, and the stars, and placed them in the firmament of heaven to give light upon the earth and to rule it by day and by night.

**9.** Thus the sun, moon, and stars were appointed for day and night.

**10.** On the fifth day the LORD created all the living creatures and beings that dwell in the heavens, and all the birds that fly above the air, and everything visible and invisible. All these the LORD created.

**11.** On the sixth day he created all the wild beasts, the animals, the creeping things, and all that he had formed and ordered; and he created Adam in his image and in his likeness.

**12.** He entrusted to him all the wild beasts and animals he had made, that he might rule over them, and also all the living creatures, the wild beasts, the fish, and the great creatures that are in the sea.

**13.** He also gave him the sheep and all the cattle upon the earth, and the creatures both visible and invisible.

**14.** He appointed the son of man whom he had made in his image and likeness, and commanded him to eat, to tend the garden, and there to praise the LORD.

**15.** He commanded him not to transgress the commandment of the LORD and not to eat from the tree that reveals good and evil, the tree of death.

**16.** For he said, “On the day that you eat from this tree, you shall surely die.”

**17.** Through the deception of the serpent, our mother Eve was led astray. She ate from that tree and gave it to our father Adam.

**18.** He ate from that tree and brought death upon himself and upon his children.

**19.** The LORD was angry with our father Adam because he transgressed his commandment and ate from the tree from which the LORD had commanded him not to eat. He drove him from the garden and gave him this earth of thorns and thistles, which had been cursed on his account when he transgressed the commandment, so that he should cultivate it and eat the reward of his labor by the sweat of his face.

**20.** When the LORD brought him out into this earth, Adam entered into sorrow and grief of heart. He began to work the ground and to eat through the sweat of his face, with groaning and toil.

## 1 Meqabyan 28

**1.** Afterward they lived and their descendants multiplied. Among them were those who praised and honored the LORD and did not depart from his commandment.

**2.** There were also prophets who foretold what had happened and what would happen, and there were descendants who sinned, lied, and committed violence. Cain, the firstborn of Adam, became violent and killed his brother Abel.

**3.** The wrath of the LORD was kindled against Cain because he killed his brother Abel, and the LORD was also angry with the earth because it had drunk his blood.

**4.** The LORD said to Cain, “Where is your brother Abel?” Cain answered in the pride of his heart, “Am I the keeper of Abel my brother?”

**5.** Abel was righteous, but Cain became a sinner by killing his righteous brother Abel.

**6.** Again righteous Seth was born. Adam begot three daughters and four sons, and among them were both good and evil. *The digital Geʽez source splits this sentence across lines numbered 6, 3, and 4; they are restored here as the single continuous verse 6.*

**7.** Among their descendants were prophets and righteous people, and among them also were sinners and violent people.

**8.** There were good and blessed ones among them who did the will of their father Adam. Everything Adam told his son Seth was handed down from Adam until righteous Noah, who kept the commandment of the LORD.

**9.** Noah in turn charged his children and told them the will of the LORD. They were to tell their own children and charge them according to the will of the LORD, just as their father Noah had told them, so that they would not turn aside from the commandment of the LORD.

**10.** Thus they continued teaching those who came after them, their children.

**11.** But Satan opposed those who obeyed. He spoke to them through idols, through the tombs of their fathers and carved images, and they did everything that this teacher of evil commanded them.

**12.** They worshiped idols according to their customs until righteous Abraham arose, who did the will of the LORD.

**13.** The covenant of the LORD was established with him by fire and whirlwind, for he was the first among his kindred to walk in the path of righteousness.

**14.** The LORD swore to him that he would give the land as an inheritance to him and to his seed forever.

**15.** In the same way he made a covenant with Isaac to give him the inheritance of Abraham his father, and likewise with Jacob to give him the inheritance of Isaac his father.

**16.** After them he made their descendants, the children of Jacob, kings and priests from among the tribes of Israel. He blessed them and said to them, “Be fruitful and multiply exceedingly.”

**17.** He gave them the inheritance of their fathers, yet they did not cease provoking the LORD even while he fed them and cared for them in everything.

**18.** When destruction came upon them, then they sought him; they turned and went to the LORD, for the LORD loved them and had mercy on them.

**19.** He did this not because of them alone, but because of their fathers, for he is merciful and compassionate toward all that he created.

**20.** He stretches out his right hand with blessing to satisfy the hungry soul. He opens his eye in mercy and multiplies grain for food.

**21.** He gives food to the young ravens and to all who call upon him. He also delivers the children of Israel from the hand of their enemies and adversaries when they cry out to him.

**22.** Yet again they return to provoking him, and he sends against them nations and enemies who surround them, destroy them, kill them, and take them captive.

**23.** Then again they cry to the LORD with shouting and tears, and he sends them help. At times he delivered them by the hand of prophets.

**24.** At other times he delivered them by the hand of judges. But when they provoked the LORD, he made their enemies masters over them, and they took them captive and oppressed them.

**25.** David arose and delivered them from the hand of the Philistines. Again they provoked the LORD, and he sent against them those who oppressed them.

**26.** At another time he delivered them by the hand of Jephthah. Again they forgot the LORD who had delivered them, and he sent against them wicked enemies who afflicted them, carried them away, and took them captive, because the LORD repaid them according to their deeds.

**27.** Again they cried out to him when they were humbled, and he delivered them by the hand of Gideon. Again they provoked the LORD by the works of their hands.

**28.** Then he again sent those who afflicted them. They returned, wept, and cried out to the LORD.

**29.** Again he delivered them by the hand of Samson. They rested for a little while, then rose again to provoke the LORD by the evil they had done before.

**30.** Once more he sent others who oppressed them. Again they cried and wept before the LORD that he might send them help, and he delivered them by the hand of Deborah and Barak.

**31.** Again they lived for a few days worshiping the LORD, and again they forgot him and provoked the LORD by returning to their former evil.

**32.** He again sent others who oppressed them, and again he delivered them by the hand of Judith. They rested a little, then rose again to provoke the LORD as before by their evil.

**33.** He raised up nations to judge them. They shouted and cried out to the LORD, and he delivered them by the hand of Mattathias and his sons.

**34.** For he struck down the head of Akemelek the Reʾaytawi, who had come to wage war against the land of Judah. When that warrior died, his army was scattered and fled, and the children of Israel slew them until none remained.

**35.** Afterward they rested for a little while, then rose again to provoke the LORD. He sent nations to rule over them; again they cried and shouted to the LORD, and he was moved by their weeping and their cry, for again and again they provoked the LORD and forgot his commandment.

**36.** They were taken captive and carried away to the land of the Chaldeans together with their priests.

**37.** Even there the wicked children of Israel did not cease provoking the LORD, practicing uncleanness and idolatry and joining themselves to the nations who had taken them captive.

**38.** The LORD was angry there also because of their evil, even to the point of destroying them utterly. He cast wrath upon Artaxerxes, king of the Chaldeans, so that a thousand dinars were to be added to the king’s treasury and judgment was decreed against them on the appointed day, that none of their seed should remain in the land of Persia, from Hindeke as far as Ethiopia.

**39.** Thus a royal letter was written by command of the king, and he gave it the seal that was in his hand so that it would reach every region of Persia.

**40.** They were to kill them in a single day, as the king had commanded, intending to wipe them out; and their gold and silver plunder was ordered to be brought into the king’s treasury.

**41.** When the children of Israel heard this, they cried, wept, and called upon the LORD. They told Mordecai, and Mordecai told Esther.

**42.** Esther said, “Fast, pray, and cry out to the LORD, the God of Israel—all the congregation of the children of Israel, wherever you are.”

**43.** Mordecai put on sackcloth and placed ashes upon his head. They fasted, prayed, and repented wherever the children of Israel were.

**44.** Esther humbled herself greatly. Though she was queen, she clothed herself in sackcloth, spread ashes upon herself, let down her hair, and did not anoint herself with perfume as the queens of Persia did. She wept and cried to the LORD from the depth of her heart, to the God of her fathers.

**45.** Because of this the LORD gave her favor before Artaxerxes, king of Persia, and she made a great banquet before the God of her fathers.

**46.** The king and Haman entered the banquet that Esther had prepared. There the LORD repaid Haman according to what he had intended to do to Mordecai, and they hanged him upon a high tree.

**47.** A royal decree was issued that the Jews, wherever they were, should be left in freedom: they were not to be enslaved, plundered, oppressed, or deprived of their possessions.

**48.** Their cities were not to be destroyed, nor their livestock taken. They were to be loved and honored wherever they lived throughout all the land of Persia, for in this way the LORD showed mercy to Israel when they petitioned him and cried out to him in repentance.

**49.** But whenever they provoked him, he sent against them nations who oppressed them. Then they wept, cried, and called out that he might send them help and deliver them from the hand of those who afflicted them.
