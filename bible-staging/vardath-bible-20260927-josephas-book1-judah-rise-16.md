### The Death of Mattathias and the Rise of Judah Maccabee

When Mattathias became old and knew that his death was near, he called his five sons to him.

He said:

“My sons, the struggle in Judah will become fiercer because we have risen to defend our people.

“Be zealous for God, for his sanctuary, and for his people.

“Do not fear death when you contend for what is right. Remember those among our fathers who were faithful in their generations and received honor from God.”

Then Mattathias spoke to Simon.

“My son, God has given you wisdom and understanding. Give counsel to your brothers and to the people. Be to them as a father, and let them listen to your judgment.”

After this he called Judah.

He said:

“My son Judah, you are called Maccabee because of your strength. God has given you courage and the heart of a warrior.

“Use the strength you have received in the service of God.

“Go through the land in every direction and contend against those who oppress the people.

“Be the commander of your brothers and of those who fight with you.”

Mattathias then blessed his sons and strengthened them for the work that remained.

When he had finished speaking, he died and was gathered to his fathers.

Judah, called Maccabee, rose in his place.

His brothers, the household of his father, and the company of the faithful joined him.

Judah armed himself for war and went out against the enemies of Israel.

He became feared among those who enforced the decrees of Antiochus.

He pursued those who attacked the people, struck the forces sent against him, and strengthened the camps of Judah.

His name spread through the land because of the battles he fought and because he risked his life for his people.

Thus Judah son of Mattathias became the second leader of the Maccabean house in the struggle against the Greek kings.
