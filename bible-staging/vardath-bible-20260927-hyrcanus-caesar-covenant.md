### Hyrcanus Sends to Caesar

Hyrcanus, ruler of the Jews, sent messengers to Caesar, ruler of the Romans.

He gave them a letter asking Caesar to renew the covenant that had existed between Hyrcanus and Rome.

When the messengers of Hyrcanus came to Caesar, he received them with honor.

He allowed them to sit in his presence, unlike the ordinary custom by which envoys from other kings were received.

He listened to their requests, dealt kindly with them, and answered the letter of Hyrcanus.

Caesar then wrote a covenant concerning Hyrcanus and the Jews.

### The Covenant of Caesar

Caesar wrote in this manner:

“From Caesar, king of kings, to the Roman authorities in Tyre and Sidon: peace.

“The letters of Hyrcanus son of Alexander have reached me, and I was pleased by their arrival.

“I have learned the truth of the friendship that he and his people have shown toward Rome.

“His loyalty has been made known especially through Antipater, whom he sent to assist Mithridates when the Egyptian forces came against him.

“Antipater labored in our service, rescued our ally from danger, and helped bring the Egyptians into obedience.

“He afterward continued in my service in the eastern lands.

“Because of these things I establish this covenant with Hyrcanus and the Jews.

### Tribute for the House of God

“I command that the inhabitants of the coast from Gaza as far as Sidon deliver each year to the great house of God in Jerusalem the tribute that is required from them.

“I also command that the people of Sidon provide to this same house of God twenty thousand measures of wheat each year according to the obligation laid upon them.

### Restoration of the Lands

“I command that Laodicea and its districts be restored, together with the other territories that formerly belonged to the Jews as far as the bank of the Euphrates.

“The places won by the Maccabees, the sons of the Hasmoneans, and the lands formerly held by the Jews are likewise to be restored to Hyrcanus son of Alexander, ruler of the Jews.

“Their fathers had taken these places by the sword.

“Pompey later removed territories from the hands of Aristobulus, Hyrcanus, and the rulers who followed them.

“I now order that these possessions be returned to Hyrcanus and the Jews.

### The Covenant to Remain

“This covenant is established by me and is to remain in force under every ruler who comes after me over Rome.

“Whoever opposes it, breaks it, changes it, or removes anything from it shall bring a curse upon himself, upon his children, and upon his land.

“When you have read this letter, copy the covenant upon tablets of bronze in the language of the Romans and in Greek.

“Set the bronze tablets openly in the great temple of Zeus, which is Jupiter, at Tyre and Sidon.

“Let every person who sees them know the covenants and agreements that I have established for Hyrcanus and the Jews.”

In this manner Caesar renewed the covenant and ordered it to be publicly preserved.
