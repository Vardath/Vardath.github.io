### The Beginning of Herod Son of Antipater

When Antipater saw the weakness of Hyrcanus and the uncertainty of his resolve, he and his sons took greater control of the affairs of the kingdom.

Antipater appointed his son Phasael over Jerusalem.

He placed his other son Herod over Galilee.

Herod was at that time sixteen years old.

From then on much of the power of government was in the hands of Antipater and his sons, while Hyrcanus retained chiefly the name of rule.

### Hezekiah and His Company

There was in those days a man named Hezekiah.

He was bold and powerful and had gathered around him a company of men like himself.

They repeatedly went into the lands of Syria.

They plundered property, killed men, and ravaged the country.

Their attacks became a serious burden upon those who lived in the regions under Syrian authority.

Sextus Caesar, a kinsman of Caesar who had authority in Syria, learned what Hezekiah and his companions were doing.

He feared the growing harm that they were causing and wrote to Herod son of Antipater.

He said:

“You know the favor that Caesar has shown to your father.

“You also know your father's obedience to Caesar, his service to him, and the wars in which he fought against Caesar's enemies.

“It is fitting that you follow your father in the same course.

“You have heard what Hezekiah and his companions are doing to the people under my authority.

“I therefore ask you to act against him and deliver us from his violence.

“If you do this, Caesar will remember your service and show you favor, and we also will not fail to reward you.”

### Herod Kills Hezekiah

When the letter reached Herod, he went out from Galilee with his companions.

He found Hezekiah returning from the land of Syria and not expecting an attack.

Herod fell upon him suddenly.

Hezekiah was killed together with many of the men who were with him.

Herod then sent word to Sextus Caesar and reported what he had done.

Sextus sent him a large amount of money.

Leading Romans also sent him valuable gifts and thanked him for his action.

The position of Herod therefore became stronger.

His name spread widely, and the number of men who followed him increased.

### The Jews Complain to Hyrcanus

When the Jews learned that Hezekiah had been killed, the matter weighed heavily upon them.

They gathered before Hyrcanus and said:

“How long will you continue to endure what Antipater and his sons are doing?

“The kingdom has passed into their hands.

“They govern it, act within it, and do whatever they choose.

“You possess little more than the name of king.

“Antipater wins favor with the rulers of Rome by his gifts and alliances.

“His son Phasael acts within your own city without your knowledge and without seeking your counsel.

“His other son Herod has taken control of Galilee.

“Yet he was not satisfied with this.

“He has killed Hezekiah, the mighty man whom the surrounding peoples feared.

“Hezekiah was a benefactor to his own people, and even if he had committed offences, he had not first been condemned by lawful judgment to death.

“Herod killed him in order to gain favor with foreign rulers and to obtain their gifts.

“In doing this he acted unjustly and made himself liable to judgment.

“It is therefore your duty, O king, to summon him before the judges so that he may answer for what he has done.”

They repeated these words to Hyrcanus continually.

The families of the young men who had been killed with Hezekiah also came before him.

Their women stood near the gates crying out, lamenting, and asking that Herod be made to answer for the blood that had been shed.

At last Hyrcanus yielded to their continual complaints.

He wrote to Herod and commanded him to come quickly to Jerusalem.

### Herod Appears before the Council

Herod came from Galilee with a company of his followers.

He appeared before the council of the seventy elders.

He did not come dressed as a humbled accused man.

His clothing was splendid.

His hair was carefully arranged.

A weapon was fastened upon him.

The men who accompanied him were also well dressed and armed.

When the elders saw Herod and his companions in this manner, fear entered them.

Those who had spoken against him while he was absent became silent when he stood before them.

No one wished to repeat openly all that had been said against him before.

The elders, Hyrcanus, and Herod continued in discussion until it appeared that the judgment might turn against Herod.

### Hyrcanus Delays the Judgment

When Hyrcanus saw what was happening, he said to the elders:

“It is better that we stop this matter for today.

“Tomorrow we will meet again and consider it.”

Hyrcanus said this because he wished to delay the judgment and prevent an immediate sentence of death against Herod.

He also intended to give Herod an opportunity to escape.

### The Warning of Shammai

A man named Shammai, a disciple of Hillel, was present among the elders.

When he saw what had happened, he rebuked the council.

He said:

“Have you not always seen that a man who is brought before judges because of a grave accusation comes humbly?

“He wears dark clothing.

“His hair is disordered.

“He lowers himself before the court and seeks mercy.

“But look at this young man Herod.

“He has come in the opposite manner.

“He has not shown the humility of an accused man.

“He stands before you in fine clothing with his hair arranged, and armed men surround him.

“He has done this because he despises this court and because he has resolved that if judgment goes against him, he will defend himself by force.

“He intends to overcome the judgment and save himself from death.

“I do not speak these words against Herod alone.

“I speak also against you, O judges, because you have allowed him to appear before you in this manner.

“You have been frightened by him.

“You have fallen silent.

“You have failed to pronounce judgment according to what is right.

“Therefore I tell you that a time will come when this Herod will rule over you.

“He will shed your blood and destroy your houses.

“He will not spare Hyrcanus, who has favored him and protected him from judgment.

“He will take the kingdom for himself.”

### Herod Leaves Jerusalem

Hyrcanus remained determined to postpone the matter.

The elders and all who had assembled departed.

During the third part of the night, Herod secretly left Jerusalem.

He went into the land of Syria and came to Sextus Caesar.

Sextus received him and advanced him.

He placed Herod in authority over the Syrians.
