## 1 Meqabyan 6

**1.** Concerning the LORD it is written for kings: “Righteousness, righteousness,” for in righteousness he establishes the kings who do his will.

**2.** He causes their dwelling to shine with an indescribable light, where Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, David, Solomon, Hezekiah, and all the good kings dwell, because they did the will of the LORD. Their dwelling is not like an earthly palace, but a palace in heaven, exceedingly awesome and pure, its pavement gleaming with gold, silver, and precious stones.

**3.** Its appearance surpasses human thought. The palace of the kingdom of heaven shines with precious stones—pazion, meregdi, telma, and werawre.

**4.** It is adorned with every precious jewel beyond human imagining. The royal palace shines exceedingly; its maker fashioned it according to his wisdom. The pavement of the kingdom of heaven is pure and gleams with gold, silver, yākint, ahder, asfar, stones of many colors, and melat.

**5.** Thus it is beautiful, exceedingly radiant, pure, and full of light.

**6.** It is founded in righteousness and justice and fashioned in compassion and mercy.

**7.** There flows the water of life, exceedingly pure like the sun, and there is a dwelling of light encircled by the heights of Hermon.

**8.** Around its house are planted the sweet fruits of paradise, each with its own appearance and fragrance, together with vineyards, olives, oil-bearing trees, and every good and fragrant fruit.

**9.** When a mortal enters there, the sweetness and fragrance of its fruit are such that the soul is overwhelmed by the abundance of joy that is upon it.

**10.** There the good kings who did the will of the LORD rejoice. Their entrance and their dwelling are known: they stand in the place of eternal life in the kingdom of heaven.

**11.** What is seen in heaven has its likeness on earth. As their kingdoms were great upon the earth, and as people honored them and bowed before them, so in heaven they are honored, exalted, and made glad—if they did good during their lives upon the earth.

**12.** But those who were unjust in their kingdoms and in the authority the LORD gave them, who did not judge with righteousness and justice and did not rescue the oppressed, the stranger, or the orphan, because they ignored the cry of the poor and needy—

**13.** who did not deliver the poor and needy from the hand of those who oppressed them, who did not feed the hungry from their provisions, who did not give drink to the thirsty from their drink, and who did not turn their ears toward the cry of the children of men—

**14.** these also will be taken into the dwelling of Sheol, into the deepest darkness. As great as their honor was, so much greater will be their humiliation and disgrace when that great day made by the LORD and his wrath comes upon them. As David said in the Psalm, “O LORD, do not rebuke me in your wrath, nor discipline me in your anger.”

**15.** But as for those who keep your law while they are kings, rulers, and governors of the world upon the earth—

**16.** in heaven there is One who rules all. The souls of all are in his hand, and the life of all is in his mouth. He gives honor to those who honor him and loves those who love him, for he rules all and judges all.

**17.** He examines the heart and the inward parts, for he is King of heaven and earth. He answers the prayer of the one who prays to him with a pure heart.

**18.** He breaks the pride of the mighty who oppress widows and orphans.

**19.** It was not by your own power that you received this kingdom, nor by your own ability that you sat upon this throne. He himself willed it and seated you on the throne of the kingdom to test whether you would judge his people rightly, unlike Saul in his day, who ruled the people but disobeyed the word of the LORD and the word of Samuel the prophet and did not destroy the king of Amalek and his people.

**20.** For the LORD said to Samuel the prophet, “Go and tell Saul: Go and attack the land of Amalek and destroy all their kings and inhabitants, from man to beast, because they have provoked me by transgressing the law, by worshiping gods and bowing to idols, by their abominations, and by every unclean and worthless deed.”

**21.** Because they had provoked the LORD, he sent Saul to destroy them.

**22.** But Saul spared their king and many of their animals, as well as women and virgins, keeping them for the young men. Therefore the LORD said to Samuel the prophet, “He has rebelled against my word and has not obeyed my command. Go and strip him of his kingdom.”

**23.** Then Samuel anointed David the son of Jesse to reign in Saul’s place over Israel.

**24.** And an evil spirit came upon Saul and tormented him.

**25.** For the LORD had given him the kingdom so that he would do his will; but when he refused to do his will, the LORD removed it from him. He said to Samuel, “Go and tell him: Is this how you repay the LORD who made you king over his people Israel and seated you upon the throne of his kingdom?”

**26.** You did not understand how great an honor and greatness he had given you.

**27.** Samuel the prophet went to King Saul and came to him while he was standing at the table, with Agag king of Amalek beside him.

**28.** Samuel said to him, “Why have you disobeyed the LORD, who commanded you to destroy both the people and the animals?”

**29.** Then Saul was afraid and rose from his seat. He seized Samuel’s garment, but as Samuel turned away, the garment tore.

**30.** Samuel said to Saul, “The LORD has torn the kingdom away from you.”

**31.** Saul again said to Samuel, “Honor me before the people, and intercede for me before the LORD so that he may forgive me.” But Samuel rejected his plea, for he feared the word of the LORD his Creator and did not fear a mortal king.

**32.** Therefore Samuel struck Agag king of Amalek while food was still in his mouth.

**33.** As for Saul, the one who had rejected the word of the LORD, an evil spirit seized him and struck his mind, for the LORD does not show partiality to a king who commits rebellion. He is the King of kings who commands all.

**34.** There is no one who commands him, for he is Lord of all creation. He removes all authority from kings and rulers who do not fear him.

**35.** He also removed the kingdom from Saul and from his son, just as it is said: “The house of David shall go upward, but the house of Saul shall go downward.”

**36.** The LORD took vengeance on the seed of the house of Saul because Saul had rebelled against him and had not destroyed those enemies who had provoked the LORD by their evil deeds. For whoever refuses to take vengeance on the enemy of the LORD makes himself an enemy of the LORD.

**37.** Whoever refuses to punish the sinner when he is able and has authority to do so is truly an enemy of the LORD, because he does not uphold the law of the LORD.
