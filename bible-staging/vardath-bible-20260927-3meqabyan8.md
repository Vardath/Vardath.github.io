## 3 Meqabyan 8

**1.** Job trusted in the LORD, and he delivered him from all his affliction that the devil, the enemy of humankind, had brought upon him. He did not complain, but blessed the LORD his God, and his heart was not saddened by the affliction that came upon him. He said, “The LORD gave, and the LORD has taken away. As the LORD willed, so it has been. In everything, blessed be the name of the LORD in heaven and on earth.”

**2.** When the LORD saw that Job’s heart was pure, the LORD received him with great honor.

**3.** He gave him wealth greater than the wealth he had before, and he healed him from his wounds because of his patience in everything that came upon him, for he was exceedingly patient.

**4.** Likewise, if you also are patient under what adversaries send upon you, you shall be blessed.

**5.** Therefore endure, and do not let your hearts be troubled by the affliction that comes upon you, so that the LORD may be a shelter for you from those who oppose you, and also for your children and for the children of your children after you.

**6.** Trust in him, and he will be your shelter. Call upon him, and he will hear you. Hope in him, and he will be merciful to you. Ask him, and he will be a father to you.

**7.** Remember Esther and Mordecai, Judith and Gideon, Barak and Deborah, Samson and Jephthah.

**8.** And others like them strengthened themselves in trusting in the LORD, and their enemies did not overcome them.

**9.** Those who intend evil against them he repays with evil, for the LORD is righteous and shows no partiality. As for all who fear him and practice righteousness, he preserves their souls and gives them honor and favor.

**10.** He makes them rejoice in their coming in and their going out, in their death and in their life, in their dwelling and in their rising, for he gives life and he kills.

**11.** He chastens, and he has mercy.

**12.** He burns, and he gives food; he humbles, and he exalts.
