**23.** Woe to you, wretched daughter of Jerusalem, for you abandoned me who created you and worshiped foreign gods.

**24.** I will repay you according to your deeds and take vengeance upon you according to your rebellion, because you angered me, despised my word, and did not make your deeds good.

**25.** I will delay toward you and will not hasten your deliverance, because you rebelled against my word and did not remain faithful to the covenant that you made with me: that you would keep my commandments, that I would remain with you and save you from all who opposed you, and that you would keep the ordinances I commanded you.

**26.** You kept none of these things. Therefore I turned away from you, and at the judgment I will take vengeance upon you. Did I not create you? Yet you did not keep my word or my commandment, though I blessed you so that you might be mine.

**27.** You made yourself a stranger to me, just as Sodom and Gomorrah made themselves strangers to me.

**28.** I destroyed them in my wrath and rooted them out. You have become as though from the same root as Sodom and Gomorrah, whom I destroyed and whose remembrance I wiped from the earth so that they would not continue in their corruption, because they angered me who created them. A man went after another man’s wife, and they committed fornication outside the law with men and with animals as with women.

**29.** There was no fear of the LORD before their eyes, from the least of them to the greatest. No one rebuked another so that he might cease; instead they helped one another in every evil deed and were filled with rebellion, violence, and all wrongdoing, for their works were evil.

**30.** Every evil work was established in their thoughts—pride, strife, and greed.

**31.** Therefore the LORD abandoned them and overturned their cities. Some he burned with fire down to their foundations, and none remained within them; they were destroyed and rooted out forever.

**32.** They will remain bound in their sins until the day of judgment, even to the great day of judgment. He will neither pity nor spare them, because they angered me with their evil deeds.

**33.** I abandoned them; and likewise you, wretched daughter of Jerusalem, will be bound on the day of judgment. You will find no excuse when I seize you in my wrath, because all your deeds are violence, strife, greed, fornication, false speech, every path of error, and uncleanness that I do not love.

**34.** I made you for honor, but you made yourself for shame. I said that you were mine, but you made yourself another’s.

**35.** I chose you for holiness, but you became the devil’s. Shall I not take vengeance upon you according to the evil of your deeds?

**36.** I will increase severe punishment upon you because you did not hear my words or keep the commandments that I gave you when I loved you. For I am the LORD who created you, and I will be angry with all sinners like you and repay them according to their wickedness on the day of judgment.

**37.** I will judge you together with them, because you did not keep my word and you despised my judgment.

**38.** Woe to you, Sodom and Gomorrah, for there is no fear of the LORD in your hearts.

**39.** Likewise you, city of Jerusalem, will be condemned together with them in the fire of Gehenna, and all who have done deeds like yours will descend together to the Gehenna prepared for them, from which there is no escape forever.

**40.** You and she, because you did not keep my word and my commandments, will descend together into Sheol on the day of judgment.

**41.** But those who kept my word and my commandment will receive what sinners stored up; the righteous will eat, and the good will share in what the wicked acquired, according as the LORD commanded. The righteous and the good will rejoice and be glad.

**42.** But sinners and wrongdoers will weep and grieve over all the errors by which they strayed from my commandment.

**43.** The one who keeps my word and walks in my commandment is the one who will receive blessing and be honored before me.

**44.** Everyone who keeps my word and performs my commandment will eat the blessing of the land and will enter and dwell where the good and righteous kings dwell.
