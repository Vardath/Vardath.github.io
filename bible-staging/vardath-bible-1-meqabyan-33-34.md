## 1 Meqabyan 33

**1.** “Again I fed them with manna in the wilderness, with bread, as David said: ‘The children of men ate the bread of his angels.’ All these things I did for them so that they might worship me in righteousness and uprightness.

**2.** “But they did not worship me, and I rejected them,” says the LORD who rules over all. “They provoked me and walked in a way that was not mine.

**3.** “I will repay them according to their conduct and according to the work of their hands. I rejected them because they grew weary of worshiping me and would not remain in my ordinances and my counsel; and I will bring them down into the judgment decreed in heaven.

**4.** “On earth also I will shorten their days, because they did not keep my commandment and I became angry with them.

**5.** “And you, O king, are you not a man who will die and decay? Tomorrow you will become as wood and plants and dust.

**6.** “Yet now you boast and exalt yourself as though you would never die.

**7.** “You are a man who appears alive today and tomorrow is dead,” says the LORD who rules over all.

**8.** “But if you keep my word and my commandment, I will give you the holy city as an inheritance together with those who do my will—the glorious kings whose dwelling is bright, whose crowns are beautiful, and whose thrones of gold and silver are prepared for those who sit upon them.

**9.** “You shall rejoice in that city, which is a prepared dwelling for those who do good.

**10.** “But those who do evil are not worthy to enter that city into which the glorious kings enter,

**11.** because they did not keep my word and my ordinance,” says the LORD who rules over all.

## 1 Meqabyan 34

**1.** The kingdom of Media will pass away, and the kingdom of Rome will become exceedingly strong over the kingdom of Macedonia, the kingdom of Nineveh, and the kingdom of Persia.

**2.** The kingdom of Ethiopia will rise over the kingdom of Alexandria, and the kingdom of Moab over Amalek, for they will rise up.

**3.** Brother will rise against brother, and the LORD will execute vengeance among them according to what he has spoken, until they consume one another.

**4.** For it is said: “Kingdom will rise against kingdom, people against people, and city against city.

**5.** “There will be upheaval, killing, famine, conflict, confusion, and the violence of the seasons, for peace and love will be taken away and the punishment of the LORD will descend upon the earth.

**6.** “The day of the LORD will come suddenly, like lightning that is seen from the east even to the west.

**7.** “Then everyone will be repaid according to his deeds, according to the labor of his hands and the fruit of his conduct. For the LORD himself has said, ‘On the day of judgment I will take vengeance upon them, and in due time their foot will slip, for the day of their end has drawn near.’

**8.** “At that time the LORD will bring to an end the workers of evil who do not walk in his law, casting them into everlasting judgment: the people of Judah, Cyprus, Samaria, Damascus, and the people of Babylon,

**9.** “and those who dwell in the islands, Arabia, Hindeke, Noba, Saba, Egypt, Ethiopia, and all who dwell in their territories.

**10.** “Then they will know that I am the LORD who judges heaven and earth; I am the one who gives honor and favor, and I am the one who kills and gives life.

**11.** “I am the one who makes the sun rise and sends it toward its setting, and I am the one who brings good and evil.

**12.** “I am the one who brings against you a people whom you do not know, who will eat the fruit of your labor and the produce of your cattle and your sheep.

**13.** “They will slaughter your children before your eyes and carry them away captive, and you will not be able to deliver them. They will consume your pasture and your green growth because you did not fear the commandment of the LORD which you heard, and because the Spirit of the LORD did not rest upon you.

**14.** “But the one upon whom the Spirit of the LORD dwells knows all things, as Nebuchadnezzar said to Daniel the prophet: ‘I see that the Spirit of the LORD is upon you.’

**15.** “For the one who has the Spirit of the LORD knows all things. Even what is hidden becomes open to him, and nothing is concealed from the Spirit of the LORD; he knows both the hidden and the revealed.

**16.** “For we are human beings: today we live and tomorrow we die, and all the deeds we have done in secret will be revealed.

**17.** “As gold and silver are tested by fire, so sinners will be tested on the last day because they did not keep the commandment of the LORD.

**18.** “Then the deeds of all the children of Israel and of all the nations will be tested.”
