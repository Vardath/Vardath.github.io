## 2 Meqabyan 5

**1.** Meqabis left behind young children. They grew up according to the order their father had established for them, kept the discipline of their household, guarded all that belonged to them, and did not oppress the poor, the orphan, or the widow.

**2.** They feared the LORD, gave from their possessions to the poor, kept everything their father had entrusted to them, comforted widows and orphans in their affliction, became to them as father and mother, rescued them from the hands of their oppressors, and consoled them in every trouble and sorrow that befell them.

**3.** They continued living and doing these things for five years. *The digital Geʽez source splits “five years” into a stray line numbered 5 before verse 4; the phrase is restored here as the completion of verse 3.*

**4.** After this, Ṣiruṣaydan king of the Chaldeans came and destroyed all their cities. He took the children of Meqabis captive and devastated all their country.

**5.** He plundered all their possessions, seized them, and carried them into the land of the idol-worshipers. Those people did not walk in the commandment or judgment of the LORD, but in every evil thing—in fornication, uncleanness, greed, violence, and blasphemy—and they did not remember their God.

**6.** They ate carrion, blood, flesh of reptiles, torn flesh, and everything that does not please the LORD. They had no law from among the righteous commandments written in the Torah.

**7.** They did not know the LORD as their Creator and Savior, the one who brought them forth from their mothers’ wombs and fed them with what was fitting.

**8.** They had no law of justice. They walked in fornication, strife, sexual immorality, and uncleanness. They took their fathers’ wives and their mothers’ sisters; they took their own sisters and their relatives, and they had no law.

**9.** Their whole way was slippery, dark, unclean, and full of fornication.

**10.** But the children of Meqabis guarded themselves from all these forbidden things. They did not eat carrion, torn flesh, or any of the uncleanness and sins of the Chaldeans. Their evil deeds were many—too many to be written in this book—the deeds of those Aramaean people, sinners and lawless men, unbelieving and violent, full of filth, uncleanness, and strife.

**11.** There was nothing among their deeds that pleased the LORD their Creator.

**12.** They worshiped an idol named Baal-Peor and trusted in it as though it were the LORD their Creator, though it was mute and deaf, unable to see or hear. It was a carved image, the work of human hands, plated with gold and silver—a work of mortals that had neither knowledge nor breath.

**13.** It does not eat and does not drink.

**14.** It does not kill and does not give life.

**15.** It does not plant and does not uproot.

**16.** It does not harm and does not do good.

**17.** It does not make poor and does not make rich.

**18.** It does not chastise and does not show mercy. It is only a snare that deceives the foolish people of the Chaldeans.

## 2 Meqabyan 6

**1.** The proud Ṣiruṣaydan appointed false priests and ministers for his gods in opposition to the LORD.

**2.** They sacrificed to those gods and poured out offerings before them.

**3.** It seemed to him that they were the ones who ate and drank.

**4.** He gave them the firstborn of cattle and sheep, goats and kids. They sacrificed evening and morning, and he ate from that unclean sacrifice.

**5.** They did not practice these things by themselves alone, but compelled other people and forced them to sacrifice to their gods.

**6.** When they saw that the children of Meqabis were upright and worshiped the LORD their Creator, the false ministers of the gods tried to lead them astray so that they would sacrifice and eat from the unclean offering. But these blessed ones refused them.

**7.** They could not persuade them, because they kept their father’s commandment, stood firm in doing good, and feared the LORD exceedingly.

**8.** So they slandered them, reviled them, and oppressed them.

**9.** They told Ṣiruṣaydan the king that the children of Meqabis had refused to bow down and sacrifice to his gods.

**10.** Because of this the king became angry and grieved. He commanded that they be brought before him. When they were brought and stood before him, he said to them, “Sacrifice and bow down to my gods.”

**11.** They answered him, “We will not obey you in this matter, and we will not sacrifice to your unclean gods.”

**12.** He tortured them in many ways, but he could not overcome them, because they had made their hearts steadfast toward the LORD.

**13.** He kindled a fire and cast them into it, and they gave their souls to the LORD.

**14.** After their death they appeared alive to him, holding their swords, while he slept upon his royal bed at night; and he became exceedingly afraid.

**15.** He said to them, “What shall I do for you, my lords? Leave me and do not take my life. I will do everything you command me.” And they terrified him greatly.

**16.** They told him everything that was fitting, saying, “Remember that the LORD is your Creator. There is one who will strip you of this pride and bring you down into the lowest Gehenna with the devil your father. There you will complete all your judgment, because you burned us with fire although we had committed no offense against you, but worshiped the LORD our Creator and bowed before him in the fear of the LORD.

**17.** “For he is the Creator of all, who made heaven and earth and the sea and everything in them:

**18.** “the sun, the moon, the stars, and every created thing that the LORD himself created.

**19.** “There is no other God besides him in heaven above or on the earth beneath. He is able to do all things, and nothing is impossible for him. He kills and gives life; he chastens and shows mercy.

**20.** “There is none who can escape from his hand, for he is ruler of heaven and earth.

**21.** “Nothing that he created transgresses his commandment except you and those who mock like you, whose hearts Satan your father has blinded. You and your gods will descend together into Gehenna, from which there is no escape forever.

**22.** “You did not do this by yourself alone, but Satan your teacher taught you this evil deed so that you would insult us.

**23.** “You did not know the LORD your Creator, but made yourself out to be like the LORD who created you.

**24.** “You exalted yourself in the works of your hands and in your idols until the LORD brings you low and sends you down to the depths of Gehenna, taking vengeance upon you for every offense and sin you committed upon the earth.”
