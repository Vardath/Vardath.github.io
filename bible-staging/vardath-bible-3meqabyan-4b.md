**19.** “When you rebelled against the praise of his holy name and exalted yourself, he created Adam his servant so that he might be praised through the humble one whom he formed from the earth.

**20.** “For the LORD hears the prayer of the poor from afar, and the praise of the humble is pleasing to the LORD.

**21.** “But the speech of the proud the LORD rejects, for he does not delight in the strength of a horse, nor is he pleased in the power of a man; the LORD is pleased with those who fear him.

**22.** “They weep and cry aloud because of the sins they have committed.

**23.** “But you were unable to bow yourself in repentance.

**24.** “Yet he who was earth returned in repentance, weeping and lamenting because of his sin before the LORD his Creator.

**25.** “But because of the pride of your heart and the stiffness of your neck, you did not know the way of peace or repentance, and you were unable to humble yourself before the LORD your Creator with repentance, weeping, and tears.

**26.** “But he who was earth and dust, poor and lowly, returned with weeping and tears into repentance, into the way of peace and humility.

**27.** “But you did not humble yourself, nor did you bend your heart before the one who created you.

**28.** “He, however, humbled himself, bowed himself because of his transgression, and did not exalt himself.

**29.** “And his error did not arise from himself, but from you; for you conceived and rooted rebellion and sought to carry him with you into destruction through your pride.

**30.** “He himself knows that this came through the pride of your heart, for before he created both of you he knew you and knew your ways.

**31.** “As for him, because pride had not become rooted within him, he returned through repentance, through weeping, and through tears.

**32.** “But because of the pride of your heart you were unable to bow yourself in repentance. Whoever sins and then humbles himself turns away the evil pride of his former transgression; whoever sins and then bows himself and weeps before the LORD his Creator—

**33.** “He who truly repents finds the way of salvation, so that the heart of his Lord may show compassion toward him. He bows before his Master and his Lord, and the LORD turns from the punishment that had been prepared, because his servant has humbled himself before him with abundant repentance and prostration; and he forgives him his former error.

**34.** “If he does not return to his former transgression, this is complete repentance. Adam did not forget the thought of his God, but humbled himself in repentance before the one who created him.

**35.** “You also, humble yourself before him and repent to the one who created you. Do not place blame upon him or upon the work of his hands, for the Lord who created them knows their weakness, because they are flesh and blood.

**36.** “And when their souls depart from their bodies, their bodies become dust until the day that he wills.”
