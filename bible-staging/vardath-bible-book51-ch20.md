## 1 Meqabyan 20

**1.** Remember, our brothers, and do not forget what was told to you before: the LORD preserves the righteousness of those who do what is good.

**2.** Their fruit will multiply upon the earth, their memory will remain for good forever, and their offspring will not cease from the earth.

**3.** He will deliver them from the hand of all who oppose them and from their enemies who seek them, for on their behalf he contends and does not abandon them into the hand of their adversaries.

**4.** He will be their helper in the time of their distress. Those who love his name he will protect, and he will forgive them all their sins.
