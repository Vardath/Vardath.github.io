## 1 Meqabyan 11

**1.** Armon and her strongholds have fallen, and those who worship idols in Zebulun and Edom will be brought low at that time, for the LORD has repaid them according to their wickedness and according to the work of their hands. Tyre and Sidon will weep, for the one who will avenge their evil and their idols has drawn near—the evil they practiced from their youth and did not abandon even to old age.

**2.** Therefore the LORD will take vengeance on them because they practiced abomination, uncleanness, fornication, and idolatry. The daughters of Judah also will be brought low, because they did not stand firm in the commandment of the LORD their Creator.

**3.** In the same way, at the resurrection of the dead the shame of Jerusalem will be revealed, because she did not walk in the law and judgment of the LORD, but walked in violence and in the killing of the righteous.

**4.** At that time the LORD will search her with the lamp of his wisdom, and he will avenge upon her all the evil she practiced in the days of her youth and did not abandon through the days of her maturity even to her old age.

**5.** She entered the grave and became dust like her fathers before her, who walked according to their own way and rejected the commandment of the LORD, so that at the resurrection he may take vengeance upon them.

**6.** For Moses said concerning them, “Their vine is from the vine of Sodom.

**7.** “Their growth is from Gomorrah; their clusters are clusters of gall, and their fruit is bitter.

**8.** “Their wine is the venom of serpents of the earth and the deadly poison of asps.”
