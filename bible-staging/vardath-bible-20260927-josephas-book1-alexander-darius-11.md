### Alexander Defeats Darius

Alexander went out from Jerusalem strengthened by what the high priest had told him and marched against Darius.

The armies met in battle. Alexander prevailed, Darius was killed, and Alexander took possession of his kingdom. Thus, in this account, the dominion of the Persians came to its end and the rule of the Greeks became established.

Alexander brought the lands of Darius under his authority and made Alexandria in Egypt a royal city.

Afterward he continued his campaigns toward the east. He went as far as India and extended his dominion over many peoples and kingdoms.

Alexander ruled for twelve years.

When he was returning toward his own land, his life came to an end.

Before his death he arranged the government of the territories he had conquered among the commanders who served under him, and after him the Greek kingdoms were divided among different rulers.

From that time the people of Israel came under the authority of the Greek kings.
