## 1 Meqabyan 10

**1.** If it were not so, why did the ancient fathers—Adam, Abel, Seth, Noah, Shem, Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, Joseph, Moses, and Aaron—desire to be buried nowhere except beside the graves of their fathers?

**2.** Was it not so that at the resurrection they might rise together with their kindred, and that their bones might not be counted among the bones of the wicked, idolaters, and pagans?

**3.** Do not deceive your mind by saying, “How will the dead rise after they have died, when many have been cast together into one grave and their bones have decayed?”

**4.** When you look into a grave and cannot find even a handful of their dust, do not say in the foolishness of your heart, “How will the bones of the dead rise?”

**5.** Do you say that what you sow will not sprout? What you sow does indeed sprout.

**6.** In the same way, the LORD who sowed the souls will raise them swiftly. He will not delay in raising them by his life-giving word, for he brought mankind from nonexistence into true existence.

**7.** Again, from existence he brought mankind into death, and again from death he will bring them into existence.

**8.** The LORD is able to give life and to raise up.
