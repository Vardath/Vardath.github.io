### Alexander's Kingdom Becomes Secure

The reading of the next few lines in the surviving working source is damaged.

It records a further campaign in which a ruler came with a large army against Alexander.

Alexander prevailed over him, and the opposing ruler was killed.

Alexander then returned to Jerusalem victorious.

After these struggles the Jews received him with honor, those who continued to oppose him were subdued, and the affairs of his kingdom became firmly established.

His power increased and his authority grew strong.

### The Death of Alexander son of Hyrcanus

Afterward Alexander was struck by a severe illness.

The sickness remained upon him for three years, weakened his body, and consumed his strength.

While he was still ill, news came that one of the cities under his authority had rebelled.

Alexander nevertheless marched against it.

His wife Alexandra went with him, together with his household and attendants.

He encamped against the city and laid siege to it.

While the siege continued, Alexander's illness grew worse and he understood that death was near.

Alexandra came to him weeping and said:

“My lord, you know the hostility that exists between you and the Pharisees.

“Our sons are still young, and I am a woman and too weak to withstand them.

“What is your counsel, and what do you advise me to do?”

Alexander answered her:

“I will tell you what to do when I have paid the debt of death.

“Conceal my death and keep the matter secret from the army and from the people until this city has been taken, for its fall is near.

“When you have captured it, conduct matters as though I were still alive until everything there has been put in order.

“Then return to Jerusalem carrying my body with you.

“Summon the leading men of the Pharisees and receive them with honor.

“When they have assembled, say to them:

“‘Alexander has died.

“‘You know the hostility that existed between him and you and what he did against you.

“‘I now place his body in your hands.

“‘Do with it whatever you think proper.

“‘As for me, I will govern according to your counsel and will not oppose you in the matters you judge right.’

“If you speak to them in this way, they will not dishonor my body.

“I know their character and the mercy that is among them.

“Afterward they will support you in the kingdom and will help preserve it for you until our sons are grown and able to rule.

“The common people incline toward the Pharisees, listen to them, and submit to their direction.”

After Alexander had given this counsel, he died.

Alexandra concealed his death as he had commanded.

She continued the siege until the city was taken.

Then she returned to Jerusalem carrying Alexander's body.

She summoned the leading Pharisees and spoke to them according to everything Alexander had told her.

They answered her kindly.

They openly displayed grief and mourning for Alexander and spoke to the people in his honor.

Alexander was buried with the kings who had gone before him.

The Pharisees then advised the people to make Alexandra queen.

The people accepted their counsel.

Alexandra became ruler of the Jews, while the Pharisees were given great authority in the ordering of public affairs.

Alexander son of Hyrcanus had ruled for twenty-seven years.

He left two sons, Hyrcanus and Aristobulus.
