### Judah’s Campaigns after the Dedication

After the dedication of the sanctuary, Judah went out toward the land of Edom.

Gorgias came against him with a large force.

Judah attacked the army of Gorgias, defeated it, and put the soldiers to flight.

Gorgias escaped toward Arabia and joined Timotheus.

Timotheus then gathered Macedonian and Arabian forces and moved against the Jews living in Gad and Gilead.

Many of the Jews there were killed.

Those who remained sent messengers to Judah, saying:

“Come quickly and deliver us, for the sword of Timotheus is consuming our people.”

Other messengers came with news that the Jews in the regions of Tyre and Sidon were also being attacked.

When Judah heard these things, he fasted and prayed.

He selected men prepared for battle and hurried across the Jordan.

Simon also went with a force into Galilee, fought those who oppressed the Jews there, and delivered the people.

Afterward he returned toward Jerusalem with those he had rescued.

### Judah in Gilead

Judah crossed the Jordan and entered Gilead.

There he found Timotheus attacking a fortified place in which Jews had taken refuge.

The forces of Timotheus were strong and numerous, while Judah’s company was smaller.

The battle became fierce.

In the midst of the fighting Judah cried to God for help.

Then he saw five young horsemen clothed in shining garments.

Two appeared before Judah and protected him, while the others fought against the army of Timotheus.

Judah understood the vision as help sent from heaven.

He encouraged his men and pressed the attack.

The army of Timotheus broke and fled.

Judah pursued them toward the Jordan and continued driving them from the land.

Timotheus gathered those who remained to him and attempted to resist again, but Judah attacked with such force that his army was scattered once more.

Thus the Jews in Gilead were delivered from the army that had come against them.
