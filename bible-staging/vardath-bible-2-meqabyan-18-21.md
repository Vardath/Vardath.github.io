## 2 Meqabyan 18

**1.** How greatly you err, you who do not believe in the resurrection of the dead! When they take you to a place you do not know, then you will repent where repentance no longer profits you, when they cast you into judgment because you did not believe in the resurrection of the dead in flesh and soul together.

**2.** You will be repaid according to what you have done, whether good or evil, because you hardened the hearts of your companions, saying, “We believe that the dead, who have become dust and earth, will not rise.”

**3.** Because of this they lead their companions astray. There is no escape from their death and no strength against their punishment. No toil will free them, for they must stand before the judgment of the LORD.

**4.** They will be greatly ashamed when he rebukes them in his wrath and is angry with them because of all the evil they have done. Not knowing the way of the LORD, they speak although they themselves do not know from where they came.

**5.** Nor do they know where they are going: into the judgment of burning fire. According to the perversity of their hearts they teach their companions, for they are bitter and crooked in their deeds, defiled, and teachers of twisted words, saying, “There is no resurrection of the dead.”

**6.** At that time they will know that the dead rise, and they will be condemned because they did not believe in the resurrection of the dead for all the children of men.

**7.** For we are all the offspring of Adam, and through Adam we died, because through the error of our father Adam the sentence of death came upon us all from the LORD.

**8.** And again, so that we may be repaid for what we ourselves have done, we will rise together with Adam our father, for through the transgression of our father Adam death came to reign in the world.

**9.** Because of Adam’s transgression we received this recompense: our flesh is hidden in the grave and our bones are broken down.

**10.** The earth drinks our marrow and our fat. Our flesh is mingled with dust and buried in the ground, and our pleasant voice is buried in bitterness.

**11.** The light departs from our bright eyes, our beauty decays in the grave, and it becomes dust.

**12.** Where is the beauty of the handsome young men whose stature was like Adam’s, and where is the sweetness of their voices? Where is the greatness of the mighty?

**13.** Where is the strength of kings and the glory of rulers, adorned with horses, decorated with gold and silver, and clothed in splendid possessions that glitter?

**14.** Where is the sweetness of rich food and of pleasant wine?

## 2 Meqabyan 19

**1.** O earth, you have gathered kings and rulers, the great and the honored, beautiful women and fair virgins.

**2.** You have gathered the strong-handed, those of fine appearance and beautiful form, those possessing knowledge and understanding, and those whose voices were pleasant like a speaking organ and like the sound of harp and lyre.

**3.** You have gathered those whose singing was beautiful and gladdened the heart like the drinking of wine, whose eyes were bright like the morning star.

**4.** Their right hands were strong to give, to receive, and to fight, and their feet were beautiful to behold and swift like well-made wheels.

**5.** And you, O Death, have taken the souls of these good ones, for you were sent according to the will of the LORD.

**6.** And you also, O earth, have gathered many bodies, those whom the LORD brought forth from you and returned again to you. From you we came forth and to you we return. By the will of the LORD we rejoiced upon you.

**7.** You became a foundation for our bodies. We walked upon you and slept within you. We ate your fruit, and you ate our flesh.

**8.** We drank from the dew of your springs, and you drank from the spring of our blood. We ate from the richness of your soil, and you consumed the marrow of our bones.

**9.** We ate from the sweetness of your soil and from your good dew, as the LORD commanded you for our nourishment. You took the beauty of our flesh and made it dust, as the LORD commanded you for your nourishment.

**10.** O Death, you have gathered many mighty kings and rulers. You did not fear their majesty or their greatness, for the one who created them commanded you, and you did not despise the poor.

**11.** You did not spare those of beautiful appearance, nor the mighty and strong. You did not spare the rich or the poor, the handsome or the beautiful, the old or the young, men or women.

**12.** You did not spare those who thought what is good and did not transgress the commandment, nor those who thought evil, who became like beasts in their deeds. You did not spare those perfect in appearance, in voice, or in the sweetness of their speech.

**13.** You did not spare those whose words were bitter and whose mouths were full of cursing. You gathered their souls into your storehouses, whether into the storehouses of light or those of darkness.

**14.** The earth also gathered their bodies, whether in the rocks or in the soil, until the day of the blast of the horn when the dead will rise.

**15.** Then those who acted will be repaid according to the deeds they have done. At the blast of the horn and by the command of the LORD, the dead will rise in the blink of an eye, and those who did good will rejoice.

## 2 Meqabyan 20

**1.** Believe me: none of the deeds we have done upon the earth will be abandoned or hidden when we stand before him in fear and trembling.

**2.** If we have not carried provisions for our journey and have no garment for our nakedness,

**3.** if we have no staff for our hands and no sandals for our feet,

**4.** and if we do not know the road to the place where they take us—whether through a pit or a ravine, through darkness, through thorn and briar, through the sea or into the lake of Sheol—

**5.** those who take us are beings we do not know, and we do not hear their speech.

**6.** We do not see their faces, for they are darkness, and into darkness they lead us.

**7.** As David the prophet said: “When my soul failed within me, you, O LORD, knew my path. In the way in which I walked they hid a snare for me. I turned to my right and looked, but there was no one who knew me; there was nowhere for me to escape.”

**8.** He said this because he knew that demons would mock him and lead him where he did not know the way. He would turn to the right and to the left and find no one who knew him.

**9.** There is no one there who knows him; he is alone in the midst of the angels of darkness.

**10.** But the spiritual angels of light are sent to the good, to receive the souls of the righteous and take them to the enclosure of the light of life.

**11.** Likewise the angels of darkness and the demons are sent to the souls of sinners, to receive them and take them into the everlasting judgment prepared for them, so that they may be repaid according to the deeds they have done.

**12.** Woe to the souls of sinners who are taken into destruction, where there is no rest, salvation, escape, or departure forever from the misery that overtakes them.

**13.** Woe to the sinners, for they walked in the way of Cain and perished in the reward of Balaam’s iniquity. They sinned in what they did, for through greed and the taking of gain they went down into ruin, seeking to seize by violence possessions that were not their own.

**14.** They will be repaid according to the deeds they have done in the judgment of burning fire.

## 2 Meqabyan 21

**1.** Where are those who gathered the possessions of others by violence, possessions that were not their own, and the works of their hands?

**2.** They seize the possessions of others without payment and gather them without knowing that the day of their death will reach them, and then they will leave their wealth to another.

**3.** Their children will not rejoice in the wealth of their fathers, for these are the houses of sinners. If they increase that wealth by theft and violence, they are sinners like their fathers; and the same is true if they increase it by robbery.

**4.** The possessions of their fathers will not profit them, for they were gathered by oppression. Like the vapor of a cloud, like smoke carried away by the wind, like the fruit of grass that is shaken off, and like wax melting before the face of fire, so the glory of sinners will disappear.

**5.** As David said: “I saw the wicked exalted and lifted up like a cedar of Lebanon. I passed by, and behold, he was gone. I sought him, but his place was not found.”

**6.** So sudden is the destruction of sinners, so that those who oppress their companions may not boast as though they would never die merely because they gathered the possessions of others by violence.

**7.** O fools, remember that you will pass away, and your wealth will pass away with you. Even if your gold and silver become abundant, all of it will become something that has passed away.

**8.** Even if you multiply children, there will be many graves; and even if you multiply houses, they will become a desolation.

**9.** Even if you multiply livestock, it will become spoil for your enemies, because you did not do the will of the LORD your God. Nothing upon which you laid your hands will remain, because it was not blessed.

**10.** This is true whether it is in the house or in the countryside, in fields, gardens, threshing floors, or winepresses.

**11.** Even in the fruit of your womb you will not rejoice; instead sorrow will come upon you from every enemy, because the LORD will not make you prosper together with all the people of your household, for you did not keep the commandments of the LORD.

**12.** But from those who keep his law, his commandments, and his ordinances he will not withhold his blessing. He will give them everything they ask, and he will bless for them the fruit of their land and the fruit of their womb.

**13.** He will set them above all the nations around them, so that they will rule and not be ruled, and he will give them every blessing in their gardens and fields.

**14.** He will bless everything to which they set their hands: the fruit of their labor, the increase of their livestock, and he will make them rejoice in the fruit of their womb.

**15.** He will not diminish their livestock, and he will save them from every affliction, disease, weakness, and danger, whether known or unknown.

**16.** He will stand for them in justice and save them from distress, from evil matters, and from every adversary. From the beginning, concerning the service of the tabernacle, when there is a Levite who serves and keeps the service of the tabernacle and walks according to the will of the LORD under the first law, they are to give him the tithe of everything according to the law and every firstborn, from human beings even to animals.

**17.** Moses also commanded cities of refuge throughout all their territories, as he commanded Joshua the son of Nun, so that the killer who killed unknowingly might be protected, while the matter of one who killed knowingly must be brought to judgment.

**18.** They are to bring the case before those who judge and those who acquit.

**19.** They must question him among you: “Did he previously have hatred against the man?” If he says, “An iron object, a stone, or a piece of wood fell from my hand without my knowing and killed the one upon whom it fell,” and you know this to be true, save him, for he acted unknowingly.

**20.** But if he acted knowingly, he will be repaid according to his offense and no one will pardon him. If he acted unknowingly and your inquiry has shown it, release him so that he does not die, for he acted without knowledge.

**21.** Thus Moses established these things for the children of Israel so that they would not transgress the commandment of the LORD, but would restrain themselves from every evil.

**22.** He commanded them to walk in all his judgments and righteousness, to keep far from violence, theft, murder, and everything that is not fitting for the children of men, so that those who walk in the commandment of the LORD may restrain themselves from every evil according to what was established for them.

**23.** He commanded them not to depart from the commandment he established for them according to the pattern in heaven, which is the tabernacle, so that they might save their souls and find their dwelling with their fathers who did good.

**24.** Those who believed in the word of the LORD and walked in his commandment are called the generation of the righteous, for they are of Adam and Seth, those who do the will of the LORD and do not turn aside.

**25.** For we are the seed of Adam. In his image he made us, and in his likeness he created us, so that we may do what is good and everything that pleases the LORD.

**26.** If we do good, we will inherit the land of life with those who did good, for he does not separate or show partiality among those who love him.

**27.** He loves them greatly and hears the prayer of those who call upon him with purity. He receives the repentance of those who repent earnestly and gives strength and steadfastness to those who keep his law, his ordinance, and his commandment.

**28.** Those who do his will, whether those of former times or those of later times, will rejoice with him in his everlasting kingdom. To him they raise glory from now and forever and ever. Amen.
