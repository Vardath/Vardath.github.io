## 3 Meqabyan 2

**1.** “Those whom you are unable to overcome, like Job my servant, I will cause to inherit your throne in glory; and those who withstand your deception I will reward with the kingdom of heaven,” says the LORD who rules all.

**2.** “But I will use every kind of cunning against the children of Adam, and I will not leave them standing in good works if I am able to lead them astray. I will act craftily against all the children of Adam and make the desires of the world sweet to them.

**3.** “I will tempt them through desire for food and drink, through love of clothing and possessions, and through the desire to give and to take.

**4.** “I will tempt them through love of seeing, hearing, wandering about, and touching; through abundance of speech born of pride; and through love of sleep and dreams.

**5.** “I will tempt them through excessive drinking and drunkenness, through the sight of the beautiful women of this world, and through strong and pleasing fragrances.

**6.** “I will tempt them through hatred and slander of their companions, and again through the sight of beautiful women of this world and through alluring fragrances.

**7.** “Through all these things I will oppose them so that they may not be able to attain salvation. I will turn them away from the way of the LORD so that they may enter destruction with me, for because of them I was driven from my throne.”

**8.** And the prophet said, “O ruin and destroyer, is this how you exalt yourself against the creature of the LORD? You altered the commandment of the LORD through your pride, through the hardness of your heart, and through rebellion against your Creator, and you did not praise your God.

**9.** “When your Creator became angry with you, he cast you away because of your evil deed. Why then do you contend against the poor man whom his God created from earth and dust, fashioned according to his will, and placed for his glory?

**10.** “Though you were spiritual, you exalted yourself against the word of God who created you from the flame of fire and from wind.

**11.** “When you boasted, the LORD saw your evil deed and created another singer in your place to praise his name, because you and your demons rebelled against the commandment of the LORD.

**12.** “He made Adam and his children to praise the name of the LORD in place of the praise that had belonged to you and your host, whom the LORD rejected because of your pride, for you exalted yourself above all the angels who were like you among the host of angels.

**13.** “Therefore the LORD removed you from among all the chiefs of the angels who were like you, together with your host who were created with you. **14.** “Therefore, so that he might be praised among the humble, he created Adam from the soil of the earth and gave him a law and a commandment that he should not eat from the fruit of the tree.

**15.** “He set him over all that he had created and instructed him, saying, ‘Eat from every tree that is in the garden, but from the one tree of death do not eat, lest you bring death upon yourself.’

**16.** “When you heard this word, you poured the venom from your mouth upon Eve, the bone from Adam’s side.

**17.** “You ensnared the innocent sheep with an evil snare so that you might make him like yourself, a transgressor of the commandment.

**18.** “You deceived Eve, who had been created like a gentle dove and did not know you. You deceived her through your twisted word and crooked speech. After you deceived that first one, she went and deceived the servant of the LORD, the first man who had been created from the earth.

**19.** “Through your pride you cast him into confusion and made him transgress the word of his Creator. Through your pride you brought the poor man low.

**20.** “Through your evil you removed him from the love of his God. Because of you he was driven from the Paradise of joy, and by your snare you deprived him of the food of the garden.

**21.** “Through your lie you deprived him of the drink of Paradise, for from the beginning you opposed the gentle servant so that you might bring him down into your death in Sheol and remove him from the love of his God, who brought him from non-being into true existence.

**22.** “Though he was earthly, God made him spiritual and speaking, so that he might praise and sing to his God with his soul, his body, and his thought.
