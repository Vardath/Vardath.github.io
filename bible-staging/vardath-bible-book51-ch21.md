## 1 Meqabyan 21

**1.** David trusted in the LORD, and the LORD became his refuge because he trusted in him; he delivered him from the hand of King Saul.

**2.** He delivered David also in the days of Absalom his son, in the days of the Elophlaweans, in the days of the Amalekites and Edomites, and from the one among the four Rephaim. From all those who rose against him the LORD delivered David, because he trusted in him and kept his law.

**3.** But the wicked kings who did not trust in him the LORD did not save, for they were defeated by the hand of their enemies; victory comes from the LORD.

**4.** Hezekiah trusted in the LORD, and he delivered him from the hand of the proud Syrian.

**5.** But Manasseh his son was defeated by the hand of his enemy because he did not place his trust in the LORD. Those who overcame Manasseh did not merely defeat him; they bound him and carried him away to their country, because he had not trusted in the LORD or feared the one who had honored, exalted, and magnified him.

**6.** At that time the LORD withheld from him the gift he had given, because Manasseh had not made his way good before the LORD his God, so that his days might be prolonged, his enemy opposed for him, and strength, firmness, completeness, and protection be given to him.

**7.** For it is better to trust in the LORD than in a multitude of armies, and better than trusting in horses, shields, and bows.

**8.** Whoever trusts in the LORD will live, be honored, and be greatly exalted.

**9.** But those who do not trust in the LORD and instead trust in the abundance of their wealth will be cast away from the grace he gave them, for the LORD shows no partiality.

**10.** Those who trust in him he guards; but those who treat him as foolish, he gives over to their own foolishness. He does not hasten to help them in the time of their distress when their enemies rise against them, because they did not strengthen their hearts in following the LORD and keeping his commandments.

**11.** But the one who strengthens himself in following the LORD, following his commandments, and keeping his law—the LORD will be his refuge in the time of his distress.

**12.** He will make him rejoice in the defeat of his enemy, in the spoil of his enemy’s cattle, in captives from the enemy’s land, in the rains of the season, in the fruit of his crops, in the filling of his storehouses, and in the fruit of his gardens.

**13.** He will bless him with the rains of spring and autumn, with grass and green growth, and with what comes in each of its seasons, so that the people under his hand may rejoice.

**14.** They will eat the labor of others and be satisfied with the abundance taken from their enemies. They will take cattle, herds, and sheep as spoil, eat from another’s table, and take captive the children of their enemies.

**15.** These things the LORD does for the one who loves him; but the one who hates him he gives over to the plunder of his enemy.

**16.** He binds his hands and feet and casts him into the hand of his enemy. He makes him an object of ridicule to his adversaries and does not make him rejoice in the offspring of his house, because he became a transgressor of the commandment of the LORD and a shedder of blood.

**17.** He will not stand firm in judgment. The LORD repays the fruit of their deeds to those who do evil, giving evil to the workers of evil when he repays wickedness.

**18.** But concerning those who do good, it has been commanded by the LORD that good be repaid to them and that he keep them under his authority.

**19.** He has authority over all creation that he created. He gives eternal life to those who do good, and they praise the LORD who created them. Of all the creatures he made, none turns aside from his commandment except mankind alone.

**20.** All the others know him and remain within his commandment and order, according as the LORD commanded every creature to live according to its appointed order.

**21.** But mankind transgresses against the one who crowned him and placed him over all the wild animals, the beasts, and all the birds that the LORD created.

**22.** Whether in the sea or upon the dry land, everything the LORD created he gave to our father Adam, that he might use them according to his will, eat them as the plants of the earth, rule over them, and make them serve him. Whether wild animals or domestic beasts, the LORD commanded them to obey mankind. In the same way, people who are made rulers are to obey the LORD, who gave them honor and placed them over what he created; they should please him and obey him.

**23.** But if they turn aside from his commandment, he removes them from the authority he gave them and gives it to the one who does his will, for he is ruler of heaven and earth.

**24.** Whomever he wills he appoints, and whomever he wills he removes. He kills and gives life; he chastens and he shows mercy.

**25.** There is no other like him and none who can contend with him, for he is Lord of everything he created. There is no other God besides him in heaven above or on the earth beneath.

**26.** He appoints and removes; he kills and gives life; he chastens and shows mercy; he brings low and makes rich.

**27.** He receives the petitions of those who ask him. Whoever does his will with a pure heart, he hears their prayer and grants their desire in everything they ask of him.

**28.** He commands both small and great things on their behalf, and all that belongs to them in the mountains, hills, caves, trees, throughout the earth, in the sea, and on the dry land.

**29.** All these things belong to those who do the will of their God. He does not deprive them of his blessing, but gives them the reward of their praise.

**30.** In heaven he gives them the honor he prepared for their fathers Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and for David, Hezekiah, and Samuel, who did not turn aside from the commandment and judgment of the LORD.

**31.** He gives them the inheritance he swore to their fathers Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, who from ancient times pleased him, so that they may rejoice in his kingdom.
