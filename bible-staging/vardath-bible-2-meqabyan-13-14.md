## 2 Meqabyan 13

**1.** These Meqabyan, the children of Meqabis, believed and gave their souls over to death, refusing to eat the sacrifice of idols. *The digital Geʽez source splits this opening sentence across a stray line numbered 5; it is restored here as verse 1.*

**2.** They gave their souls to the fire so that they might be saved from the fire of heaven, knowing that this world passes away.

**3.** They said, “Pleasure does not remain forever.” They knew that the reproach of the LORD is more terrible than the reproach of mortals, and that the wrath of the LORD is more terrible than the wrath of a king.

**4.** One day of rejoicing in the courts of Paradise is better than thousands of years of life upon the earth, and one hour of your mercy, O LORD, is better than length of days.

**5.** For what are the days of a person? Do they not pass like a shadow and like wax before the face of fire?

**6.** But you, O LORD, remain forever. Your years have no end, and your remembrance is from generation to generation.

**7.** The children of Meqabis considered all these things and things like them. They took courage, trusted in the LORD, and refused to eat the unclean sacrifice.

**8.** Because of this they offered their bodies as testimony for the LORD, knowing that the dead would rise and knowing that there would be judgment after the resurrection.

**9.** Those who do not know and do not believe in the resurrection of the dead should learn from these good men of one lineage, who together gave their souls over to death, knowing that their latter life would be better than this passing earthly life. *The digital source breaks this sentence across a stray line numbered 5; no extra verse or numeral is created from that defect.*

**10.** They saw that everything passes away and gave their souls to death so that they might receive reward from the LORD because they believed in him, did not bow to idols, and did not eat the dead and unclean sacrifice.

**11.** Therefore they knew that he would make them rejoice on the last day in soul and body. Though they had wives and children, they did not cling to the sweetness of this world or fear the bitterness of death; they gave their souls to death, knowing that there would be a resurrection on the last day in soul and body.

**12.** They knew that those who keep the ordinance of the LORD will reign in the world to come from generation to generation and for length of days, where there is no sickness or sorrow.

**13.** There is no sickness or sorrow there, and there is no death. Together with those who believed the word of the LORD—good kings and rulers—they gave their souls to death as witnesses in the midst of fire, seeing in the thought of their hearts what was to come afterward.

**14.** They hoped for the day when their faces would shine more brightly than the sun and when they would rejoice in his love at the resurrection of all, in soul and body.

## 2 Meqabyan 14

**1.** It grieves me exceedingly, makes me mourn, and troubles my heart concerning the Jews, Samaritans, Pharisees, and Sadducees who do not believe in the resurrection of the dead. Some Jews say, “Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die, and afterward there is nothing that we see.”

**2.** The Samaritans say, “Our flesh will not rise, because it becomes dust.”

**3.** “If there is a resurrection, we believe only in the resurrection of our souls, because the soul does not die even when the body dies. It is like the wind, which is unseen, and like the sound of thunder, which is heard but is not seen.”

**4.** “But our flesh is visible, and it becomes dust and decay, for creatures consume it and what grows in the grave consumes it.”

**5.** “Those things that consumed it also become dust, and no trace of them is found. They become like grass and like something that was never created.”

**6.** The Pharisees say, “We believe in the resurrection of the dead, but in heaven the souls will be clothed with another body, not the body that was upon the earth.”

**7.** The Sadducees say, “After our soul goes out from our body, we will not rise from the dead. There is no resurrection of the dead, neither for soul nor for body; after we die, we will not rise.”

**8.** Because of this they err greatly, and I mourn over them, for they speak blasphemy against the LORD and against his glory.

**9.** They have no hope of salvation because they did not believe the LORD who justifies them; they have no hope of salvation or of the resurrection of the dead.

**10.** O blind Jew, do you think yourself greater than the LORD who brought you from where you did not exist and, while you were yet an embryo, made you a human being? From your seed he caused your soul and body to spring up in his image and likeness.

**11.** Do not think that you can escape from the hand of the LORD. You will rise even if you do not wish to rise, and you will be judged even if you do not wish to be judged, for the rope of Sheol is already around your neck and will seize you on the day of your death.

**12.** From within your mother that rope of Sheol grew with you from its root. It was placed around your neck, and as you grew, it also grew.

**13.** When you die it will be placed upon your neck, and when its root is pulled it will bring you forth.

**14.** It draws its branches, for all sinners are caught within it as though ropes were around their necks.

**15.** It will bring all the souls of sinners from wherever they are, from the ends of heaven. In the same way your own rope will pull you, bring your soul from wherever you are, and return you into the river of Sheol.

**16.** Your body also will be restored and made complete like the body of our father Adam. The dew of the mercy of the LORD will raise you, and there you will be repaid according to the offenses and sins you committed in your unbelief.

**17.** Do not think that by deceiving another you can deceive yourself, remaining in the grave in your error and saying, “There is no resurrection of the dead,” so that people might turn aside from the commandment of the LORD.

**18.** Who will leave you in dust and earth? Rather, he will raise you so that he may repay you according to the deeds you have done.

**19.** At that time, whether your spirits are among the winds, your dust in the earth, or your flame in the fire, they will come forth.

**20.** So also the spirit of life that was breathed upon you within the enclosure of darkness.

**21.** These things are known; but the souls of the righteous are enclosed in Paradise with rejoicing.

**22.** But you, O Jew, Samaritan, Pharisee, and Sadducee, until the time of your judgment you will be bound in darkness.

**23.** Then you will see how the LORD repays you according to your deeds because you caused your own mind to go astray.

**24.** You tell them, “The dead will not live. Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die.” With your own mouth, while sitting upon the seat of Moses, you say, “There is no resurrection of the dead.”

**25.** You go astray in this way although you know the book of the Torah and teach the words of the Scriptures. How can you lead people astray by your lie when you yourself have neither learned nor understood?

**26.** By twisting the words of the Scriptures you mislead the people of the LORD through your evil teaching and your unworthy words.

**27.** You will be repaid according to your deeds and according to the fruit of your lips, for the LORD shows no partiality. But those who teach what is good he will reward from the good things he has prepared for those who love him.

**28.** You have nowhere to escape from the hand of the one who judges you. You and those whom you taught will be judged together.

**29.** Know that the dead will rise. Those who kept his commandment will rise, and nothing will prevent their standing, for his commandment will bring them forth as the earth brings forth grass when rain strikes it.

**30.** As a moist tree brings forth leaves when rain waters the earth, and as wheat or grain bears its fruit and cannot withhold that fruit when the LORD wills it,

**31.** and as a woman with child, when her labor comes, cannot close her womb after hearing the command of the LORD but must bring forth by necessity because the dew from the LORD descends upon her,

**32.** so also the earth will bring forth those who were deposited in it. It cannot close its womb after hearing the command of the LORD, but must bring them forth by necessity because the dew from the LORD descends upon it.

**33.** Bodies will be gathered again into their places where their corpses fell. The treasury-houses of souls will be opened, and the souls will return into the bodies from which they first departed.

**34.** At the blast of the horn the dead will rise in the blink of an eye. They will stand before the LORD, and he will repay them according to the works of their hands.

**35.** Then you will see that you have risen from the dead and you will marvel at all the deeds you performed upon the earth when you see them written before your eyes. At that time you will repent in a place where repentance no longer profits you.

**36.** Then you will know that you have risen from the dead and that you will be repaid according to the deeds you have done.
