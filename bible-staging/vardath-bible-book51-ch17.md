## 1 Meqabyan 17

**1.** Edom and Amalek do not worship the LORD, in whose hand are the judgment of heaven and earth. They do not fear to violate the house of his sanctuary, for they are a rebellious people who do not walk in righteousness.

**2.** There is no fear of the LORD before their eyes, but rather fornication, the shedding of blood, the eating of what has been slaughtered for idols, what has died of itself, and every similar abomination and defilement.

**3.** They have neither faith nor integrity, for they hate what is good and do not know the LORD or the way of peace. Instead they practice robbery, strife, every unclean work, disturbance, song, and revelry, just as their father Seblanios taught them.

**4.** He rules them together with his demons and teaches them every evil work that belongs to him: violence, strife, taking another’s possessions by force, theft, eating what has died of itself, eating what is unclean, and fornication.

**5.** He teaches them every thing like these: going in to another man’s wife, shedding blood, eating what has been sacrificed to idols and what has died of itself, killing a human life by violence, jealousy, deceit, greed, and every evil deed that the LORD does not love. This enemy, Seblanios, brings them this teaching so that he may turn them away from the way of the LORD, ruler of the whole world.

**6.** But the way of the LORD is humility and gentleness, love of one’s neighbor, reconciliation between brothers, mutual love, and peace with all people.

**7.** Do not be respecters of persons, deceivers, wrongdoers, contentious people, those who go in to another man’s wife, workers of evil and violence against their neighbors, or those who entice their companions into wrongdoing.

**8.** Such people make signs and shake their heads, devise evil, and counsel deception so that they may bring people down into the judgment that endures forever.
