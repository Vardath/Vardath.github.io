## 1 Meqabyan 18

**1.** Remember that you will go to the LORD, in whose hand are all things, and you will stand before him to be judged for the deeds you have done.

**2.** So it was also in ancient times with the children of the giants, who were evil and proud. None among them was greater or stronger than the others in stature and in the greatness of their arms, yet they did not set the LORD before them and did not know that he was their Creator who brought them from nonexistence.

**3.** Their fathers had been angels who praised with the angels in heaven; but when their desire deceived them, they descended into the judgment that endures forever.

**4.** They took women from the daughters of the children of men. The LORD had allowed them to encounter human flesh in order to test the pride of their hearts and whether they would keep his commandment and his law.

**5.** They did not keep the commandment, and he brought them down into the judgment of burning fire with the devil their father. Because of them the days of mankind were shortened, for the LORD was angry with the angels who sinned like men.

**6.** They also drew the children of men into sin with them and brought them down into Sheol together with their own judgment.

**7.** Formerly the days of mankind reached nine hundred years; but the days of mankind were shortened to one hundred and twenty years because the angels went astray with the daughters of Cain.

**8.** And the LORD said, “My Spirit shall not remain with them, for they are flesh and blood.”

**9.** Because of this our days were shortened, and some die while they are still children. Our days became fewer than the days of our fathers before us because of our transgressions and our sins.

**10.** But the days of our fathers were long because they kept his commandment and did not anger the LORD.

**11.** They corrected their daughters so that they might teach them, and corrected their sons so that they would not transgress the commandment of the LORD.

**12.** Therefore their days were prolonged in righteousness, because they and their sons and daughters did not turn aside from the commandment of the LORD.
