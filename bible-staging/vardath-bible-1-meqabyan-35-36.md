## 1 Meqabyan 35

**1.** Woe to you, mighty men of Israel, for the LORD is angry with you because you have not made justice good for the orphan.

**2.** Woe to you who frequent the house of drinking morning and evening, becoming drunk and perverting judgment, who do not establish justice for orphans and widows but dwell in fornication and the works of the flesh.

**3.** Thus says the LORD to the mighty of Israel: “Woe to you if you do not walk in my commandment, keep my word, and love what I love.

**4.** “I will bring upon you punishment, pestilence, and sickness, and you will be consumed like wood eaten by worms and moths, until no trace of you is found within your territories.

**5.** “Your land will become desolate. Everyone who had seen it before will clap his hands and marvel over it, saying, ‘Is this not the land that was full of blessing and every good thing? Has the LORD made it like this because of the evil of those who lived in it?’

**6.** “Woe to that land! It exalted its heart, lifted its head, and stretched out its neck until the LORD humbled it to the dust of the earth because of the pride of those who dwelt in it. It became desolate, and thorns and thistles sprang up upon it.

**7.** “It became a place of ruin and desolation, a waste land in which wild beasts dwell.

**8.** “It became a terror to those who pass through it, for the wrath of the LORD fell upon it and it drank from the cup of his wrath because of the pride of its heart and the evil of those who inhabited it.”

## 1 Meqabyan 36

**1.** Do not boast, O Macedonians, and do not stiffen your necks, O Amalekites, for the LORD lives who will take vengeance upon you.

**2.** Though you exalt yourselves as far as heaven, you will descend into Sheol.

**3.** From the days of Israel’s first entrance into the land of Egypt, and through the kingdoms of the Medonians and the Moabites, the word was: “Do not boast, for it is not fitting to mock the LORD who is over you.”

**4.** And you, son of an Ishmaelite handmaid, why do you stretch out your neck toward what is not yours? Do you not remember that the LORD will judge you on the day of judgment, when he rises to judge the earth?

**5.** Then you will be repaid according to the work of your hands. “Why do you lift up your heart and stretch out your neck?” says the LORD who rules over all.

**6.** “I will repay your mockery as you mocked those who were not yours, and I sent you where you chose to go because you desired to do evil.

**7.** “So I also will do to you,” says the LORD who rules over all. “But if you do good and love what I love, I will hear you in everything you ask of me.

**8.** “I will do your will if you do my will. I will oppose your enemy for you, and I will bless your work, your seed, and your children.

**9.** “I will bless the offspring of your cattle and your sheep, and I will bless everything to which you put your hand, if you walk in my commandment and do what I love,” says the LORD who rules over all.

**10.** “But if you do not do my will and do not walk in my commandment, my judgment will find you. All the judgment I declared beforehand will come upon you, and you will not be able to escape my wrath that comes upon you continually, because you did not remain in my commandment or walk according to my judgment.

**11.** “You did not return what I entrusted to you, but turned away from my praise and my worship, although I created you when you did not exist.

**12.** “I gave you honor and glory among the people beneath you, so that you might exercise the authority given to you: to kill and preserve life, to build and tear down, to honor and humble, to raise up and bring low. This authority was given to you.

**13.** “If you do the will of the LORD and walk in his commandments, he will love you, so that you may rejoice with him in his kingdom and have a portion with those who inherit the holy city.

**14.** “For he has said, ‘If they remain faithful to me, I will give them glory and favor as an inheritance and make them rejoice in the house of my prayer. They shall be chosen and acceptable like an offering of sacrifice,’” says the LORD who rules over all.

**15.** Do not grow weary of doing good or of walking in the path of salvation, so that you may pass from death into life.

**16.** Those who persevere in doing good the LORD entrusts to every good thing, that they may be his servants, as Job was, whom the LORD guarded from every evil.

**17.** So also Abraham, whom he delivered when the kings were slain; and as he delivered Moses from the hand of Pharaoh, and delivered from the Canaanites among whom Abraham lived, who sought evening and morning, day and night, to draw his soul down into their idols.

**18.** But he endured and refused their corrupt pleasures even while they dragged him toward them.

**19.** He refused them and feared the LORD who had created him, for Abraham was faithful to the LORD in whom he had trusted from his youth.

**20.** He did not diminish his worship of the LORD until the day of his death, for he loved the LORD exceedingly and did not turn aside from his commandment until the day he died. He taught his children to keep the commandment of the LORD.

**21.** They too did not turn aside from his commandment, following what their father had done. The seed of Abraham, his servants Isaac and Jacob, did not turn aside from the commandment of the LORD. Because of them the LORD spoke to his angels,

**22.** saying, “Upon the earth is Abraham my beloved, Isaac my child, and Jacob in whom my soul delights. Through them I have been praised,” says the LORD who rules over all.

**23.** But the children of Israel continually provoked him, even while he comforted them and fed them with manna in the wilderness.

**24.** Their garments did not wear out and their feet did not crack, for they were fed with manna, the bread of understanding.

**25.** Yet their hearts continually withdrew far from the LORD, and they had no hope of salvation because from ancient times they were workers of evil.

**26.** They were not like their fathers Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, who pleased the LORD by the goodness of their lives. Instead they became like a crooked bow, continually provoking him with their idols, upon their hills and mountains, eating upon the mountains, among the trees, and at the places of idolatry.

**27.** They slaughtered and sacrificed, rejoicing in the works of their hands. They ate what had been sacrificed, drank the wine of their libations, sang, and danced with demons.

**28.** The demons made sweet to them all their songs and dances, all their drunkenness, their uncontrolled sexual immorality, their greed, and their strife—things in which the LORD takes no pleasure.

**29.** They sacrificed to the images of Canaan, to Baal, to Marya of Midian, to the idols of the Philistines, to Dagon, Apollo, Artemis, and Ration.

**30.** They worshiped all the gods of the nations around them, doing everything the nations did according to what they saw and heard: all their dances, songs, and ritual cries.

**31.** So did the people of Israel who said, “We worship the LORD,” yet did not do his law and his commandment which Moses had told them in the Torah: to keep the commandments of the LORD and abstain from the worship of idols,

**32.** and not worship other gods besides the God of their fathers, who fed them with the grain of the fields, raised them upon the strength of the land, satisfied them with honey from the rock, and fed them with manna.

**33.** He withheld from them nothing that they desired or wanted, for he was their God and fed them according to their desire.

**34.** Yet they did not cease provoking the LORD, and whenever he made them rejoice they forgot the LORD.

**35.** When he chastened them, they cried out to him, and he delivered them from the distress that had overtaken them. Then they rejoiced openly and lived for many days.

**36.** Afterward their hearts turned again and they returned to provoking the LORD as before. He raised up against them nations from around them to kill them, enslave them, and oppress them.

**37.** Again they turned back, went, and cried out to the LORD their God.

**38.** He showed them mercy, not because of themselves, but because of their fathers, with whom he had sworn and established his covenant: Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, who from ancient times pleased the LORD by the goodness of their lives.

**39.** He loved those who loved him and kept his commandment, and promised to multiply their seed like the sand of the sea and like the stars of heaven.

**40.** The sand of the sea signifies the souls of sinners among the children of Israel at the resurrection of the dead, those who will enter the sea of fire.

**41.** But the souls of the righteous from the seed of the children of Israel are like shining stars above the heavens. For the LORD said to Abraham, “Look at the heavens by night and count the stars of heaven, if you are able to count them. So shall your righteous seed shine like the stars in the kingdom of heaven.”

**42.** Again he said to him, “Look into the sea and at the course of the river; see the sand in it and count it, if you are able to number it. So shall be your seed who go down into Sheol at the resurrection of the dead.”

**43.** Abraham believed in the LORD, and because of this it was accounted to him as righteousness. His hope was fulfilled upon the earth, and after the barrenness of his wife Sarah, she received a son, Isaac.

**44.** Again in heaven he will receive the kingdom because of his faith, for he believed that the dead will rise and that those who have done good will go into everlasting life,

**45.** while those who have done evil will go into everlasting judgment at the resurrection of the dead. The righteous who have done good will reign with him forever and ever.

**46.** Those who have done evil will be judged forever and ever. Amen and amen.
