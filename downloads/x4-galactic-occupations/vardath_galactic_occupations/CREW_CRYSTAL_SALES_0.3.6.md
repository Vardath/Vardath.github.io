# Existing-crew crystal counter sales — 0.3.6 DEV

Updated 8 November 2026. This implements the requested crew visit in addition to the docking-loop, passenger, galaxy-routing and payment repairs in [the current log review](LOG_REVIEW_0.3.6.md). **There is no X4 executable/save here. Native room streaming, physical paths and this complete new sale loop remain unverified in game.** Passing source and mocked tests do not establish those results.

## Actual implementation

The miner retains the resource-backed off-screen simulation: real field navigation, active work time and native yield depletion create a finite saved crystal batch. It does not follow the player or generate free inventory on a timer. Visible harvesting retains the existing real deposit/turret/drop path, which still needs live verification.

At a permitted faction buyer station, an available existing service crew member or marine is preferred; the existing captain is the fallback. Busy, mission, transferring, hacking or already deployed sale actors are excluded. No replacement crew is created. The same actor keeps their original role, control post and allegiance. Their temporary/busy flags and native Stay value are saved and restored. No crew roster template is removed.

The ship must actually dock and resume its own occupation under the new bounded service handoff. The MD registry then finds a real station inventory trader at a native itemtrader slot, excluding ship dealers. If none is present, it requests native trader-corner population at that actual station, at most once per station per game minute. It does not invent a trading room, counter or merchant. Missing usable rooms/counters/crew times out preparation after five minutes, retains the stock and ends the unsuccessful visit before another buyer is attempted.

Native `npc_move_to` sends the same representative from the ship to a genuine customer position at the trader's counter. The actual actor must be ashore in the actual merchant's station room, within five metres, no longer walking, and remain there for five seconds. A saved counter lease prevents two VGO ships claiming the same merchant simultaneously. The sale helper rechecks the actual counter/actor/dock/reservation both before and after a further ten seconds of work. A phase flag alone cannot authorize a sale.

The representative's departure snapshot caps this visit's goods. Native `add_inventory` transfers only those finite saved quantities to the **actual foreign trader**. Only the observed merchant inventory increase is removed from the saved batch and invoiced. Refused or partially accepted additions leave the unsold remainder intact. Player inventory is not swept and sale goods are not put into the crew's inventory, avoiding an Inventory Collector race.

The price is each native crystal ware's average price. This is a defined custom broker exchange; it does not call the player's Trade UI or claim a nonexistent autonomous NPC trading API. Native `reward_player` credits the player, and only its observed positive balance delta is receipted. An unpaid invoice is saved and partial-payment retries request only the remainder, without adding goods to the merchant twice. Small trade reputation follows full settlement and the shared faction cooldown. Pre-update invoices already backed by saved sale goods remain collectible.

After the sale, the same actor receives a native return walk to the saved ship location. The occupation cannot voluntarily launch with that deployed representative ashore. A paid visit with zero remaining stock still resumes until the crew return is observed. Original actor flags are restored at an actual ship room and native post placement restores their usual working location. A stalled return walk is stopped after two minutes and retried for the same actor; no return is fabricated. The active dock handoff is renewed while the owned occupation legitimately waits there.

Direct player orders retain priority. If they take the ship away, the representative waits at that station until the same ship redocks; the mod does not teleport a duplicate captain aboard. A destroyed representative ends the visit as failed without a false return receipt; remaining stock/unpaid claims are retained. An unwalkable return or destroyed station can still require player intervention. No engine runtime is available to establish how every installed station/ship interior handles these paths.

## Files and native provenance

`md/VGO_CrystalShop.xml` owns the persistent actor visit. `vardath.go.crystal.shop`, `.atcounter` and `.settle` manage the finite visit, actual counter authorization and stock/payment settlement. `sellcrystals`, `crystal.commercial` and `depart` preserve pending trips and prevent occupation departure before crew return. Hacker selection excludes the sale-reserved actor.

The pinned native 9.00 sources inspected are [npc_instantiation.xml](https://github.com/ArtByunex/x4-foundation-artbyunex/blob/e3e8a345e5624db118bff3eb171b60b0993fcc2c/md/npc_instantiation.xml), [npc_itemtrader.xml](https://github.com/ArtByunex/x4-foundation-artbyunex/blob/e3e8a345e5624db118bff3eb171b60b0993fcc2c/md/npc_itemtrader.xml) and [npc_state_machines.xml](https://github.com/ArtByunex/x4-foundation-artbyunex/blob/e3e8a345e5624db118bff3eb171b60b0993fcc2c/md/npc_state_machines.xml). These verify `PlaceItemTrader`, actual itemtrader slots, native NPC movement, Stay handling and stopping a stuck walk. The native UI ware-list signal does not itself add inventory, so the mod does not invent a payload or use it as proof of goods transfer. The sale does not depend on the unverified remote station-hack API.

## Checks actually performed

23 retail cases execute the shipped MD/AI with mocked native movement/inventory/credit actions. They cover same-actor outward/return paths, original crew traits/control post, captain fallback, real counter/dwell/reservation, absent trader and population throttling, missing customer positions, interruption, failed preparation, actor destruction, paid empty-stock resume, stuck-return retry, handoff renewal, refused/partial merchant acceptance, saved partial credit, no duplicate transfer and snapshot caps. Nine further cases execute persistent service-invoice accounting for escort/patrol and morale reputation. The full build has **203 Python contracts and 78 Lua mocks**. XML/API/reference, native diff selector/grammar and ZIP integrity checks are separate.

Those tests cannot establish that native rooms stream or that the engine accepts each NPC move, inventory addition, payment or UI event in the installed game. The supplied debug(9) predates this implementation: it proves 72 simulated items and one patrol payment, **no crystal sale**. No new sale is asserted from that old log.

## Live acceptance

1. Install 0.3.6 over the same extension while X4 is closed, retain Protected UI and Inventory Collector, and load the existing save. Use an armed miner with actual saved stock and an available crew member. Record their name/role and initial stock/player balance.
2. Observe remote mining in a permitted field, finite depletion and travel to an actual dockable faction buyer. Successful DockAt must resume the occupation instead of entering another immediate Undock. There must be no sale just for landing.
3. Watch the existing crew member physically leave, reach an actual trading-corner merchant's counter and stop there. Verify finite merchant inventory acceptance, matching stock reduction, actual player credits and a vanilla transaction/General receipt. New stock acquired after departure must remain for the next visit.
4. Watch the **same actor return aboard** before the occupation launches. Check original role/control post/traits, then verify another mining/sale assignment. Repeat with captain fallback, a capital ship's pier and an off-screen station. Room/path failures are failed/blocked visits, not proof of success.
5. Save/load during walking out, at the counter, after payment with crew still ashore and while walking back. Reject or partially apply a credit in an instrumented test. Only the saved unpaid balance may be retried and the merchant must not receive the same goods twice. Test refused merchant inventory addition: preserve unsold stock, no receipt or reputation.
6. Test no trader, no counter/customer position, full/busy crew, two miners at one merchant, a vanished trader and a stalled walk. Verify throttling/stock preservation and restoration of the existing crew. Give a direct order while the actor is ashore, then redock the same ship to recover them. No replacement actor or fabricated return may appear.
7. Repeat a verified foreign escort/patrol arrival. Check its 12,000/8,000 Cr fee respectively, a real vanilla receipt and controlled reputation. Rejected/partial credits must retain an unpaid claim, retry once per minute, then mark fully paid without repeating work or paying twice. Test repeat-off standby after settlement. Morale must improve actual crew before its zero-cash reputation reward.
